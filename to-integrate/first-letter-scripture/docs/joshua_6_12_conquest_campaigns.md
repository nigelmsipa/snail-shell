# Joshua 6-12: The Conquest Campaigns

## Part III: Central Campaign (Chapters 6-9)

### Chapter 6: Jericho Falls

#### 6:1-7 — Battle Instructions
**Lore:** "Now Jericho was shut up inside and outside because of the people of Israel" (6:1). Sealed city. "And the LORD said to Joshua, 'See, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and mighty men of valor. You shall march around the city, all the men of war going around the city once. Thus shall you do for six days. Seven priests shall bear seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark'" (6:2-4). Seven priests, seven trumpets, seven days. "On the seventh day you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the trumpets. And when they make a long blast with the ram's horn, when you hear the sound of the trumpet, then all the people shall shout with a great shout, and the wall of the city will fall down flat'" (6:5). Walls fall at shout. Joshua arranges: armed guard, seven priests with trumpets, ark, rear guard (6:6-7).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Divine battle plan*

#### 6:8-14 — Six Days of Silent Marching
**Lore:** Priests march with trumpets blowing continually (6:8-9). "But Joshua commanded the people, 'You shall not shout or make your voice heard, neither shall any word go out of your mouth, until the day I tell you to shout'" (6:10). Complete silence except trumpets! One circuit daily for six days (6:11-14). Test of obedient faith.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Silent obedience*

#### 6:15-21 — Seventh Day Victory
**Lore:** Seventh day: seven circuits starting at dawn (6:15). "At the seventh time, when the priests had blown the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, 'Shout, for the LORD has given you the city. And the city and all that is within it shall be devoted to the LORD for destruction (חֵרֶם, cherem)'" (6:16-17). Only Rahab spared. Warning about devoted things (6:18-19). "The wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, every man straight before him" (6:20). Total destruction executed (6:21).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Walls fall by faith*

#### 6:22-27 — Rahab Saved, City Cursed
**Lore:** Spies rescue Rahab's family (6:22-23). City burned, metals to treasury (6:24). "But Rahab the prostitute and her father's household and all who belonged to her, Joshua saved alive. And she has lived in Israel to this day" (6:25). "Joshua laid an oath on them at that time, saying, 'Cursed before the LORD be the man who rises up and rebuilds this city, Jericho'" (6:26). Fulfilled 1 Kings 16:34.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Promise kept*

### Chapter 7: Achan's Sin

#### 7:1-9 — Defeat at Ai
**Lore:** "But the people of Israel broke faith in regard to the devoted things, for Achan...took some of the devoted things. And the anger of the LORD burned against the people of Israel" (7:1). One man's sin affects all. Spies report Ai weak (7:2-3). About 3,000 attack, flee, 36 killed (7:4-5). Joshua mourns, questions God (7:6-9).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Sin brings defeat*

#### 7:10-15 — God Reveals Sin
**Lore:** "The LORD said to Joshua, 'Get up! Why have you fallen on your face? Israel has sinned; they have transgressed my covenant'" (7:10-11). Stop praying—deal with sin! "I will be with you no more, unless you destroy the devoted things" (7:12). Lot system to reveal guilty (7:13-15).
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Hidden sin exposed*

#### 7:16-26 — Achan Executed
**Lore:** Lot progressively identifies Achan (7:16-18). "I saw...a beautiful cloak from Shinar, and 200 shekels of silver, and a bar of gold...I coveted them and took them" (7:20-21). Process: saw→coveted→took→hid. All Israel stones Achan and family (7:24-26). Valley of Achor = "trouble."
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Covetousness punished*

### Chapter 8: Victory at Ai

#### 8:1-13 — Ambush Strategy
**Lore:** "Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Take all the fighting men with you" (8:1). After sin purged, assurance returns. "Lay an ambush against the city" (8:2). 30,000 set ambush west of Ai (8:3-9). Main force will feign retreat (8:5-6). Human strategy + divine promise.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: God gives strategy*

#### 8:14-29 — Ai Destroyed
**Lore:** King takes bait, city empties in pursuit (8:14-17). "Then the LORD said to Joshua, 'Stretch out the javelin that is in your hand toward Ai'" (8:18). Signal to ambush. City trapped between forces, 12,000 killed (8:19-25). "Joshua did not draw back his hand with which he stretched out the javelin until he had devoted all the inhabitants" (8:26). King hanged until sunset (8:29).
*Verses: 16 | Theme: Strategic victory*

#### 8:30-35 — Covenant Renewal at Ebal
**Lore:** Altar built on Mount Ebal per Deuteronomy 27 (8:30-31). Law written on stones (8:32). Nation divided between Gerizim and Ebal (8:33). "There was not a word of all that Moses commanded that Joshua did not read before all the assembly" (8:35).
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Covenant renewed*

### Chapter 9: Gibeonite Deception

#### 9:1-15 — Crafty Treaty
**Lore:** Coalition forms against Israel (9:1-2). Gibeonites pretend to be from far country with worn provisions (9:3-13). "So the men took some of their provisions, but did not ask counsel from the LORD" (9:14). CRITICAL FAILURE! "Joshua made peace with them and made a covenant with them" (9:15).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Failure to seek God*

#### 9:16-27 — Deception Discovered
**Lore:** Three days later, deception revealed (9:16-17). "We have sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel, and now we may not touch them" (9:19). Oath binding even if obtained by fraud. Gibeonites become "cutters of wood and drawers of water for the house of my God" (9:23). Saved but enslaved.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Sacred oath kept*

## Part IV: Southern and Northern Campaigns (Chapters 10-12)

### Chapter 10: Southern Campaign

#### 10:1-15 — Sun Stands Still
**Lore:** Five kings attack Gibeon for making peace (10:1-5). Gibeon calls for help (10:6). Joshua's night march, surprise attack (10:7-10). "The LORD threw down large stones from heaven on them...There were more who died because of the hailstones than the sons of Israel killed with the sword" (10:11). Joshua prays: "Sun, stand still at Gibeon, and moon, in the Valley of Aijalon" (10:12). "The sun stopped in the midst of heaven and did not hurry to set for about a whole day. There has been no day like it before or since, when the LORD heeded the voice of a man, for the LORD fought for Israel" (10:13-14).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Cosmic miracle*

#### 10:16-28 — Five Kings Executed
**Lore:** Five kings hide in cave at Makkedah (10:16-17). Trapped while battle continues (10:18-21). "Put your feet on the necks of these kings" (10:24). Symbolic victory. Kings hanged until sunset, cave becomes tomb (10:26-28).
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Kings under feet*

#### 10:29-43 — Southern Cities Fall
**Lore:** Swift campaign: Libnah (10:29-30), Lachish (10:31-32), Gezer reinforcements destroyed (10:33), Eglon (10:34-35), Hebron (10:36-37), Debir (10:38-39). "So Joshua struck the whole land, the hill country and the Negeb and the lowland and the slopes, and all their kings. He left none remaining, but devoted to destruction all that breathed, as the LORD God of Israel commanded" (10:40). "And Joshua captured all these kings and their land at one time, because the LORD God of Israel fought for Israel" (10:42).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Total southern victory*

### Chapter 11: Northern Campaign

#### 11:1-9 — Waters of Merom Victory
**Lore:** Jabin of Hazor leads massive northern coalition "like the sand that is on the seashore, with very many horses and chariots" (11:1-4). Camp at waters of Merom (11:5). "Do not be afraid of them, for tomorrow at this time I will give over all of them, slain, to Israel. You shall hamstring their horses and burn their chariots" (11:6). Joshua attacks suddenly (11:7). "The LORD gave them into the hand of Israel, who struck them and chased them as far as Great Sidon" (11:8). "And Joshua did to them just as the LORD said to him: he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire" (11:9). Prevents trust in military technology.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Horses hamstrung*

#### 11:10-15 — Hazor Burned
**Lore:** "Joshua turned back and captured Hazor and struck its king with the sword, for Hazor formerly was the head of all those kingdoms" (11:10). Chief city taken. "And they struck with the sword all who were in it, devoting them to destruction; there was none left that breathed. And he burned Hazor with fire" (11:11). Only Hazor burned in north. "Joshua took all these kings and their land at one time" (11:12). "Just as the LORD had commanded Moses his servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did. He left nothing undone of all that the LORD had commanded Moses" (11:15). Complete obedience.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Chief city burned*

#### 11:16-23 — Summary of Conquest
**Lore:** Geographic summary: "So Joshua took all that land" from Negeb to Lebanon (11:16-17). "Joshua made war a long time with all those kings" (11:18). Only Gibeon made peace; all others fought (11:19). "For it was the LORD's doing to harden their hearts that they should come against Israel in battle, in order that they should be devoted to destruction" (11:20). Divine hardening like Pharaoh. Anakim destroyed except in Gaza, Gath, Ashdod (11:21-22). "So Joshua took the whole land, according to all that the LORD had spoken to Moses. And Joshua gave it for an inheritance to Israel according to their tribal allotments. And the land had rest from war" (11:23). Mission accomplished.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Land rests from war*

### Chapter 12: List of Defeated Kings

#### 12:1-6 — Kings East of Jordan (Moses)
**Lore:** "Now these are the kings of the land whom the people of Israel defeated and took possession of their land beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise" (12:1). Sihon of Heshbon and Og of Bashan defeated by Moses (12:2-6). Foundation for Transjordan inheritance.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Moses's victories*

#### 12:7-24 — Kings West of Jordan (Joshua)
**Lore:** "And these are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the people of Israel defeated on the west side of the Jordan" (12:7). List of 31 kings follows (12:9-24). Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, Eglon, Gezer, Debir, Geder, Hormah, Arad, Libnah, Adullam, Makkedah, Bethel, Tappuah, Hepher, Aphek, Lasharon, Madon, Hazor, Shimron-meron, Achshaph, Taanach, Megiddo, Kedesh, Jokneam, Dor, Goiim, Tirzah. "In all, thirty-one kings" (12:24). Receipt of God's faithfulness. Memorial list proving complete victory.
*Verses: 18 | Theme: 31 kings defeated*
