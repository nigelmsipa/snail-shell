# Joshua 1-5: Entering the Promised Land

## Overview
Joshua = Jesus in Hebrew (יְהוֹשֻׁעַ, Yehoshua = "Yahweh saves"). Moses is dead. The wilderness generation is gone. A new generation stands at Jordan's banks ready to possess the promise. Joshua leads as a type of Christ who brings God's people into their inheritance. The book divides into: Entering (1-5), Conquering (6-12), Dividing (13-21), and Farewell (22-24).

---

## Part I: Preparation and Commission (Chapters 1-2)

### Chapter 1: Be Strong and Courageous

#### 1:1-9 — God's Charge to Joshua
**Lore:** "After the death of Moses the servant of the LORD, the LORD said to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses' assistant, 'Moses my servant is dead. Now therefore arise, go over this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving to them'" (1:1-2). Leadership transition. "Every place that the sole of your foot will tread upon I have given to you, just as I promised to Moses" (1:3). Promise renewed. "From the wilderness and this Lebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites to the Great Sea toward the going down of the sun shall be your territory" (1:4). Vast boundaries—never fully realized due to disobedience. "No man shall be able to stand before you all the days of your life. Just as I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not leave you or forsake you" (1:5). Divine presence promised. Hebrews 13:5 quotes this. "Be strong and courageous" (חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ, chazaq ve'ematz) repeated 3x in passage (1:6, 7, 9). **Key verse:** "This Book of the Law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate on it day and night, so that you may be careful to do according to all that is written in it. For then you will make your way prosperous, and then you will have good success" (1:8). Success formula: meditate + obey = prosper. Not military genius but God's Word. Psalm 1:2-3 echoes. "Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be frightened, and do not be dismayed, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go" (1:9). Fear nothing when God present!
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Success through God's Word*

#### 1:10-18 — Joshua Commands the People
**Lore:** "Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people, 'Pass through the midst of the camp and command the people, "Prepare your provisions, for within three days you are to pass over this Jordan"'" (1:10-11). Three days preparation—resurrection typology. To Reubenites, Gadites, half-Manasseh: "Remember the word that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you...Your wives, your little ones, and your livestock shall remain in the land that Moses gave you beyond the Jordan, but all the men of valor among you shall pass over armed before your brothers and shall help them" (1:12-14). Unity required—fight first, then rest. Numbers 32 promise kept. "Until the LORD gives rest to your brothers as he has to you, and they also take possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving them. Then you shall return to the land of your possession" (1:15). Conditional return. "And they answered Joshua, 'All that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go. Just as we obeyed Moses in all things, so we will obey you'" (1:16-17). Complete submission. "Whoever rebels against your commandment and disobeys your words, whatever you command him, shall be put to death. Only be strong and courageous'" (1:18). Death for rebellion. Echo God's words back to Joshua!
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Unity under leadership*

### Chapter 2: Rahab's Faith

#### 2:1-7 — Spies Hidden in Jericho
**Lore:** "And Joshua the son of Nun sent two men secretly from Shittim as spies, saying, 'Go, view the land, especially Jericho.' And they went and came into the house of a prostitute whose name was Rahab and lodged there" (2:1). Two spies (not twelve like Numbers 13—learned from failure). Rahab = prostitute (זוֹנָה, zonah), yet God uses her! "And it was told to the king of Jericho, 'Behold, men of Israel have come here tonight to search out the land'" (2:2). Immediately detected. "Then the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, 'Bring out the men who have come to you, who entered your house, for they have come to search out all the land'" (2:3). Royal demand. "But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. And she said, 'True, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from. And when the gate was about to be closed at dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Pursue them quickly, for you will overtake them'" (2:4-5). Bold lie to protect God's servants. "But she had brought them up to the roof and hid them with the stalks of flax that she had laid in order on the roof" (2:6). Flax = linen material. "So the men pursued after them on the way to the Jordan as far as the fords. And the gate was shut as soon as the pursuers had gone out" (2:7). City sealed.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Faith protects spies*

#### 2:8-14 — Rahab's Confession and Covenant
**Lore:** "Before the men lay down, she came up to them on the roof and said to the men, 'I know that the LORD has given you the land, and that the fear of you has fallen upon us, and that all the inhabitants of the land melt away before you'" (2:8-9). Faith confession! "For we have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom you devoted to destruction" (2:10). Forty years later, still trembling from Exodus! "And as soon as we heard it, our hearts melted, and there was no spirit left in any man because of you, for the LORD your God, he is God in the heavens above and on the earth beneath" (2:11). **Gentile monotheistic confession!** "Now then, please swear to me by the LORD that, as I have dealt kindly with you, you also will deal kindly with my father's house, and give me a sure sign that you will save alive my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and all who belong to them, and deliver our lives from death" (2:12-13). Covenant request for family. "And the men said to her, 'Our life for yours even to death! If you do not tell this business of ours, then when the LORD gives us the land we will deal kindly and faithfully with you'" (2:14). Oath sworn. Hebrews 11:31: "By faith Rahab the prostitute did not perish." James 2:25: justified by works.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Faith saves family*

#### 2:15-24 — The Scarlet Cord
**Lore:** "Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was built into the city wall, so that she lived in the wall" (2:15). Strategic location—casemate wall construction. "And she said to them, 'Go into the hills, or the pursuers will encounter you, and hide there three days until the pursuers have returned'" (2:16). Wise counsel—three days again. "The men said to her, 'We will be guiltless with respect to this oath that you have made us swear. Behold, when we come into the land, you shall tie this scarlet cord in the window through which you let us down'" (2:17-18). **Scarlet cord** (חוּט הַשָּׁנִי, chut hashani) = salvation sign. Like Passover blood (Ex 12:13). Type of Christ's blood. "And you shall gather into your house your father and mother, your brothers, and all your father's household. Then if anyone goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood shall be on his own head, and we shall be guiltless" (2:18-19). Must stay inside for protection—like Passover night. "But if you tell this business of ours, then we shall be guiltless" (2:20). Secrecy required. "And she said, 'According to your words, so be it.' Then she sent them away, and they departed. And she tied the scarlet cord in the window" (2:21). Immediate obedience. They hide three days, return: "Truly the LORD has given all the land into our hands. And also, all the inhabitants of the land melt away because of us" (2:24). Mission successful. Matthew 1:5: Rahab in Christ's genealogy—married Salmon, mother of Boaz!
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Scarlet cord salvation*

## Part II: Crossing and Consecration (Chapters 3-5)

### Chapter 3: Jordan Parts

#### 3:1-6 — Preparation at the River
**Lore:** "Then Joshua rose early in the morning and they set out from Shittim. And they came to the Jordan, he and all the people of Israel, and lodged there before they passed over" (3:1). Final approach. "At the end of three days the officers went through the camp and commanded the people, 'As soon as you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God being carried by the Levitical priests, then you shall set out from your place and follow it'" (3:2-3). Ark leads—God's presence goes first. "Yet there shall be a distance between you and it, about 2,000 cubits in length. Do not come near it, in order that you may know the way you shall go, for you have not passed this way before" (3:4). 2,000 cubits = ¾ mile. Holy distance. New path. "Then Joshua said to the people, 'Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you'" (3:5). Sanctification before miracle. "And Joshua said to the priests, 'Take up the ark of the covenant and pass on before the people.' So they took up the ark of the covenant and went before the people" (3:6). Priests lead with ark.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Consecrate for wonders*

#### 3:7-17 — Waters Stand in a Heap
**Lore:** "The LORD said to Joshua, 'Today I will begin to exalt you in the sight of all Israel, that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I will be with you'" (3:7). Divine endorsement. "And you shall command the priests who bear the ark of the covenant, 'When you come to the brink of the waters of the Jordan, you shall stand still in the Jordan'" (3:8). Step in first, then miracle. Joshua announces: "Here is how you shall know that the living God is among you and that he will without fail drive out from before you the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, and the Jebusites" (3:10). Seven nations listed. "Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is passing over before you into the Jordan" (3:11). Universal lordship. "Now when the people set out from their tents to pass over the Jordan with the priests bearing the ark of the covenant before the people, and as soon as those bearing the ark had come as far as the Jordan, and the feet of the priests bearing the ark were dipped in the brink of the water (now the Jordan overflows all its banks throughout the time of harvest)" (3:14-15). **Flood stage!** Spring barley harvest. Maximum difficulty. "The waters coming down from above stood and rose up in a heap very far away, at Adam, the city that is beside Zarethan" (3:16). Waters heap up 15+ miles north at Adam (fitting name—new beginning for new Adam/humanity). "And those flowing down toward the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, were completely cut off" (3:16). Dead Sea flow stops. "And the people passed over opposite Jericho. Now the priests bearing the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firmly on dry ground in the midst of the Jordan, and all Israel was passing over on dry ground until all the nation finished passing over the Jordan" (3:17). Priests stand still while nation crosses. New Exodus! Psalm 114:3: "The Jordan turned back."
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Jordan parts at flood*

### Chapter 4: Memorial Stones

#### 4:1-14 — Twelve Stones from Jordan
**Lore:** "When all the nation had finished passing over the Jordan, the LORD said to Joshua, 'Take twelve men from the people, from each tribe a man, and command them, saying, "Take twelve stones from here out of the midst of the Jordan, from the very place where the priests' feet stood firmly, and bring them over with you and lay them down in the place where you lodge tonight"'" (4:1-3). Memorial stones from riverbed. "Then Joshua called the twelve men from the people of Israel, whom he had appointed, a man from each tribe" (4:4). Representatives. "And Joshua said to them, 'Pass on before the ark of the LORD your God into the midst of the Jordan, and take up each of you a stone upon his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the people of Israel, that this may be a sign among you'" (4:5-6). Each carries tribal stone. "When your children ask in time to come, 'What do those stones mean to you?' then you shall tell them that the waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD. When it passed over the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off. So these stones shall be to the people of Israel a memorial forever" (4:6-7). **Pedagogical purpose!** Teaching tool for generations. "And the people of Israel did just as Joshua commanded" (4:8). "And Joshua set up twelve stones in the midst of the Jordan, in the place where the feet of the priests bearing the ark of the covenant had stood; and they are there to this day" (4:9). Second memorial IN river (visible at low water?). "The people passed over in haste. And when all the people had finished passing over, the ark of the LORD and the priests passed over before the people" (4:10-11). Ark crosses last. "The sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh passed over armed before the people of Israel, as Moses had told them. About 40,000 ready for war passed over before the LORD for battle, to the plains of Jericho" (4:12-13). Transjordan tribes keep promise. "On that day the LORD exalted Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they feared him just as they had feared Moses, all the days of his life" (4:14). Leadership confirmed.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Memorial for teaching*

#### 4:15-24 — Jordan Returns, Camp at Gilgal
**Lore:** "And the LORD said to Joshua, 'Command the priests bearing the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan.' So Joshua commanded the priests, 'Come up out of the Jordan'" (4:15-17). Divine timing. "And when the priests bearing the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up from the midst of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests' feet were lifted up on dry ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and overflowed all its banks, as before" (4:18). **Instant return!** "The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and they encamped at Gilgal on the east border of Jericho" (4:19). Tenth of Nisan = day to select Passover lamb (Ex 12:3). "And those twelve stones, which they took out of the Jordan, Joshua set up at Gilgal" (4:20). **Gilgal** (גִּלְגָּל) = "rolling." "And he said to the people of Israel, 'When your children ask their fathers in times to come, "What do these stones mean?" then you shall let your children know, "Israel passed over this Jordan on dry ground." For the LORD your God dried up the waters of the Jordan for you until you passed over, as the LORD your God did to the Red Sea, which he dried up for us until we passed over, so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of the LORD is mighty, that you may fear the LORD your God forever'" (4:21-24). Evangelistic purpose: all nations know God's might. Comparison to Red Sea explicit. 1 Corinthians 10:1-2: baptism typology.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Waters return instantly*

### Chapter 5: Circumcision and Commander

#### 5:1-12 — Reproach of Egypt Rolled Away
**Lore:** "As soon as all the kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan to the west, and all the kings of the Canaanites who were by the sea, heard that the LORD had dried up the waters of the Jordan for the people of Israel until they had crossed over, their hearts melted and there was no longer any spirit in them because of the people of Israel" (5:1). Terror grips Canaan. Perfect time to attack? No—time to circumcise! "At that time the LORD said to Joshua, 'Make flint knives and circumcise the sons of Israel a second time'" (5:2). Flint = ancient practice. "So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the sons of Israel at Gibeath-haaraloth" (5:3). "Hill of Foreskins"! "And this is the reason why Joshua circumcised them: all the males of the people who came out of Egypt, all the men of war, had died in the wilderness on the way after they had come out of Egypt" (5:4). Old generation dead. "Though all the people who came out had been circumcised, yet all the people who were born on the way in the wilderness after they had come out of Egypt had not been circumcised" (5:5). Forty years uncircumcised—covenant sign neglected. "So it was their children, whom he raised up in their place, that Joshua circumcised. For they were uncircumcised, because they had not been circumcised on the way" (5:7). "When the circumcising of the whole nation was finished, they remained in their places in the camp until they were healed" (5:8). Vulnerable but protected by terror (5:1). "And the LORD said to Joshua, 'Today I have rolled away the reproach of Egypt from you.' And so the name of that place is called Gilgal to this day" (5:9). Gilgal = "rolling away." "Reproach" = slavery stigma removed. "While the people of Israel were encamped at Gilgal, they kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month in the evening on the plains of Jericho" (5:10). First Passover in Canaan! "And the day after the Passover, on that very day, they ate of the produce of the land, unleavened cakes and parched grain. And the manna ceased the day after they ate of the produce of the land" (5:11-12). Manna stops—40 years of supernatural provision ends. Now eat Canaan's fruit.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Covenant renewed, manna ceases*

#### 5:13-15 — Captain of the LORD's Host
**Lore:** "When Joshua was by Jericho, he lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, a man was standing before him with his drawn sword in his hand. And Joshua went to him and said to him, 'Are you for us, or for our adversaries?'" (5:13). Joshua challenges armed figure near enemy city—brave! "And he said, 'No; but I am the commander of the army of the LORD. Now I have come.' And Joshua fell on his face to the earth and worshiped and said to him, 'What does my lord say to his servant?'" (5:14). **Theophany!** Pre-incarnate Christ. Not for Israel or enemies—has own agenda. Joshua immediately worships (only God receives worship—Rev 22:8-9). "Commander" (שַׂר, sar) = prince, captain. "And the commander of the LORD's army said to Joshua, 'Take off your sandals from your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy.' And Joshua did so" (5:15). Exact words to Moses at burning bush (Ex 3:5)! Joshua = new Moses. Holy ground at edge of enemy territory. This encounter reveals true nature of conquest: not Israel's war but God's. Christ leads heavenly army. Revelation 19:11-14: Christ leads armies of heaven. Daniel 10:13, 21; 12:1: Michael the archangel? Some identify this figure as Michael, others as Christ Himself. Worship suggests deity. Message: Joshua not supreme commander—he's under orders from heaven's Captain!
*Verses: 3 | Theme: Christ commands the war*
