# Joshua 13-22: Land Allotment and Inheritance

## Part V: Transjordan and Judah's Inheritance (Chapters 13-15)

### Chapter 13: Land Remaining and Eastern Tribes

#### 13:1-7 — Land Still Unconquered
**Lore:** "Now Joshua was old and advanced in years, and the LORD said to him, 'You are old and advanced in years, and there remains yet very much land to possess'" (13:1). Incomplete conquest acknowledged. "This is the land that yet remains: all the regions of the Philistines, and all those of the Geshurites...all Lebanon, toward the sunrise...all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, even all the Sidonians" (13:2-6). Philistine coast, Lebanon, Sidon unconquered. "I myself will drive them out from before the people of Israel. Only allot the land to Israel for an inheritance, as I have commanded you" (13:6). God will complete what Joshua started. "Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance to the nine tribes and half the tribe of Manasseh" (13:7). Distribute by faith, not full possession.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Incomplete conquest*

#### 13:8-33 — Eastern Tribes' Inheritance
**Lore:** "With the other half of the tribe of Manasseh the Reubenites and the Gadites received their inheritance, which Moses gave them, beyond the Jordan eastward" (13:8). Moses's allocation confirmed. Territory from Arnon to Hermon detailed (13:9-13). "Only to the tribe of Levi he gave no inheritance. The offerings by fire to the LORD God of Israel are their inheritance" (13:14). Levites inherit God Himself. Reuben's portion (13:15-23): from Arnon gorge to north of Dead Sea. Includes cities of Sihon. Gad's portion (13:24-28): Gilead region, half of Ammon. Half-Manasseh (13:29-31): Bashan, Og's kingdom, 60 cities. "These are the inheritances that Moses distributed in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan east of Jericho. But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance. The LORD God of Israel is their inheritance, just as he said to them" (13:32-33). Levites' unique status repeated.
*Verses: 26 | Theme: Eastern inheritance*

### Chapter 14: Caleb's Reward

#### 14:1-5 — Inheritance Principles
**Lore:** "These are the inheritances that the people of Israel received in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun and the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the people of Israel gave them to inherit" (14:1). Three-person commission. "Their inheritance was by lot, just as the LORD had commanded by the hand of Moses for the nine and one-half tribes" (14:2). Lot removes human favoritism. "For Moses had given an inheritance to the two and one-half tribes beyond the Jordan, but to the Levites he gave no inheritance among them. For the people of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim" (14:3-4). Joseph's double portion maintains twelve. "The people of Israel did as the LORD commanded Moses; they allotted the land" (14:5). Obedient distribution.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Inheritance by lot*

#### 14:6-15 — Caleb Claims Hebron
**Lore:** "Then the people of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal. And Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, 'You know what the LORD said to Moses the man of God in Kadesh-barnea concerning you and me'" (14:6). Caleb recalls spy mission. "I was forty years old when Moses the servant of the LORD sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land, and I brought him word again as it was in my heart. But my brothers who went up with me made the heart of the people melt; yet I wholly followed the LORD my God" (14:7-8). Faithful minority report. "And Moses swore on that day, saying, 'Surely the land on which your foot has trodden shall be an inheritance for you and your children forever, because you have wholly followed the LORD my God'" (14:9). Moses's promise quoted. "And now, behold, the LORD has kept me alive, just as he said, these forty-five years since the time that the LORD spoke this word to Moses, while Israel walked in the wilderness. And now, behold, I am this day eighty-five years old. I am still as strong today as I was in the day that Moses sent me; my strength now is as my strength was then, for war and for going and coming" (14:10-11). 85 years old, still warrior-strong! "So now give me this hill country of which the LORD spoke on that day, for you heard on that day how the Anakim were there, with great fortified cities. It may be that the LORD will be with me, and I shall drive them out just as the LORD said" (14:12). Requests hardest territory—giants' stronghold! "Then Joshua blessed him, and he gave Hebron to Caleb the son of Jephunneh for an inheritance...because he wholly followed the LORD, the God of Israel" (14:13-14). Reward for wholehearted devotion. "Now the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath-arba. (Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim.) And the land had rest from war" (14:15). City of giants' ancestor becomes Caleb's.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Wholehearted faith rewarded*

### Chapter 15: Judah's Territory

#### 15:1-12 — Judah's Boundaries
**Lore:** Detailed southern boundary from Dead Sea to Mediterranean (15:1-4). Eastern boundary: Dead Sea (15:5a). Northern boundary from Dead Sea to Mediterranean via Jerusalem area (15:5b-11). Western boundary: Mediterranean coast (15:12). Largest tribal territory.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Judah's vast borders*

#### 15:13-19 — Caleb Conquers Hebron and Debir
**Lore:** "According to the commandment of the LORD to Joshua, he gave to Caleb the son of Jephunneh a portion among the people of Judah, Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron" (15:13). Within Judah's portion. "And Caleb drove out from there the three sons of Anak, Sheshai and Ahiman and Talmai, the descendants of Anak" (15:14). Giants defeated! "And he went up from there against the inhabitants of Debir" (15:15). "And Caleb said, 'Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and captures it, to him will I give Achsah my daughter as wife'" (15:16). Daughter as prize. "And Othniel the son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb, captured it. And he gave him Achsah his daughter as wife" (15:17). Nephew wins. Achsah asks father for springs of water, receives upper and lower springs (15:18-19). Wise daughter secures water rights.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Giants defeated*

#### 15:20-63 — Cities of Judah
**Lore:** List of cities in Negeb (15:21-32), lowland (15:33-47), hill country (15:48-60), wilderness (15:61-62). 29 + 14 + 38 + 11 + 6 = 98 cities total. "But the Jebusites, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the people of Judah could not drive out, so the Jebusites dwell with the people of Judah at Jerusalem to this day" (15:63). **First failure noted**—Jerusalem remains Jebusite until David (2 Sam 5:6-9).
*Verses: 44 | Theme: Cities and failure*

## Part VI: Joseph's Inheritance and Remaining Tribes (Chapters 16-19)

### Chapters 16-17: Ephraim and Manasseh

#### 16:1-10 — Ephraim's Territory
**Lore:** Joseph's lot described (16:1-4). Ephraim's specific boundaries (16:5-9). "However, they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, so the Canaanites have lived in the midst of Ephraim to this day but have been made to do forced labor" (16:10). **Second failure**—compromise for convenience.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Ephraim's compromise*

#### 17:1-13 — Manasseh's Territory
**Lore:** Manasseh's allotment, including Gilead and Bashan east of Jordan (17:1-6). Zelophehad's five daughters receive inheritance—Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, Tirzah (17:3-6). Women's rights established. Boundaries detailed (17:7-11). "Yet the people of Manasseh could not take possession of those cities, but the Canaanites persisted in dwelling in that land. Now when the people of Israel grew strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, but did not utterly drive them out" (17:12-13). **Pattern of compromise**—convenience over complete obedience.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Incomplete obedience*

#### 17:14-18 — Joseph's Complaint
**Lore:** "Then the people of Joseph spoke to Joshua, saying, 'Why have you given me but one lot and one portion as an inheritance, although I am a numerous people, since all along the LORD has blessed me?'" (17:14). Complaint about insufficient land. "And Joshua said to them, 'If you are a numerous people, go up by yourselves to the forest, and there clear ground for yourselves in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, since the hill country of Ephraim is too narrow for you'" (17:15). Joshua: clear the forest! "The people of Joseph said, 'The hill country is not enough for us. Yet all the Canaanites who dwell in the plain have chariots of iron'" (17:16). Fear of iron chariots. "Then Joshua said to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, 'You are a numerous people and have great power. You shall not have one allotment only, but the hill country shall be yours, for though it is a forest, you shall clear it and possess it to its farthest borders. For you shall drive out the Canaanites, though they have chariots of iron, and though they are strong'" (17:17-18). Challenge to conquer by faith, not complain.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Overcome by faith*

### Chapter 18: Shiloh and Seven Tribes

#### 18:1-10 — Tabernacle at Shiloh
**Lore:** "Then the whole congregation of the people of Israel assembled at Shiloh and set up the tent of meeting there. The land lay subdued before them" (18:1). **Central worship established!** Shiloh becomes religious center. "There remained among the people of Israel seven tribes whose inheritance had not yet been apportioned. So Joshua said to the people of Israel, 'How long will you put off going in to take possession of the land, which the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you?'" (18:2-3). Rebuke for delay! "Provide three men from each tribe, and I will send them out that they may set out and go up and down the land. They shall write a description of it with a view to their inheritances" (18:4). Survey commission: 21 men. "You shall describe the land in seven divisions and bring the description here to me. And I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God" (18:6). Sacred lots at sanctuary. Survey completed, lots cast "before the LORD in Shiloh" (18:8-10).
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Worship centralized*

### Chapter 19: Final Tribal Allotments

#### 19:1-51 — Seven Remaining Tribes
**Lore:** Simeon within Judah (19:1-9)—absorbed into Judah, fulfills Jacob's prophecy (Gen 49:7). Zebulun (19:10-16), Issachar (19:17-23), Asher (19:24-31), Naphtali (19:32-39), Dan (19:40-48)—northern territories. Dan unable to hold territory, migrates north to Laish/Dan (19:47). "These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun and the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the people of Israel distributed by lot at Shiloh before the LORD, at the entrance of the tent of meeting. So they finished dividing the land" (19:51). Distribution complete. "When they had finished distributing the several territories of the land as inheritances, the people of Israel gave an inheritance among them to Joshua the son of Nun...Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim. And he rebuilt the city and settled in it" (19:49-50). **Joshua takes last**—servant leadership.
*Verses: 51 | Theme: Joshua inherits last*

## Part VII: Cities of Refuge and Levites (Chapters 20-21)

### Chapter 20: Cities of Refuge

#### 20:1-9 — Six Cities Designated
**Lore:** "Then the LORD said to Joshua, 'Say to the people of Israel, "Appoint the cities of refuge, of which I spoke to you through Moses, that the manslayer who strikes any person without intent or unknowingly may flee there"'" (20:1-3). Fulfills Numbers 35. Protection from blood avenger (20:3-5). "And he shall remain in that city until he has stood before the congregation for judgment, until the death of him who is high priest at the time. Then the manslayer may return to his own town" (20:6). High priest's death = release. West of Jordan: Kedesh (Naphtali), Shechem (Ephraim), Hebron (Judah). East: Bezer (Reuben), Ramoth (Gad), Golan (Manasseh) (20:7-8). "These were the cities designated for all the people of Israel and for the stranger sojourning among them, that anyone who killed a person without intent could flee there, so that he might not die by the hand of the avenger of blood, till he stood before the congregation" (20:9). Justice with mercy. Type of Christ our refuge (Heb 6:18).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Mercy for manslayers*

### Chapter 21: Levitical Cities

#### 21:1-42 — Forty-Eight Cities for Levites
**Lore:** "Then the heads of the fathers' houses of the Levites came to Eleazar the priest and to Joshua the son of Nun" (21:1). "The LORD commanded through Moses that we be given cities to dwell in, along with their pasturelands for our livestock" (21:2). Kohathites (priests): 13 cities from Judah, Simeon, Benjamin (21:4). Other Kohathites: 10 cities from Ephraim, Dan, Manasseh (21:5). Gershonites: 13 cities from northern tribes (21:6). Merarites: 12 cities from Reuben, Gad, Zebulun (21:7). Total: 48 cities throughout Israel. Strategic dispersal—teachers in every region. "And the LORD gave them rest on every side just as he had sworn to their fathers. Not one of all their enemies had withstood them, for the LORD had given all their enemies into their hands" (21:44).
*Verses: 42 | Theme: Levites dispersed*

#### 21:43-45 — God's Faithfulness Summary
**Lore:** "Thus the LORD gave to Israel all the land that he swore to give to their fathers. And they took possession of it, and they settled there. And the LORD gave them rest on every side just as he had sworn to their fathers. Not one of all their enemies had withstood them, for the LORD had given all their enemies into their hands. Not one word of all the good promises that the LORD had made to the house of Israel had failed; all came to pass" (21:43-45). **CLIMACTIC SUMMARY!** Every promise fulfilled. Foundation for covenant faithfulness.
*Verses: 3 | Theme: Every promise kept*

### Chapter 22: Eastern Tribes Return

#### 22:1-9 — Joshua Dismisses Eastern Tribes
**Lore:** "At that time Joshua summoned the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and said to them, 'You have kept all that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you and have obeyed my voice in all that I have commanded you'" (22:1-2). Commendation for faithfulness. "You have not forsaken your brothers these many days, down to this day, but have been careful to keep the charge of the LORD your God" (22:3). Years of warfare together. "And now the LORD your God has given rest to your brothers, as he promised them. Therefore turn and go to your tents in the land where your possession lies" (22:4). Released to return. "Only be very careful to observe the commandment and the law that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, to love the LORD your God, and to walk in all his ways and to keep his commandments and to cling to him and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul" (22:5). Final charge. Joshua blesses them, sends them with plunder to share (22:6-8). "So the people of Reuben and the people of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh returned home" (22:9).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Faithful service rewarded*

#### 22:10-34 — The Altar of Witness Crisis
**Lore:** Eastern tribes build large altar at Jordan (22:10). "And the people of Israel heard it said, 'Behold, the people of Reuben and the people of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built an altar at the frontier of the land of Canaan'" (22:11). Western tribes interpret as apostasy, gather for war at Shiloh (22:12). Send Phinehas and ten chiefs to investigate (22:13-14). "What is this breach of faith that you have committed against the God of Israel in turning away this day from following the LORD by building yourselves an altar this day in rebellion against the LORD?" (22:16). Accusation of rebellion. "Is not the sin of Peor enough for us?" (22:17). Remember past judgment. Eastern tribes explain: "The Mighty One, God, the LORD! The Mighty One, God, the LORD! He knows; and let Israel itself know! If it was in rebellion or in breach of faith against the LORD, do not spare us today" (22:22). Solemn oath. "No, but we did it from fear that in time to come your children might say to our children, 'What have you to do with the LORD, the God of Israel? For the LORD has made the Jordan a boundary between us and you'" (22:24-25). Fear of exclusion. "Therefore we said, 'Let us now build an altar, not for burnt offering, nor for sacrifice, but to be a witness between us and you'" (22:26-27). **Altar of witness, not worship!** "Far be it from us that we should rebel against the LORD and turn away this day from following the LORD by building an altar for burnt offering, grain offering, or sacrifice, other than the altar of the LORD our God that stands before his tabernacle!" (22:29). Only one altar for sacrifice. "When Phinehas the priest and the chiefs...heard the words that the people of Reuben and the people of Gad and the people of Manasseh spoke, it was good in their eyes" (22:30). Crisis resolved by communication. "The people of Reuben and the people of Gad called the altar Witness, 'For,' they said, 'it is a witness between us that the LORD is God'" (22:34). Named "Ed" (witness).
*Verses: 25 | Theme: Unity preserved*
