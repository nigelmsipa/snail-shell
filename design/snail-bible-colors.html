Snail-Shell — Bible Translation Color System

One accent per translation. Same paper everywhere. Color you can read like a date.

1560 · oldest · warmnewest · cool · 2022
The background stays the same cream paper everywhere — only the accent changes per translation (title rule, lemma, verse markers, and the highlighter behind the active word). The rule that makes the set read as one family instead of a scatter: Lock saturation & lightness. Vary only the hue.
