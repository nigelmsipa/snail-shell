# Numbers 34-36: Boundaries, Cities, and Final Laws

## Part XV: Canaan's Boundaries and Levitical Cities (Chapters 34-35)

### Chapter 34: Boundaries of the Promised Land

#### 34:1-15 — The Land's Borders Defined
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Command the people of Israel, and say to them, When you enter the land of Canaan (this is the land that shall fall to you for an inheritance, the land of Canaan as defined by its borders)'" (34:1-2). God defines exact boundaries—precision shows this is real, not metaphorical. **South boundary** (34:3-5): from Wilderness of Zin along Edom, southern end of Dead Sea, south of Scorpion Pass to Zin, south of Kadesh-barnea, to Hazar-addar, Azmon, Brook of Egypt, to Mediterranean Sea. **West boundary** (34:6): Mediterranean coast. **North boundary** (34:7-9): From Mediterranean to Mount Hor (different from Aaron's Mount Hor), to Lebo-hamath, Zedad, Ziphron, Hazar-enan. **East boundary** (34:10-12): From Hazar-enan to Shepham, down to Riblah, along east side of Sea of Chinnereth (Galilee), down Jordan River, to Dead Sea. Clear legal description. Fulfills promise to Abraham (Gen 15:18-21). Israel never fully possessed all this—Solomon's kingdom came closest (1 Kgs 4:21). Prophetic vision of restored kingdom (Ezek 47:13-23). Spiritual application: God's promises are specific, concrete. Heaven is real place (John 14:2-3).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Promised land defined*

#### 34:16-29 — Leaders to Divide the Land
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'These are the names of the men who shall divide the land to you for inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun'" (34:16-17). Two leaders: priest + military commander. Then one chief per tribe (34:18). Names listed (34:19-28): Caleb (Judah), Shemuel (Simeon), Elidad (Benjamin), Bukki (Dan), Hanniel (Manasseh), Kemuel (Ephraim), Elizaphan (Zebulun), Paltiel (Issachar), Ahihud (Asher), Pedahel (Naphtali). No Reuben, Gad, half-Manasseh—they already received east side (34:14-15). "These are the men whom the LORD commanded to divide the inheritance for the people of Israel in the land of Canaan" (34:29). Divine appointment of human administrators. God works through ordained leadership. Joshua 14-21 records actual distribution.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Appointed distributors*

### Chapter 35: Cities of Refuge and Levitical Cities

#### 35:1-8 — Forty-Eight Cities for Levites
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying, 'Command the people of Israel to give to the Levites some of the inheritance of their possession as cities for them to dwell in. And you shall give to the Levites pasturelands around the cities'" (35:1-3). Levites = no tribal land (18:23-24), but need places to live. Each tribe gives cities from their inheritance (35:8). "The cities that you give to the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, where you shall permit the manslayer to flee, and in addition to them you shall give forty-two cities. All the cities that you give to the Levites shall be forty-eight cities, with their pasturelands" (35:6-7). 48 total: 6 refuge cities + 42 regular. From large tribes, take more; from small, less (35:8). Distributes Levites throughout Israel. Priests/teachers among all tribes. Spiritual influence spread nationally. Church should permeate society likewise.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Levites distributed*

#### 35:9-34 — Cities of Refuge for Manslayers
**Lore:** "And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan, then you shall select cities to be cities of refuge for you, that the manslayer who kills any person without intent may flee there'" (35:9-11). Six cities of refuge (35:13-14): three west of Jordan, three east. Purpose: protect accidental killers from blood avenger (גֹּאֵל הַדָּם, goel hadam, "avenger of blood"—nearest kinsman obligated to execute murderer). "The cities shall be for you a refuge from the avenger, that the manslayer may not die until he stands before the congregation for judgment" (35:12). **Distinguishes murder from manslaughter:** Murder = premeditated, using weapon, from hatred (35:16-21). Death penalty mandatory. No ransom accepted (35:31). Manslaughter = accidental, unintentional, no prior hatred (35:22-23). Congregation judges; if manslayer, he stays in refuge city until high priest dies (35:25). After high priest's death, may return home safely (35:28). "The land cannot be cleansed of the blood that is shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it. You shall not defile the land in which you live, in the midst of which I dwell, for I the LORD dwell in the midst of the people of Israel" (35:33-34). Life sacred; bloodshed defiles land. Refuge cities = mercy within justice. Typology: Christ our refuge (Heb 6:18). Fleeing to Him = safety from judgment. High Priest's death = atonement enabling freedom. Jesus = High Priest whose death liberates forever.
*Verses: 26 | Theme: Refuge from death*

## Part XVI: Inheritance Protection (Chapter 36)

### Chapter 36: Marriage Within Tribes

#### 36:1-13 — Zelophehad's Daughters Must Marry Within Tribe
**Lore:** "The heads of the fathers' houses of the clan of the people of Gilead the son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the clans of the people of Joseph, came near and spoke before Moses and before the chiefs, the heads of the fathers' houses of the people of Israel" (36:1). Follow-up to ch 27. Concern: "If they are married to any of the sons of the other tribes of the people of Israel, then their inheritance will be taken from the inheritance of our fathers and added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry. So it will be taken away from the lot of our inheritance" (36:3). If Zelophehad's daughters marry outside Manasseh, land transfers to other tribes. At Jubilee (every 50 years, Lev 25), land returns to original tribe—but if daughters married out, it stays with husband's tribe (36:4). Permanent loss. "And Moses commanded the people of Israel according to the word of the LORD, saying, 'The tribe of the people of Joseph is right. This is what the LORD commands concerning the daughters of Zelophehad: "Let them marry whom they think best, only they shall marry within the clan of the tribe of their father"'" (36:5-6). Solution: marry within tribe. "The inheritance of the people of Israel shall not be transferred from one tribe to another, for every one of the people of Israel shall hold on to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers. And every daughter who possesses an inheritance in any tribe of the people of Israel shall be wife to one of the clan of the tribe of her father, so that every one of the people of Israel may possess the inheritance of his fathers. So no inheritance shall be transferred from one tribe to another, for each of the tribes of the people of Israel shall hold on to its own inheritance'" (36:7-9). Tribal boundaries preserved. Daughters obey: "The daughters of Zelophehad did as the LORD commanded Moses, for Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, were married to sons of their father's brothers. They were married into the clans of the people of Manasseh the son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of their father's clan" (36:10-12). Happy ending! Women honored (ch 27), tribal integrity maintained (ch 36). Balance of individual rights and community good.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Tribal integrity*

#### 36:13 — Conclusion of Numbers
**Lore:** "These are the commandments and the rules that the LORD commanded through Moses to the people of Israel in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho" (36:13). Final verse. Closes book at same location as it began (1:1). Forty years compressed. From Sinai (chs 1-10) to Kadesh rebellion (chs 11-20) to Plains of Moab victories (chs 21-36). Two generations: faithless dies, faithful rises. Moses led them here but won't cross over. Joshua ready. Laws given. Land mapped. Cities assigned. Everything prepared for conquest. Deuteronomy follows—Moses's final sermons before death. Then Joshua leads crossing. Numbers = transition book. From wilderness wandering to Canaan's edge. From rebellion to readiness. From death to new life. Spiritual parallel: Christian journey from Egypt (salvation) through wilderness (sanctification) to Promised Land (glorification). We're pilgrims heading home. Hebrews 11:13-16: "These all died in faith, not having received the things promised, but having seen them and greeted them from afar, and having acknowledged that they were strangers and exiles on the earth. For people who speak thus make it clear that they are seeking a homeland...they desire a better country, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed to be called their God, for he has prepared for them a city."
*Verses: 1 | Theme: Preparation complete*
