# Leviticus 17-27: The Holiness Code (Properly Divided)

## Part V: Blood, Sex, and Social Holiness (Chapters 17-20)

### Chapter 17: The Sacredness of Blood

#### 17:1-9 — One Altar, No Field Slaughter
**Lore:** "Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the people of Israel and say to them, This is what the LORD has commanded" (17:2). Any Israelite slaughtering ox, lamb, or goat anywhere must bring it to tent entrance as offering, or "that man shall be guilty of bloodshed" and cut off (17:3-4). Purpose: centralize worship, prevent offering to "goat demons" (שְׂעִירִם, se'irim, pagan deities) they worshiped in Egypt (17:5-7). Anyone offering burnt/sacrifice elsewhere = cut off (17:8-9). Centralization prevents syncretism.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Centralized worship*

#### 17:10-16 — Life is in the Blood
**Lore:** "If any one...eats any blood, I will set my face against that person who eats blood and will cut him off from among his people. For the life (נֶפֶשׁ, nephesh, "soul") of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it for you on the altar to make atonement for your souls, for it is the blood that makes atonement by the life" (17:10-11). Central theological statement: blood = life, reserved for atonement. Even foreigners forbidden (17:12). Hunter must pour out blood, cover with earth (17:13-14). Eating animals found dead (still contain blood) = unclean till evening (17:15-16). Hebrews 9:22: "Without shedding of blood there is no forgiveness." John 6:53-56: Jesus redefines "drinking blood" as covenant participation. Lord's Supper.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Blood for atonement*

### Chapter 18: Sexual Purity

#### 18:1-5 — Distinct from Egypt and Canaan
**Lore:** "I am the LORD your God. You shall not do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived, and you shall not do as they do in the land of Canaan, to which I am bringing you" (18:2-3). Israel's ethics must be distinct. "You shall therefore keep my statutes and my rules; if a person does them, he shall live by them: I am the LORD" (18:5). Obedience → life. Quoted in Rom 10:5, Gal 3:12.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Distinct morality*

#### 18:6-18 — Forbidden Incestuous Unions
**Lore:** "None of you shall approach any one of his close relatives to uncover nakedness (גָּלָה עֶרְוָה, galah ervah, euphemism for sexual relations)" (18:6). Prohibited: mother, stepmother, sister, half-sister, granddaughter, aunt, daughter-in-law, sister-in-law, stepdaughter, woman and her daughter/granddaughter (18:7-18). Incest violates family bonds and human dignity. Protects vulnerable.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Family sanctity*

#### 18:19-30 — Other Sexual Sins and Land Defilement
**Lore:** Also forbidden: menstruating woman (18:19), neighbor's wife (adultery, 18:20), child sacrifice to Molech (18:21), homosexual acts ("you shall not lie with a male as with a woman; it is an abomination (תּוֹעֵבָה, to'evah)" 18:22), bestiality (18:23). Warnings: "Do not make yourselves unclean by any of these things, for by all these the nations I am driving out before you have become unclean, and the land became unclean, so that I punished its iniquity, and the land vomited out its inhabitants" (18:24-25). Sexual immorality defiles the land itself. "Lest the land vomit you out" (18:28). "I am the LORD your God" (18:30). 1 Cor 6:18-20: body is temple; sexual sin defiles it. Matt 5:27-28: Jesus expands to lust.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Sexual holiness*

### Chapter 19: The Heart of Holiness

#### 19:1-10 — Holiness Begins: Sabbath, Idols, Gleaning
**Lore:** "You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy (קְדֹשִׁים תִּהְיוּ כִּי קָדוֹשׁ אֲנִי)" (19:2). THE verse of Leviticus. Revere parents, keep Sabbaths (19:3). No idols (19:4). Peace offering regulations (19:5-8). Gleaning laws: leave field corners and fallen grapes for poor and sojourner (19:9-10). Ruth 2. Economic justice = holiness.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Holiness begins*

#### 19:11-18 — Social Ethics and Neighbor Love
**Lore:** "You shall not steal; you shall not deal falsely; you shall not lie to one another" (19:11). No false oaths (19:12). "You shall not oppress your neighbor or rob him. The wages of a hired worker shall not remain with you all night" (19:13). Don't curse deaf or trip blind (19:14). Impartial justice (19:15). No slander or endangering life (19:16). "You shall not hate your brother in your heart, but you shall reason frankly with your neighbor, lest you incur sin because of him. You shall not take vengeance or bear a grudge against the sons of your own people, but you shall love your neighbor as yourself (וְאָהַבְתָּ לְרֵעֲךָ כָּמוֹךָ): I am the LORD" (19:17-18). **CLIMAX!** Jesus cites as 2nd greatest commandment (Matt 22:39). Paul: "whole law fulfilled in one word" (Gal 5:14). James: "royal law" (Jas 2:8).
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Love your neighbor*

#### 19:19-25 — Mixed Things and Firstfruits
**Lore:** Don't crossbreed livestock, sow mixed seed, wear mixed fabrics (19:19)—maintaining boundaries reflects God's order. Slave woman betrothed: inquiry, guilt offering (19:20-22). Fruit trees: 3 years forbidden, 4th year holy to LORD, 5th edible (19:23-25). Acknowledging God's provision in creation patterns.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Ordered creation*

#### 19:26-37 — Pagan Practices Forbidden, Love Foreigners
**Lore:** No eating blood, divination, fortune-telling (19:26). No cutting hair edges or beard (pagan mourning, 19:27). No body cuts or tattoos for dead (19:28). Don't prostitute daughter (19:29). Keep Sabbaths, revere sanctuary (19:30). No mediums/necromancers (19:31). Honor elderly (19:32). "When a stranger sojourns with you in your land, you shall not do him wrong. You shall treat the stranger who sojourns with you as the native among you, and you shall love him as yourself, for you were strangers in the land of Egypt" (19:33-34). **Love the foreigner**—revolutionary! Honest business (19:35-36). "I am the LORD" appears 16x in ch 19. Holiness encompasses worship, ethics, economics, justice, compassion.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Comprehensive holiness*

### Chapter 20: Penalties for Defilement

#### 20:1-9 — Death for Molech, Mediums, Cursing Parents
**Lore:** Penalties for ch 18-19 violations. Child sacrifice to Molech: death by stoning (20:2). If community ignores it, God cuts off offender and family (20:3-5). Consulting mediums: God cuts off (20:6). "Consecrate yourselves, therefore, and be holy, for I am the LORD your God. Keep my statutes and do them; I am the LORD who sanctifies you" (20:7-8). Cursing parents: death (20:9). Echoes Ex 21:17. Severe penalties enforce holiness.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Capital offenses*

#### 20:10-21 — Sexual Sins and Their Penalties
**Lore:** Adultery: both die (20:10). Incest with stepmother, daughter-in-law: death (20:11-12). Homosexual acts: death (20:13). Marrying woman and her mother: all three burned (20:14). Bestiality: death for human and animal (20:15-16). Sexual relations with sister, menstruating woman, aunt: cut off or childless (20:17-21). Graduated penalties: some death, some exile/childlessness. Protects community holiness.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Sexual penalties*

#### 20:22-27 — Land Separation and Holiness
**Lore:** "You shall therefore keep all my statutes and all my rules and do them, that the land where I am bringing you to live may not vomit you out" (20:22). Land itself rejects unholy. "And you shall be holy to me, for I the LORD am holy and have separated you from the peoples, that you should be mine" (20:26). Israel's distinctiveness = holiness. Medium/necromancer: death by stoning (20:27). Exile fulfills 20:22—land "vomits" Israel in Babylonian captivity (2 Chr 36:20-21).
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Distinct for God*

## Part VI: Priestly and Festival Holiness (Chapters 21-24)

### Chapter 21: Priests Held to Higher Standard

#### 21:1-9 — General Priests: Limited Contact with Death
**Lore:** Priests may not touch corpses except close relatives: mother, father, son, daughter, brother, unmarried sister (21:1-3). No pagan mourning rituals: baldness, beard shaving, body cuts (21:5). "They shall be holy to their God and not profane the name of their God" (21:6). May not marry prostitutes, divorced, or defiled women; only virgins or priestly widows (21:7-8). Priest's daughter who prostitutes: burned with fire (21:9). Higher standard for those who serve.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Priestly purity*

#### 21:10-24 — High Priest and Physical Defects
**Lore:** High priest: even stricter. No letting hair loose or tearing clothes (mourning signs). May not go near ANY dead body, even parents (21:10-11). "He shall not go out of the sanctuary, lest he profane the sanctuary of his God, for the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is upon him" (21:12). Must marry virgin of his own people (21:13-15). Physical defects disqualify from approaching altar: blind, lame, mutilated, deformed, broken foot/hand, hunchback, dwarf, eye defect, skin disease, crushed testicles (21:16-21). May eat holy food but not approach veil or altar (21:22-23). Maintains unblemished standard—everything offered to God must be perfect. Christ: perfect High Priest, "holy, innocent, unstained" (Heb 7:26).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Unblemished priesthood*

### Chapter 22: Acceptable Sacrifices

#### 22:1-16 — Priests Must Be Clean to Eat Holy Food
**Lore:** Priests with uncleanness (discharge, leprosy, touched corpse) must not eat holy food until clean (22:1-7). Dead animals/torn animals = unclean (22:8). Priest's family may eat (22:10-13), but not hired workers. Accidental eating requires restitution + 20% (22:14-16). Profaning holy things brings guilt. Extreme care required.
*Verses: 16 | Theme: Holy food protection*

#### 22:17-33 — Only Unblemished Sacrifices Accepted
**Lore:** Animals offered must be without blemish—male for burnt offerings, either gender for freewill (22:18-19). Nothing with defect acceptable: blind, injured, maimed, ulcer, scab, discharge (22:20-24). Even foreigners' animals must be unblemished (22:25). Newborn must be with mother 7 days; from 8th day acceptable (22:26-27). Don't slaughter cow/ewe and offspring same day (22:28). Thanksgiving offering eaten same day (22:29-30). "So you shall keep my commandments and do them: I am the LORD. And you shall not profane my holy name, that I may be sanctified among the people of Israel. I am the LORD who sanctifies you, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God: I am the LORD" (22:31-33). Fourfold "I am the LORD." Perfection required. Christ: unblemished Lamb (1 Pet 1:19).
*Verses: 17 | Theme: Perfection required*

### Chapter 23: The Sacred Calendar

#### 23:1-8 — Sabbath, Passover, Unleavened Bread
**Lore:** "These are the appointed feasts (מוֹעֲדִים, mo'adim, "appointed times") of the LORD that you shall proclaim as holy convocations" (23:2). Weekly Sabbath: 7th day rest (23:3). Passover: 14th day, 1st month, twilight (23:5). Unleavened Bread: 15th day, 7 days, first and last are holy convocations, no work (23:6-8). Israel's calendar structured around God's rhythms.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Weekly and spring feasts*

#### 23:9-22 — Firstfruits and Pentecost
**Lore:** Firstfruits: Day after Sabbath during Unleavened Bread, wave sheaf before LORD, burnt and grain offerings (23:9-14). No eating new grain until offering made. Pentecost/Weeks: count 50 days from Firstfruits, bring two leavened loaves (unique—leaven allowed since not burned), burnt offerings, sin offering, peace offerings (23:15-20). Reiterate gleaning laws (23:21-22). Firstfruits = Christ resurrected, "firstfruits of those who have slept" (1 Cor 15:20). Pentecost = Holy Spirit outpouring (Acts 2).
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Harvest feasts*

#### 23:23-32 — Trumpets and Day of Atonement
**Lore:** Trumpets/Rosh Hashanah: 1st day, 7th month, trumpet blast, holy convocation, no work (23:23-25). Day of Atonement/Yom Kippur: 10th day, 7th month, "afflict yourselves" (fast), no work (23:26-28). "For on this day shall atonement be made for you to cleanse you. You shall be clean before the LORD from all your sins" (23:28). Most solemn day. Anyone not fasting or working = cut off/destroyed (23:29-30). "Sabbath of solemn rest" (23:32). Trumpets = final trumpet, resurrection (1 Thess 4:16). Yom Kippur = judgment, heavenly sanctuary cleansing (SDA theology, Dan 8:14).
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Fall solemn days*

#### 23:33-44 — Tabernacles and Festival Summary
**Lore:** Tabernacles/Booths/Sukkot: 15th day, 7th month, 7 days, first and 8th days holy convocations (23:33-36). Take choice fruit, palm fronds, leafy branches, willows; rejoice before LORD (23:40). "You shall dwell in booths for seven days...that your generations may know that I made the people of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God" (23:42-43). Reenact wilderness dwelling. Summary: Moses declared feasts (23:44). Tabernacles = Millennium or eternal dwelling with God (Zech 14:16, Rev 21:3). Annual calendar reenacts Israel's history and foreshadows Christ.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Dwelling with God*

### Chapter 24: Sanctuary Light and Blasphemy Case

#### 24:1-9 — Lampstand and Showbread
**Lore:** "Command the people of Israel to bring you pure oil from beaten olives for the lamp, that a light may be kept burning regularly (תָּמִיד, tamid, "continually")" (24:1-2). Aaron arranges lamps evening to morning (24:3-4). Twelve loaves of showbread on pure gold table, fresh each Sabbath, eaten by priests in holy place (24:5-9). "It is for him and his descendants as his portion forever from the LORD's food offerings" (24:9). Perpetual light = God's eternal presence. Bread = God's ongoing provision. Jesus: "bread of life" (John 6:35).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Light and bread*

#### 24:10-23 — Blasphemy Judged, Eye for Eye
**Lore:** Israelite woman's son (father Egyptian) blasphemed the Name (YHWH) and cursed in fight (24:10-11). Brought to Moses, God commands: all who heard lay hands on blasphemer's head, congregation stone him (24:13-14). "Whoever blasphemes the name of the LORD shall surely be put to death. All the congregation shall stone him. The sojourner as well as the native, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death" (24:15-16). Lex talionis: "Whoever takes a human life shall surely be put to death...If anyone injures his neighbor, as he has done it shall be done to him, fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth" (24:17-20). Proportional justice, not personal vengeance. Jesus reinterprets: "Do not resist the one who is evil. But if anyone slaps you on the right cheek, turn to him the other also" (Matt 5:38-39). Legal justice → personal mercy. Same law for native and foreigner (24:22). Blasphemer stoned (24:23). Holiness undergirds justice.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Justice and holiness*
