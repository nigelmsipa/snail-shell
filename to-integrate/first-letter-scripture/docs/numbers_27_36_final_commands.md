# Numbers 27-36: Final Laws and Moses's Last Words

## Part XIII: Leadership Transition and Offerings (Chapters 27-30)

### Chapter 27: Zelophehad's Daughters and Joshua Commissioned

#### 27:1-11 — Daughters' Inheritance Rights
**Lore:** "Then drew near the daughters of Zelophehad the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the clans of Manasseh the son of Joseph. The names of his daughters were: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah" (27:1). Five brave women approach Moses, Eleazar, leaders, and congregation at tent entrance (27:2). Their case: "Our father died in the wilderness. He was not among the company of those who gathered themselves together against the LORD in the company of Korah, but died for his own sin. And he had no sons. Why should the name of our father be taken away from his clan because he had no son? Give to us a possession among our father's brothers'" (27:3-4). Zel

ophehad died in wilderness (not in Korah's rebellion), left no sons—only daughters. They request inheritance to preserve father's name. Bold petition! Moses brings case before LORD (27:5). "And the LORD said to Moses, 'The daughters of Zelophehad are right. You shall give them possession of an inheritance among their father's brothers and transfer the inheritance of their father to them. And you shall speak to the people of Israel, saying, "If a man dies and has no son, then you shall transfer his inheritance to his daughter"'" (27:6-8). God affirms women's rights! Establishes inheritance order: son, daughter, brothers, father's brothers, nearest kinsman (27:8-11). Revolutionary for ancient world. Women valued, protected. Foreshadows Mary, Elizabeth, other godly women in Scripture. Church: no male/female in Christ (Gal 3:28).
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Women's inheritance*

#### 27:12-23 — Moses Views Land, Joshua Commissioned
**Lore:** "The LORD said to Moses, 'Go up into this mountain of Abarim and see the land that I have given to the people of Israel. When you have seen it, you also shall be gathered to your people, as your brother Aaron was, because you rebelled against my word in the wilderness of Zin when the congregation quarreled, failing to uphold me as holy at the waters before their eyes'" (27:12-14). Moses allowed to see Canaan, not enter (cf. Deut 32:48-52; 34:1-4). Consequence of Meribah sin (20:7-12). Moses's response: selfless concern for people. "Let the LORD, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation who shall go out before them and come in before them, who shall lead them out and bring them in, that the congregation of the LORD may not be as sheep that have no shepherd" (27:16-17). Jesus uses this imagery (Matt 9:36). God: "Take Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the Spirit, and lay your hand on him. Make him stand before Eleazar the priest and all the congregation, and you shall commission him in their sight" (27:18-19). Public ordination. "You shall invest him with some of your authority, that all the congregation of the people of Israel may obey" (27:20). Transfer of leadership. Joshua must inquire of Eleazar with Urim for decisions (27:21). Moses obeys: lays hands on Joshua before Eleazar and congregation, commissions him (27:22-23). Deuteronomy 34:9: "And Joshua...was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him." Smooth transition. Christ = ultimate Joshua (same Hebrew name: יְהוֹשֻׁעַ, Yehoshua, "Yahweh saves"), leading God's people into promised rest (Heb 4:8-10).
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Successor appointed*

### Chapters 28-29: Daily and Festival Offerings

#### 28:1-15 — Daily, Sabbath, Monthly Offerings
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Command the people of Israel and say to them, "My offering, my food for my food offerings, my pleasing aroma, you shall be careful to offer to me at its appointed time"'" (28:1-2). Detailed sacrificial calendar for life in the land. **Daily (תָּמִיד, tamid, "continual"):** 2 male lambs (year old, unblemished), one morning, one evening, with grain/drink offerings (28:3-8). **Sabbath:** 2 additional lambs + grain/drink (28:9-10). **Monthly (New Moon):** 2 bulls, 1 ram, 7 lambs (burnt), 1 goat (sin), plus accompanying grain/drink (28:11-15). Establishes rhythm: daily, weekly, monthly. Continual worship. Hebrews 10:11: priests stand daily offering same sacrifices repeatedly. Christ offered once for all (Heb 10:12).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Continual worship*

#### 28:16-29:40 — Annual Festival Offerings
**Lore:** Detailed sacrifices for seven feasts: **Passover** (14th day, 1st month, 28:16). **Unleavened Bread** (15th-21st, 28:17-25): daily 2 bulls, 1 ram, 7 lambs, 1 goat. **Firstfruits/Weeks** (28:26-31): 2 bulls, 1 ram, 7 lambs, 1 goat. **Trumpets** (1st day, 7th month, 29:1-6): 1 bull, 1 ram, 7 lambs, 1 goat. **Day of Atonement** (10th day, 7th month, 29:7-11): 1 bull, 1 ram, 7 lambs, 1 goat, plus special bull/goat for atonement (Lev 16). **Tabernacles/Booths** (15th-21st, 7th month, 29:12-38): most elaborate—descending bulls (13, 12, 11...7 over 7 days), constant rams (2), lambs (14), goats (1). **8th Day Assembly** (29:35-38): 1 bull, 1 ram, 7 lambs, 1 goat. Summary: "These you shall offer to the LORD at your appointed feasts, in addition to your vow offerings and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, and for your grain offerings, and for your drink offerings, and for your peace offerings" (29:39). These are minimums! Massive animal requirement—hundreds yearly. Anticipates land's abundance. Festival cycle reenacts Israel's history, worship, gratitude. Christ fulfills all: Passover Lamb (1 Cor 5:7), Firstfruits (1 Cor 15:20), Atonement (Heb 9:11-14), Tabernacle (John 1:14, "dwelt [tabernacled] among us").
*Verses: 89 | Theme: Festival worship*

### Chapter 30: Laws of Vows

#### 30:1-16 — Binding and Annulling Vows
**Lore:** "Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the people of Israel, saying, 'This is what the LORD has commanded. If a man vows a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath to bind himself by a pledge, he shall not break his word. He shall do according to all that proceeds out of his mouth'" (30:1-2). Men's vows binding. **Woman's vows** have conditions: **Young woman in father's house** (30:3-5): father hears vow, may confirm or annul same day. If silent, vow stands. If annuls, LORD forgives her. **Married woman** (30:6-8): husband hears, may confirm or annul same day. **Widow or divorced woman** (30:9): vows binding (no male authority). **Wife's vow** (30:10-15): husband may confirm or annul when he hears. If silent, vow stands. If annuls later, he bears her guilt. Balances personal devotion with family authority structure. Ecclesiastes 5:4-5: "When you vow a vow to God, do not delay paying it...It is better that you should not vow than that you should vow and not pay." Jesus: "Let what you say be simply 'Yes' or 'No'" (Matt 5:37). Don't make rash vows. James 5:12 echoes this.
*Verses: 16 | Theme: Sacred vows*

## Part XIV: Vengeance, Inheritance, Journey Summary (Chapters 31-33)

### Chapter 31: War Against Midian

#### 31:1-24 — Midian Defeated and Plunder Purified
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Avenge the people of Israel on the Midianites. Afterward you shall be gathered to your people'" (31:1-2). Moses's last military command. Fulfills 25:16-18. "So Moses spoke to the people, saying, 'Arm men from among you for the war, that they may go against Midian to execute the LORD's vengeance on Midian'" (31:3). 12,000 warriors (1,000 per tribe), Phinehas leads with holy vessels and trumpets (31:4-6). "They warred against Midian, as the LORD commanded Moses, and killed every male" (31:7). Kill five Midianite kings and Balaam (31:8). "But Moses was angry with the officers...who had come from service in the war. Moses said to them, 'Have you let all the women live? Behold, these, on Balaam's counsel, caused the people of Israel to act treacherously against the LORD in the incident of Peor, and so the plague came among the congregation of the LORD'" (31:14-16). Reveals Balaam's role in Baal-Peor (ch 25)! Orders: kill all male children and non-virgin women; keep virgins alive (31:17-18). Harsh but just—those who seduced Israel must die. Warriors and captives purify outside camp 7 days (31:19-24). Everything passing through fire purified (31:23). Holy war requires holy soldiers.
*Verses: 24 | Theme: Righteous vengeance*

#### 31:25-54 — Division of Spoils and Offering
**Lore:** Plunder counted: 675,000 sheep, 72,000 cattle, 61,000 donkeys, 32,000 virgins (31:32-35). Divided half to warriors, half to congregation (31:27). From warriors' half: 1/500 tribute to LORD (for Eleazar, 31:28-29). From congregation's half: 1/50 to Levites (31:30). Moses and Eleazar obey (31:31-47). Officers report: not one soldier lost! (31:48-49). Miraculous preservation. They bring gold offering (ornaments, etc.): 16,750 shekels (31:50-52). "And Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds and brought it into the tent of meeting, as a memorial for the people of Israel before the LORD" (31:54). Gratitude offering. Victory = God's, not theirs. Dedicating spoils acknowledges His sovereignty.
*Verses: 30 | Theme: Grateful dedication*

### Chapter 32: Transjordan Tribes

#### 32:1-15 — Reuben and Gad Request East Side
**Lore:** "Now the people of Reuben and the people of Gad had a very great number of livestock, and they saw the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead, and behold, the place was a place for livestock" (32:1). Conquered territory east of Jordan (Sihon/Og's land) is ideal pasture. They request: "If we have found favor in your sight, let this land be given to your servants for a possession. Do not take us across the Jordan" (32:5). Moses angry: "Shall your brothers go to the war while you sit here? Why will you discourage the heart of the people of Israel from going over into the land that the LORD has given them?" (32:6-7). Accuses them of repeating fathers' sin (spy rebellion, 32:8-13). "And behold, you have risen in your fathers' place, a brood of sinful men, to increase still more the fierce anger of the LORD against Israel. For if you turn away from following him, he will again abandon them in the wilderness, and you will destroy all this people" (32:14-15). Harsh rebuke—fears division will ruin conquest.
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Loyalty tested*

#### 32:16-42 — Agreement Reached, Land Settled
**Lore:** Reuben/Gad propose: "We will build sheepfolds here for our livestock, and cities for our little ones, but we will take up arms, ready to go before the people of Israel, until we have brought them to their place. And we will not return to our homes until each of the people of Israel has gained his inheritance" (32:16-18). Fight first, settle after. Moses accepts: "If you will do this, if you will take up arms to go before the LORD for the war, and every armed man of you will pass over the Jordan before the LORD, until he has driven out his enemies from before him and the land is subdued before the LORD, then after that you shall return and be free of obligation to the LORD and to Israel, and this land shall be your possession before the LORD" (32:20-22). Conditional approval. Half-tribe of Manasseh joins (32:33). They build cities, fortify them (32:34-42). Joshua 22:1-6: after conquest, Joshua releases them with blessing. Shows unity maintained. Sometimes God's people have different roles, but same mission.
*Verses: 27 | Theme: Conditional inheritance*

### Chapter 33: Journey Itinerary

#### 33:1-49 — Forty-Two Encampments Listed
**Lore:** "These are the stages of the people of Israel, when they went out of the land of Egypt by their companies under the leadership of Moses and Aaron. Moses wrote down their starting places, stage by stage, by command of the LORD" (33:1-2). Complete travel log. From Rameses (Egypt, 33:3-5) through wilderness: Succoth, Etham, Pi-hahiroth, Marah, Elim, Red Sea, Sin, Dophkah...Sinai (33:15), Kibroth-hattaavah, Hazeroth, Rithmah...Kadesh (33:36), Mount Hor (Aaron died, 33:38-39)...to plains of Moab (33:48-49). Forty-two stops over 40 years. Some stayed briefly, others (like Kadesh) for years. Each name = memory. God guided every step. Travel log = testimony to God's faithfulness through long, hard journey. Application: Christian life = journey with divine guidance. Hebrews 11:13: "These all died in faith, not having received the things promised, but having seen them and greeted them from afar, and having acknowledged that they were strangers and exiles on the earth."
*Verses: 49 | Theme: God's guidance remembered*

#### 33:50-56 — Command to Drive Out Canaanites
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying, 'Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, When you pass over the Jordan into the land of Canaan, then you shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you and destroy all their figured stones and destroy all their metal images and demolish all their high places'" (33:50-52). Complete removal commanded. Why? "And you shall take possession of the land and settle in it, for I have given the land to you to possess it" (33:53). God's gift requires obedience. Warning: "But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you, then those of them whom you let remain shall be as barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides, and they shall trouble you in the land where you dwell. And I will do to you as I thought to do to them" (33:55-56). Failure = ongoing trouble. Judges 1-3 shows this prophesy fulfilled. Incomplete obedience = incomplete victory. Spiritual application: sin not fully dealt with continues to plague us (Heb 12:1).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Total obedience required*
