# Deuteronomy 12-34: Law Code, Blessings, and Moses's Farewell

## Part V: The Law Code - Second Speech (Chapters 12-26)

### Chapters 12-16: Worship and Festival Laws (Summary)

#### 12:1-14 — One Central Sanctuary
**Lore:** "These are the statutes and rules that you shall be careful to do in the land...You shall surely destroy all the places where the nations whom you shall dispossess served their gods...You shall tear down their altars...But you shall seek the place that the LORD your God will choose out of all your tribes to put his name and make his habitation there" (12:1-5). Centralized worship. Later = Jerusalem Temple. Prevents syncretism.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: One place of worship*

#### 13:1-18 — Test False Prophets and Idolaters
**Lore:** If prophet or dreamer gives signs but says "Let us go after other gods," do not listen (13:1-5). Even if family member entices to idolatry, stone them (13:6-11). If city turns to idolatry, devote it to destruction (13:12-18). Absolute loyalty to Yahweh required.
*Verses: 18 | Theme: Loyalty to God alone*

#### 14:1-21 — Holiness and Dietary Laws
**Lore:** "You are the sons of the LORD your God...You are a people holy to the LORD your God" (14:1-2). Clean/unclean animal laws (echoes Lev 11). Holy living includes diet. "You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk" (14:21). Compassion even in food prep.
*Verses: 21 | Theme: Holy living*

#### 14:22-15:23 — Tithes, Sabbath Year, Firstborn
**Lore:** Tithe annually, every third year for Levites/poor (14:22-29). Every seventh year cancel debts (15:1-11). "There will never cease to be poor in the land...you shall open your hand to your brother" (15:11). Jesus quotes this (Matt 26:11). Release Hebrew servants in seventh year with generous gifts (15:12-18). Consecrate firstborn animals (15:19-23).
*Verses: 73 | Theme: Generosity and rest*

#### 16:1-17 — Three Pilgrimage Festivals
**Lore:** **Passover** (16:1-8): Remember Exodus. **Weeks/Pentecost** (16:9-12): Harvest thanksgiving. **Tabernacles/Booths** (16:13-17): Autumn ingathering. All males appear before LORD three times yearly at chosen place.
*Verses: 17 | Theme: Festival worship*

### Chapters 17-21: Leadership, Justice, War (Summary)

#### 17:8-20 — Judges and Kings
**Lore:** Difficult cases brought to Levitical priests and judge at central sanctuary (17:8-13). King must: not multiply horses, wives, or gold; write own copy of law; read it daily (17:14-20). Limits royal power. Solomon violated this (1 Kgs 10-11).
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Humble leadership*

#### 18:9-22 — True vs. False Prophets
**Lore:** Forbid divination, sorcery, necromancy (18:9-14). "The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you, from your brothers—it is to him you shall listen" (18:15). **Messianic prophecy!** Acts 3:22 applies to Jesus. Test: if prophet's word doesn't come true, he's false (18:21-22).
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Prophet like Moses*

#### 19:1-21 — Cities of Refuge and Witnesses
**Lore:** Three refuge cities west of Jordan (plus three east, 4:41-43) for accidental killers (19:1-13). Property boundaries sacred (19:14). Two/three witnesses required for conviction (19:15). False witness receives penalty they sought for accused (19:16-21). "Eye for eye, tooth for tooth" = lex talionis, limits vengeance.
*Verses: 21 | Theme: Justice and mercy*

#### 20:1-20 — Laws of Warfare
**Lore:** "When you go out to war...do not be afraid...for the LORD your God is he who goes with you to fight for you" (20:1-4). Exemptions: newly built house, new vineyard, betrothed, fearful (20:5-9). Offer peace first to distant cities; if refuse, besiege (20:10-15). Canaanite cities: devote to destruction completely (20:16-18). Don't destroy fruit trees during siege (20:19-20). Ecological wisdom in warfare!
*Verses: 20 | Theme: Holy war*

#### 21:1-23 — Unsolved Murder, Captive Wives, Firstborn Rights
**Lore:** Unsolved murder: nearest city performs heifer ritual to atone (21:1-9). Captive woman may become wife after month of mourning (21:10-14). Firstborn son gets double inheritance even if from unloved wife (21:15-17). Rebellious son stoned (21:18-21). Executed person's body not left hanging overnight (21:22-23). Paul applies this to Christ (Gal 3:13): "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree."
*Verses: 23 | Theme: Diverse laws*

### Chapters 22-26: Social Laws and Firstfruits (Summary)

#### 22:1-23:14 — Purity and Community Laws
**Lore:** Return lost property (22:1-4). No cross-dressing (22:5). Don't take mother bird with eggs (22:6-7). Build parapet on roof (22:8). No mixing: seeds, ox/donkey plowing, wool/linen (22:9-11). Tassels on garments (22:12). Sexual purity laws (22:13-30). Assembly restrictions (23:1-8). Camp purity when at war (23:9-14). Diverse regulations maintaining holiness.
*Verses: 80 | Theme: Holy community*

#### 23:15-24:22 — Justice for Vulnerable
**Lore:** Don't return runaway slave to master (23:15-16). No temple prostitution (23:17-18). No interest on loans to Israelites (23:19-20). Keep vows promptly (23:21-23). Eat neighbor's grapes/grain when passing through (23:24-25). Divorce certificate required if man dismisses wife (24:1-4). Newlyweds: husband exempt from war one year (24:5). Don't take millstones as pledge (24:6). Kidnapping = death (24:7). Leprosy protocol (24:8-9). Collateral laws (24:10-13). Pay wages same day (24:14-15). "Fathers shall not be put to death because of their children, nor shall children be put to death because of their fathers. Each one shall be put to death for his own sin" (24:16). Individual responsibility! Justice for sojourner, widow, orphan (24:17-22). Compassionate laws.
*Verses: 44 | Theme: Protect the vulnerable*

#### 25:1-19 — Fairness and Amalek
**Lore:** Flogging limit: 40 lashes (25:1-3). "You shall not muzzle an ox when it treads out the grain" (25:4). Paul applies to ministers (1 Cor 9:9; 1 Tim 5:18). Levirate marriage: brother-in-law marries childless widow to preserve name (25:5-10). Jesus references this (Matt 22:23-33). Honest weights and measures (25:13-16). "Remember what Amalek did to you on the way as you came out of Egypt, how he attacked you on the way when you were faint and weary, and cut off your tail, those who were lagging behind you, and he did not fear God. Therefore when the LORD your God has given you rest from all your enemies around you...you shall blot out the memory of Amalek from under heaven; you shall not forget" (25:17-19). Amalek = perpetual enemy (1 Sam 15; Esther 3).
*Verses: 19 | Theme: Justice and remembrance*

#### 26:1-19 — Firstfruits and Tithe Confession
**Lore:** When entering land, bring firstfruits to priest, recite: "A wandering Aramean was my father. And he went down into Egypt and sojourned there, few in number, and there he became a nation, great, mighty, and populous. And the Egyptians treated us harshly and humiliated us and laid on us hard labor. Then we cried to the LORD, the God of our fathers, and the LORD heard our voice and saw our affliction, our toil, and our oppression. And the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, with great deeds of terror, with signs and wonders. And he brought us into this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey" (26:5-9). **Creedal confession!** Condenses Exodus-Conquest story. Third-year tithe for Levites/poor (26:12-15). "You have declared today that the LORD is your God, and that you will walk in his ways, and keep his statutes and his commandments and his rules, and will obey his voice. And the LORD has declared today that you are a people for his treasured possession, as he has promised you, and that you are to keep all his commandments, and that he will set you in praise and in fame and in honor high above all nations that he has made, and that you shall be a people holy to the LORD your God, as he has promised" (26:17-19). Mutual covenant commitment.
*Verses: 19 | Theme: Covenant confession*

## Part VI: Blessings and Curses (Chapters 27-30)

### Chapter 27: Altar and Curses

#### 27:1-26 — Law on Plastered Stones, Twelve Curses
**Lore:** Upon entering land, set up large stones on Mount Ebal, plaster them, write law on them (27:2-4). Build altar of uncut stones, offer burnt offerings (27:5-7). "And Moses and the Levitical priests said to all Israel, 'Keep silence and hear, O Israel: this day you have become the people of the LORD your God. You shall therefore obey the voice of the LORD your God, keeping his commandments and his statutes, which I command you today'" (27:9-10). Then Levites pronounce twelve curses (27:15-26): idolatry, dishonoring parents, moving boundary, misleading blind, injustice to vulnerable, sexual sins, murder for hire, not confirming law. People respond "Amen!" to each. Public covenant ceremony binding all.
*Verses: 26 | Theme: Covenant curses*

### Chapter 28: Blessings and Curses

#### 28:1-14 — Blessings for Obedience
**Lore:** "And if you faithfully obey the voice of the LORD your God, being careful to do all his commandments that I command you today, the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth. And all these blessings shall come upon you and overtake you" (28:1-2). Blessings: city/field, womb/ground/livestock, basket/kneading bowl, coming/going (28:3-6). Enemies defeated (28:7). Barns blessed (28:8). Established as holy people (28:9). Nations fear you (28:10). Abundant prosperity (28:11). Rain in season (28:12). Head not tail, above not beneath (28:13). Condition: obey, don't turn to other gods (28:14).
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Abundant blessings*

#### 28:15-68 — Curses for Disobedience
**Lore:** "But if you will not obey the voice of the LORD your God or be careful to do all his commandments and his statutes that I command you today, then all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you" (28:15). Reversals of blessings: cursed city/field, basket/womb, defeated by enemies, confusion, frustration (28:16-20). Plagues, diseases, drought (28:21-24). Defeat, corpses for birds (28:25-26). Egypt's diseases (28:27). Madness, blindness, confusion (28:28-29). Betrothed wife taken, house/vineyard/flock stolen (28:30-31). Children enslaved (28:32). Crops devoured by foreigners (28:33). Oppression, robbery (28:29, 33). Boils, tumors, scurvy, itch (28:27, 35). Exile to foreign gods (28:36-37). Locusts, worms destroy crops (28:38-42). Foreigners rise above you (28:43-44). "All these curses shall come upon you and pursue you and overtake you till you are destroyed, because you did not obey the voice of the LORD your God" (28:45). More horrors: besieged, famine, cannibalism (28:52-57). "The LORD will bring you back in ships to Egypt...and there you shall offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, but there will be no buyer" (28:68). Full circle to Egypt! Fulfilled in Babylonian exile, Roman destruction. Longest curse section in Bible. Sobering warning.
*Verses: 54 | Theme: Escalating curses*

### Chapters 29-30: Covenant Renewal

#### 29:1-29 — Moab Covenant Summary
**Lore:** Moses summons Israel: "You have seen all that the LORD did before your eyes in the land of Egypt...the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs, and those great wonders. But to this day the LORD has not given you a heart to understand or eyes to see or ears to hear" (29:2-4). Need internal transformation. Recalls 40 years wilderness provision (29:5-6). Conquered Sihon and Og (29:7-8). "Therefore keep the words of this covenant and do them, that you may prosper in all that you do" (29:9). Warning against idolatry (29:16-28). "The secret things belong to the LORD our God, but the things that are revealed belong to us and to our children forever, that we may do all the words of this law" (29:29). Famous verse! God's mystery vs. revealed law.
*Verses: 29 | Theme: Hidden and revealed*

#### 30:1-20 — Choose Life!
**Lore:** When blessings/curses come and you're scattered among nations, if you return to LORD with all heart, He will restore you (30:1-5). "And the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your offspring, so that you will love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, that you may live" (30:6). **Heart circumcision by God Himself!** New covenant promise (Jer 31:31-34; Ezek 36:26). "For this commandment that I command you today is not too hard for you, neither is it far off...But the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it" (30:11, 14). Paul quotes this (Rom 10:6-8). **CLIMAX:** "See, I have set before you today life and good, death and evil...I call heaven and earth to witness against you today, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Therefore choose life, that you and your offspring may live, loving the LORD your God, obey his voice, and holding fast to him, for he is your life and length of days, that you may dwell in the land that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give them" (30:15, 19-20). **Ultimate choice!** Joshua 24:15; John 14:6. Every human faces this decision.
*Verses: 20 | Theme: Choose life or death*

## Part VII: Moses's Farewell (Chapters 31-34)

### Chapter 31: Joshua Commissioned, Song Written

#### 31:1-29 — Leadership Transition and Prophetic Song
**Lore:** Moses: "I am 120 years old today. I am no longer able to go out and come in. The LORD has said to me, 'You shall not go over this Jordan.' The LORD your God himself will go over before you" (31:2-3). Commissions Joshua publicly (31:7-8, 23). Moses writes law, gives to priests to read every seventh year at Feast of Tabernacles (31:9-13). God appears in pillar of cloud (31:15). Predicts Israel's future apostasy: "And the LORD said to Moses, 'Behold, you are about to lie down with your fathers. Then this people will rise and whore after the foreign gods among them in the land that they are entering, and they will forsake me and break my covenant that I have made with them'" (31:16). Commands Moses to write song as witness against them (31:19-22). Song of Moses (ch 32). Moses finishes writing law, places in ark (31:24-26). "For I know how rebellious and stubborn you are...How much more after my death!" (31:27). Summons elders to hear song (31:28-30).
*Verses: 29 | Theme: Transition and warning*

### Chapter 32: The Song of Moses

#### 32:1-47 — Poetic Prophecy
**Lore:** Magnificent Hebrew poetry. "Give ear, O heavens, and I will speak, and let the earth hear the words of my mouth. May my teaching drop as the rain, my speech distill as the dew" (32:1-2). Proclaims God's perfection (32:3-4). Israel's corruption (32:5-6). Recalls God's election and care (32:7-14). Israel's rebellion, idolatry (32:15-18). God's anger, hiding His face (32:19-25). But won't destroy completely lest enemies boast (32:26-27). Israel's foolishness (32:28-35). God will vindicate His people, judge enemies (32:36-43). "See now that I, even I, am he, and there is no god beside me; I kill and I make alive; I wound and I heal; and there is none that can deliver out of my hand" (32:39). Sovereignty declared. Ends: "Take to heart all the words by which I am warning you today...it is no empty word for you, but your very life" (32:46-47). Song = covenant lawsuit, prophecy, warning, hope.
*Verses: 47 | Theme: God's faithfulness despite rebellion*

#### 32:48-52 — Moses to Die on Nebo
**Lore:** "That very day the LORD spoke to Moses, 'Go up this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo...and view the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the people of Israel for a possession. And die on the mountain...as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor...because you broke faith with me in the midst of the people of Israel at the waters of Meribah-kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin, and because you did not treat me as holy in the midst of the people of Israel. For you shall see the land before you, but you shall not go there, into the land that I am giving to the people of Israel'" (32:48-52). Consequence of striking rock (Num 20:12). Tragic yet fitting end.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Moses's sentence*

### Chapter 33: Moses Blesses the Tribes

#### 33:1-29 — Tribal Blessings
**Lore:** "This is the blessing with which Moses the man of God blessed the people of Israel before his death" (33:1). Parallel to Jacob's blessings (Gen 49) and Balaam's oracles (Num 23-24). Blessings for each tribe with poetic imagery: Reuben (33:6), Judah (33:7), Levi (33:8-11), Benjamin (33:12), Joseph/Ephraim/Manasseh (33:13-17), Zebulun/Issachar (33:18-19), Gad (33:20-21), Dan (33:22), Naphtali (33:23), Asher (33:24-25). No Simeon mentioned (absorbed into Judah?). Concludes: "There is none like God, O Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to your help, through the skies in his majesty. The eternal God is your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms. And he thrust out the enemy before you and said, 'Destroy.' So Israel lived in safety, Jacob lived alone, in a land of grain and wine, whose heavens drop down dew. Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD, the shield of your help, and the sword of your triumph! Your enemies shall come fawning to you, and you shall tread upon their backs" (33:26-29). Triumphant conclusion! God's protection and blessing.
*Verses: 29 | Theme: Blessed tribes*

### Chapter 34: Moses Dies

#### 34:1-12 — Death of Moses, Joshua Succeeds
**Lore:** "Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, which is opposite Jericho. And the LORD showed him all the land, Gilead as far as Dan, all Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah as far as the western sea, the Negeb, and the Plain, that is, the Valley of Jericho the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar. And the LORD said to him, 'This is the land of which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, "I will give it to your offspring." I have let you see it with your eyes, but you shall not go over there'" (34:1-4). Sees entire Promised Land panoramically! "So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the LORD, and he buried him in the valley in the land of Moab opposite Beth-peor; but no one knows the place of his burial to this day" (34:5-6). **God Himself buries Moses!** Unknown grave prevents idolatry. "Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eye was undimmed, and his vigor unabated" (34:7). Died in full strength. "And the people of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days" (34:8). "And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him. So the people of Israel obeyed him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses" (34:9). Smooth transition. **Conclusion:** "And there has not arisen a prophet since in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face, none like him for all the signs and the wonders that the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land, and for all the mighty power and all the great deeds of terror that Moses did in the sight of all Israel" (34:10-12). Moses = greatest prophet until Jesus (John 1:17: "the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ"). Acts 3:22: Jesus = prophet like Moses. Hebrews 3:3: "Jesus has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses." Deuteronomy ends. Torah complete. Joshua leads into Canaan.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Moses's death*
