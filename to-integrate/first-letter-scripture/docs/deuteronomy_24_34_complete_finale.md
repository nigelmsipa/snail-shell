# Deuteronomy 24-34: Justice, Blessings, Curses, and Finale

## Part IX: Justice and Covenant (Chapters 24-26)

### Chapter 24: Compassionate Laws

#### 24:1-9 — Divorce, Newlyweds, Pledges, Kidnapping
**Lore:** Divorce regulated (24:1-4): certificate required, cannot remarry first husband after marrying another. Jesus: Moses permitted because of hard hearts, not God's ideal (Matt 19:8). Newlywed exemption: one year free from military/civic duty (24:5). Millstones not taken as pledge—livelihood protection (24:6). Kidnapping = death (24:7). Follow priests re: leprosy (24:8-9).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Protect vulnerable*

#### 24:10-22 — Workers, Gleaning, Individual Responsibility
**Lore:** Respect debtor's dignity (24:10-13): return cloak by sunset. Pay workers same day (24:14-15). Individual responsibility—each dies for own sin (24:16), quoted 2 Kgs 14:6. Justice for sojourner, fatherless, widow (24:17-18). Gleaning laws (24:19-22): leave forgotten sheaves, olive gleanings, grape remnants for poor. Ruth 2 example.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Care for poor*

### Chapter 25: Flogging, Levirate, Weights, Amalek

#### 25:1-10 — Flogging Limits, Ox, Levirate Marriage
**Lore:** 40 lashes maximum (25:1-3). Muzzled ox may eat (25:4)—Paul applies to ministers (1 Cor 9:9). Levirate marriage (25:5-10): brother-in-law marries childless widow, raises heir. Refusal = sandal removal, spitting ceremony. Ruth 4 example.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Family duty*

#### 25:11-19 — Honest Weights, Destroy Amalek
**Lore:** Immodest wife penalty (25:11-12). Honest scales commanded (25:13-16): dishonesty = abomination. Remember Amalek's cowardly attack (25:17-19): blot out memory. Fulfilled partially by Saul (1 Sam 15), Haman defeated (Esther).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Integrity, remember Amalek*

### Chapter 26: Firstfruits and Covenant

#### 26:1-11 — Firstfruits Confession
**Lore:** Bring firstfruits to sanctuary, recite creed (26:1-11): "Wandering Aramean was my father...Egypt...LORD brought us out...gave us land." Critical liturgical confession of salvation history. Personal appropriation. Rejoice with Levites and sojourners.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Salvation confession*

#### 26:12-19 — Tithe Declaration, Mutual Covenant
**Lore:** Third-year tithe declaration (26:12-15): given to Levite, sojourner, fatherless, widow. Confession of obedience. Mutual covenant (26:16-19): Israel declares LORD is God; LORD declares Israel is treasured possession, holy people.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Covenant commitment*

## Part X: Blessings and Curses (Chapters 27-30)

### Chapter 27: Altar and Curses

#### 27:1-10 — Altar on Mount Ebal, Write the Law
**Lore:** Upon entering land, set up large stones on Ebal, plaster them, write law (27:1-4). Build altar of uncut stones, offer sacrifices (27:5-8). "Keep silence and hear, O Israel: this day you have become the people of the LORD your God" (27:9-10). Joshua 8:30-35 fulfills this.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Write law visibly*

#### 27:11-26 — Twelve Curses Pronounced
**Lore:** Six tribes on Gerizim (blessing), six on Ebal (curse) (27:11-13). Levites pronounce twelve curses (27:15-26): idolatry, dishonoring parents, moving boundary, misleading blind, perverting justice to vulnerable, incest variations, murder for hire, not confirming law. People respond "Amen!" Public covenant binding.
*Verses: 16 | Theme: Covenant curses*

### Chapter 28: Blessings and Curses Expanded

#### 28:1-14 — Blessings for Obedience
**Lore:** Obedience brings comprehensive blessings (28:1-14): city/field, womb/livestock, basket/kneading bowl blessed. Enemies defeated. Rain in season. Head not tail, above not beneath. All conditioned on obedience.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Abundant blessing*

#### 28:15-45 — Initial Curses for Disobedience
**Lore:** Disobedience brings comprehensive curses (28:15-45): disease, drought, defeat, madness, oppression, crops devoured. Slavery threatened. Long catalog of reversals of blessings. "The LORD will bring you and your king...to a nation that neither you nor your fathers have known" (28:36). Exile prophesied.
*Verses: 31 | Theme: Comprehensive curses*

#### 28:46-68 — Escalating Curses, Return to Egypt
**Lore:** More severe curses (28:46-68): siege, cannibalism (fulfilled 2 Kgs 6:28-29), scattered among nations, no rest, trembling heart. "The LORD will bring you back in ships to Egypt...and there you shall offer yourselves for sale...but there will be no buyer" (28:68). Ultimate reversal—return to slavery. Babylonian exile, Roman destruction fulfilled this. Longest curse passage in Bible. Sobering.
*Verses: 23 | Theme: Exile prophesied*

### Chapter 29: Moab Covenant

#### 29:1-29 — Covenant Renewal, Secret Things
**Lore:** Moses summons Israel, reviews Egypt/wilderness/conquest (29:1-8). "You have not yet been given a heart to understand" (29:4). Keep covenant (29:9). Warning against idolatry (29:16-28). "The secret things belong to the LORD our God, but the things that are revealed belong to us...that we may do all the words of this law" (29:29). Famous verse—God's mystery vs. revealed law.
*Verses: 29 | Theme: Revealed and hidden*

### Chapter 30: Return and Choose Life

#### 30:1-10 — Repentance Brings Restoration
**Lore:** When exiled, if repent and return to LORD with all heart, He will restore fortunes (30:1-5). "And the LORD your God will circumcise your heart...so that you will love the LORD...with all your heart" (30:6). New covenant promise! Jeremiah 31:31-34; Ezekiel 36:26.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Heart circumcision*

#### 30:11-20 — Choose Life or Death
**Lore:** "For this commandment...is not too hard for you" (30:11). "The word is very near you, in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it" (30:14). Paul quotes (Rom 10:6-8). **CLIMAX:** "I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Therefore choose life, that you and your offspring may live, loving the LORD your God, obeying his voice, and holding fast to him" (30:19-20). Most famous choice verse. Joshua 24:15 echoes. Ultimate decision every human faces.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Choose life*

## Part XI: Moses's Final Acts (Chapters 31-34)

### Chapter 31: Joshua Commissioned, Song Written

#### 31:1-13 — Moses Commissions Joshua, Law Reading
**Lore:** Moses 120 years old, cannot cross Jordan (31:1-2). Publicly commissions Joshua (31:7-8, 23). Writes law, gives to priests (31:9). Command: read law every seventh year at Feast of Tabernacles (31:10-13). Nehemiah 8 fulfills this.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Leadership transition*

#### 31:14-30 — God Predicts Rebellion, Commands Song
**Lore:** God appears in pillar of cloud (31:15). Predicts Israel's future apostasy: "Then this people will rise and whore after the foreign gods" (31:16). Commands Moses write song as witness (31:19-22). Song of Moses (ch 32). Moses places law beside ark (31:24-26). "For I know how rebellious and stubborn you are...How much more after my death!" (31:27).
*Verses: 17 | Theme: Prophetic warning*

### Chapter 32: The Song of Moses

#### 32:1-43 — Poetic Prophecy and God's Vindication
**Lore:** Magnificent Hebrew poetry (32:1-43). "Give ear, O heavens...May my teaching drop as the rain" (32:1-2). Proclaims God's perfection (32:3-4), Israel's corruption (32:5-6), God's election and care (32:7-14), Israel's rebellion (32:15-18), God's anger (32:19-25), yet won't destroy completely (32:26-27). "See now that I, even I, am he, and there is no god beside me" (32:39). Sovereignty declared. God will vindicate His people, judge enemies (32:36-43). "For it is no empty word...but your very life" (32:47).
*Verses: 43 | Theme: Covenant lawsuit*

#### 32:48-52 — Moses Commanded to Die on Nebo
**Lore:** "Go up...Mount Nebo...and view the land of Canaan...And die on the mountain...as Aaron your brother died...because you broke faith with me...at the waters of Meribah-kadesh...you did not treat me as holy...For you shall see the land before you, but you shall not go there" (32:48-52). Consequence of striking rock (Num 20:12).
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Moses's sentence*

### Chapter 33: Tribal Blessings

#### 33:1-29 — Moses Blesses the Tribes
**Lore:** "This is the blessing with which Moses the man of God blessed the people of Israel before his death" (33:1). Poetic blessings for each tribe (33:6-25): Reuben, Judah, Levi, Benjamin, Joseph, Zebulun, Issachar, Gad, Dan, Naphtali, Asher. No Simeon. Concludes: "There is none like God, O Jeshurun...The eternal God is your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms" (33:26-27). "Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD?" (33:29).
*Verses: 29 | Theme: Final blessings*

### Chapter 34: Moses Dies

#### 34:1-12 — Death of Moses, Joshua Succeeds
**Lore:** Moses climbs Nebo, views entire Promised Land (34:1-4). "So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the LORD, and he buried him...but no one knows the place of his burial to this day" (34:5-6). God Himself buries Moses! Unknown grave prevents idolatry. "Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eye was undimmed, and his vigor unabated" (34:7). Died in full strength. People mourn 30 days (34:8). "Joshua...was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him" (34:9). Smooth transition. **Conclusion:** "And there has not arisen a prophet since in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face, none like him for all the signs and the wonders that the LORD sent him to do...and for all the mighty power and all the great deeds of terror that Moses did in the sight of all Israel" (34:10-12). Greatest prophet until Jesus (John 1:17; Acts 3:22; Heb 3:3). Torah complete. Joshua leads into Canaan.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Moses's death*
