# Deuteronomy 12-17: Proper Worship and Leadership Laws

## Part V: Second Speech - The Law Code (Chapters 12-26)

### Chapter 12: Centralized Worship

#### 12:1-7 — Destroy Idols, Worship at One Place
**Lore:** "These are the statutes and rules that you shall be careful to do in the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you to possess, all the days that you live on the earth. You shall surely destroy all the places where the nations whom you shall dispossess served their gods, on the high mountains and on the hills and under every green tree. You shall tear down their altars and dash in pieces their pillars and burn their Asherim with fire. You shall chop down the carved images of their gods and destroy their name out of that place" (12:1-3). Total eradication of Canaanite worship sites! "You shall not worship the LORD your God in that way. But you shall seek the place that the LORD your God will choose out of all your tribes to put his name and make his habitation there. There you shall go, and there you shall bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution that you present, your vow offerings, your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock. And there you shall eat before the LORD your God, and you shall rejoice" (12:4-7). **One central sanctuary** = later fulfilled by Jerusalem Temple. Prevents syncretism, unifies worship. Contrast with Canaanite "high places" scattered everywhere. Spiritual application: worship God His way, not ours (John 4:24).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: One place of worship*

#### 12:8-14 — Not Like Before, Only at Chosen Place
**Lore:** "You shall not do according to all that we are doing here today, everyone doing whatever is right in his own eyes, for you have not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance that the LORD your God is giving you" (12:8-9). Wilderness worship = temporary, decentralized. "But when you go over the Jordan and live in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to inherit, and when he gives you rest from all your enemies around, so that you live in safety, then to the place that the LORD your God will choose, to make his name dwell there, there you shall bring all that I command you: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution that you present, and all your finest vow offerings that you vow to the LORD" (12:10-11). Centralization = security + unity. "And you shall rejoice before the LORD your God, you and your sons and your daughters, your male servants and your female servants, and the Levite that is within your towns, since he has no portion or inheritance with you" (12:12). Include Levites in celebration. "Take care that you do not offer your burnt offerings at any place that you see, but at the place that the LORD will choose in one of your tribes, there you shall offer your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I am commanding you" (12:13-14). Repeated emphasis: **one place only**. Solomon builds Temple fulfilling this (1 Kgs 8). Josiah's reform enforces it (2 Kgs 23).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Centralized worship only*

#### 12:15-19 — Eat Meat Freely, But Bring Offerings to Sanctuary
**Lore:** "However, you may slaughter and eat meat within any of your towns, as much as you desire, according to the blessing of the LORD your God that he has given you. The unclean and the clean may eat of it, as of the gazelle and as of the deer" (12:15). Secular slaughter permitted anywhere (unlike Lev 17:3-4 where all slaughter = sacrifice). Why change? Canaan larger than wilderness camp; impractical to bring every meal to central sanctuary. "Only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it out on the earth like water" (12:16). Blood prohibition maintained (Gen 9:4; Lev 17:10-14). Life = blood, sacred to God. "You may not eat within your towns the tithe of your grain or of your wine or of your oil, or the firstborn of your herd or of your flock, or any of your vow offerings that you vow, or your freewill offerings or the contribution that you present, but you shall eat them before the LORD your God in the place that the LORD your God will choose" (12:17-18). Sacred offerings = only at sanctuary. Distinction between common meals (anywhere) and holy meals (central sanctuary). "And you shall rejoice before the LORD your God, you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, and the Levite who is within your towns. Take care that you do not neglect the Levite as long as you live in your land" (12:18-19). Care for Levites repeatedly commanded.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Secular vs. sacred meals*

#### 12:20-28 — When Territory Expands, Same Rules
**Lore:** "When the LORD your God enlarges your territory, as he has promised you, and you say, 'I will eat meat,' because you crave meat, you may eat meat whenever you desire. If the place that the LORD your God will choose to put his name there is too far from you, then you may kill any of your herd or your flock, which the LORD has given you, as I have commanded you, and you may eat within your towns whenever you desire" (12:20-21). Anticipates conquest and expansion. Permission to eat meat anywhere reaffirmed. "Just as the gazelle or the deer is eaten, so you may eat of it. The unclean and the clean alike may eat of it. Only be sure that you do not eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you shall not eat the life with the flesh" (12:22-23). Blood = life principle repeated (Lev 17:11). Acts 15:20, 29: Gentile Christians also forbidden blood. "You shall not eat it; you shall pour it out on the earth like water. You shall not eat it, that all may go well with you and with your children after you, when you do what is right in the sight of the LORD" (12:24-25). Obedience = blessing to generations. "But the holy things that are due from you, and your vow offerings, you shall take, and you shall go to the place that the LORD will choose, and you shall offer your burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood, on the altar of the LORD your God. The blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out on the altar of the LORD your God, but the flesh you may eat" (12:26-27). Holy offerings = central sanctuary exclusively. "Be careful to obey all these words that I command you, that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, when you do what is good and right in the sight of the LORD your God" (12:28). Obedience = perpetual blessing.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Blood prohibition maintained*

#### 12:29-32 — Do Not Inquire About Pagan Worship
**Lore:** "When the LORD your God cuts off before you the nations whom you go in to dispossess, and you dispossess them and dwell in their land, take care that you be not ensnared to follow them, after they have been destroyed before you, and that you do not inquire about their gods, saying, 'How did these nations serve their gods?—that I also may do the same'" (12:29-30). Don't even **ask** about pagan practices! Curiosity = danger. "You shall not worship the LORD your God in that way, for every abominable thing that the LORD hates they have done for their gods, for they even burn their sons and their daughters in the fire to their gods" (12:31). Child sacrifice = ultimate abomination (Lev 18:21; 20:2-5). Molech worship condemned. Ahaz and Manasseh did this (2 Kgs 16:3; 21:6). "Everything that I command you, you shall be careful to do. You shall not add to it or take from it" (12:32). God's word sufficient, complete. Don't modify (Prov 30:6; Rev 22:18-19). Safeguard against syncretism.
*Verses: 4 | Theme: No pagan inquiry*

### Chapter 13: Test Prophets and Purge Idolatry

#### 13:1-5 — False Prophet Performing Signs
**Lore:** "If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises among you and gives you a sign or a wonder, and the sign or wonder that he tells you comes to pass, and if he says, 'Let us go after other gods,' which you have not known, 'and let us serve them,' you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or that dreamer of dreams" (13:1-3). **Signs alone don't validate prophets!** Message must align with God's revelation. Satan can do signs (Ex 7:11-12; 2 Thess 2:9; Rev 13:13-14). "For the LORD your God is testing you, to know whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul" (13:3). God permits false prophets as **test of loyalty**. Will you follow spectacular signs or God's truth? "You shall walk after the LORD your God and fear him and keep his commandments and obey his voice, and you shall serve him and hold fast to him" (13:4). True discipleship = clinging to God despite counterfeits. "But that prophet or that dreamer of dreams shall be put to death, because he has taught rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you out of the house of slavery, to make you leave the way in which the LORD your God commanded you to walk. So you shall purge the evil from your midst" (13:5). Death penalty for false prophets. Protecting covenant purity. Jesus warns of false prophets (Matt 7:15; 24:24).
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Test prophets by doctrine*

#### 13:6-11 — Family Member Entices to Idolatry
**Lore:** "If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son or your daughter or the wife you embrace or your friend who is as your own soul entices you secretly, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods,' which neither you nor your fathers have known, some of the gods of the peoples who are around you, whether near you or far off from you, from the one end of the earth to the other, you shall not yield to him or listen to him, nor shall your eye pity him, nor shall you spare him, nor shall you conceal him" (13:6-8). **Closest relationships = greatest spiritual danger!** Family enticement must be resisted. Jesus: "If anyone comes to me and does not hate his own father and mother and wife and children and brothers and sisters, yes, and even his own life, he cannot be my disciple" (Luke 14:26). Loyalty to God transcends family. "But you shall kill him. Your hand shall be first against him to put him to death, and afterward the hand of all the people. You shall stone him to death with stones, because he sought to draw you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery" (13:9-10). Accuser = executioner. Public, participatory justice. "And all Israel shall hear and fear, and never again do any such wickedness as this among you" (13:11). Deterrent effect. Harsh but necessary to preserve covenant community. Spiritual application: sever relationships that lead to sin (Matt 5:29-30; 18:8-9).
*Verses: 6 | Theme: No family exception*

#### 13:12-18 — City Turns to Idolatry
**Lore:** "If you hear in one of your cities, which the LORD your God is giving you to dwell there, that certain worthless fellows have gone out among you and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods,' which you have not known, then you shall inquire and make search and ask diligently. And behold, if it be true and certain that such an abomination has been done among you, you shall surely put the inhabitants of that city to the sword, devoting it to destruction, all who are in it and its cattle, with the edge of the sword" (13:12-15). Corporate apostasy = corporate judgment. Thorough investigation required first (13:14). "You shall gather all its spoil into the midst of its open square and burn the city and all its spoil with fire, as a whole burnt offering to the LORD your God. It shall be a heap forever. It shall not be built again. None of the devoted things shall stick to your hand, that the LORD may turn from the fierceness of his anger and show you mercy and have compassion on you and multiply you, as he swore to your fathers, if you obey the voice of the LORD your God, keeping all his commandments that I am commanding you today, and doing what is right in the sight of the LORD your God" (13:16-18). Entire city = burnt offering (חֵרֶם, cherem). No plunder kept—prevents greed motive. Achan's sin (Josh 7) violated this. Spiritual war against idolatry total, uncompromising. Church discipline parallels (1 Cor 5:6-7, 13; Matt 18:15-17).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Purge apostate city*

### Chapter 14: Holiness in Diet and Tithes

#### 14:1-2 — Sons of God, Holy People
**Lore:** "You are the sons of the LORD your God. You shall not cut yourselves or make any baldness on your foreheads for the dead. For you are a people holy to the LORD your God, and the LORD has chosen you to be a people for his treasured possession, out of all the peoples who are on the face of the earth" (14:1-2). **Identity = sons of God!** Cutting/baldness = pagan mourning rituals (Lev 19:27-28; 21:5; Jer 16:6). Israel must mourn differently—as people with resurrection hope. "Holy" (קָדוֹשׁ, qadosh) = set apart for God. "Treasured possession" (סְגֻלָּה, segullah) = special treasure (Ex 19:5; Mal 3:17). Spiritual privilege brings responsibility. 1 Peter 2:9: "You are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for his own possession." Christians = spiritual Israel, God's treasured people.
*Verses: 2 | Theme: Holy identity*

#### 14:3-21 — Clean and Unclean Animals
**Lore:** "You shall not eat any abominable thing. These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep, the goat, the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountain sheep. Every animal that parts the hoof and has the hoof cloven in two and chews the cud, among the animals, you may eat" (14:3-6). Clean land animals: cloven hoof + chew cud. "Yet of those that chew the cud or have the hoof cloven you shall not eat these: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger...And the pig...is unclean for you" (14:7-8). Pig explicitly forbidden. Repeats Leviticus 11. Water creatures: fins + scales (14:9-10). Birds: list of unclean (14:11-18). Flying insects unclean except locusts (14:19-20). "You shall not eat anything that has died naturally. You may give it to the sojourner who is within your towns, that he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner. For you are a people holy to the LORD your God" (14:21a). Carcasses = unclean. Holiness = higher standard for Israel. "You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk" (14:21b). Third time mentioned (Ex 23:19; 34:26). Compassion + avoiding Canaanite fertility rituals. Rabbinic tradition extends to all meat/dairy separation. Dietary laws = constant reminder of holiness, separation from nations. Mark 7:19: Jesus declares all foods clean. Acts 10:9-16: Peter's vision abolishes distinctions. Romans 14:14, 17: food = not essence of kingdom.
*Verses: 19 | Theme: Dietary holiness*

#### 14:22-29 — Annual and Triennial Tithes
**Lore:** "You shall tithe all the yield of your seed that comes from the field year by year. And before the LORD your God, in the place that he will choose, to make his name dwell there, you shall eat the tithe of your grain, of your wine, and of your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and flock, that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always" (14:22-23). Annual tithe = worship meal at central sanctuary! Family celebration, teaching reverence. "And if the way is too long for you, so that you are not able to carry the tithe, when the LORD your God blesses you, because the place is too far from you, which the LORD your God chooses, to set his name there, then you shall turn it into money and bind up the money in your hand and go to the place that the LORD your God chooses and spend the money for whatever you desire—oxen or sheep or wine or strong drink, whatever your appetite craves. And you shall eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household" (14:24-26). Practical provision: convert tithe to money if distance great, buy food at sanctuary. Joyful feast! "And you shall not neglect the Levite who is within your towns, for he has no portion or inheritance with you" (14:27). Include Levites. "At the end of every three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your produce in the same year and lay it up within your towns. And the Levite, because he has no portion or inheritance with you, and the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow, who are within your towns, shall come and eat and be filled, that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands that you do" (14:28-29). **Third-year tithe** = local poor relief. Social safety net built into worship! Three tithes total? (1) Annual 10% for Levites (Num 18:21-24). (2) Annual festival tithe (Deut 14:22-27). (3) Triennial poor tithe (14:28-29). Or same tithe with different uses? Debated. Principle clear: worship includes generosity to vulnerable. Malachi 3:10: bring tithes to storehouse.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Tithes for worship and poor*
