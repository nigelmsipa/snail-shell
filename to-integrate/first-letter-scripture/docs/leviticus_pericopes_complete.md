# Leviticus Mnemonic Pericopes (LMP) - Complete with Proper Divisions

## Overview

**Central Theme:** "You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy" (19:2)  
**Structure:** Offerings (1-7) → Priesthood (8-10) → Purity (11-15) → Yom Kippur (16) → Holiness Code (17-27)  
**Goal:** 6-12 verse pericopes for optimal memorization

---

## Part I: Offerings (Chapters 1-7)

### Chapter 1: Burnt Offering

#### 1:1-9 — The Cattle Burnt Offering
**Lore:** "The LORD called (וַיִּקְרָא, vayikra) Moses..." (1:1). The burnt offering (עֹלָה, olah, "ascending") = total consecration. Cattle offering (1:3-9): male without blemish, lay hands, slaughter, blood on altar, burn all. "A pleasing aroma to the LORD" (1:9). Christ gave Himself completely (Eph 5:2).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Total surrender*

#### 1:10-17 — Sheep, Goats, and Birds  
**Lore:** Sheep/goats (1:10-13): same process, north side of altar. Birds for the poor (1:14-17): turtledoves or pigeons, priest wrings head, drains blood. God accommodates poverty. All can worship.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Access for all*

---

### Chapter 2: Grain Offering

#### 2:1-10 — Fine Flour and Baked Cakes
**Lore:** Grain offering (מִנְחָה, minchah, "gift"): flour, oil, frankincense (2:1-3). Baked in oven, on griddle, or pan (2:4-10). Human labor offered to God. Pairs with burnt offering = all of life.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Human labor*

#### 2:11-16 — No Leaven, Salt, and Firstfruits
**Lore:** No leaven or honey (2:11) = purity. Salt required (2:13) = covenant permanence. Firstfruits roasted with fire (2:14-16) = acknowledgment of God's provision. Christ is the bread of life (John 6:35).
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Purity and provision*

---

### Chapter 3: Peace Offering

#### 3:1-11 — Fellowship from Herd and Flock
**Lore:** Peace offering (זֶבַח שְׁלָמִים, zevach shelamim): cattle or sheep (3:1-11), male or female. Fat and organs burned, rest eaten by worshiper and priests. Shared meal with God = fellowship after atonement.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Fellowship*

#### 3:12-17 — Goat Offering and Fat Prohibition
**Lore:** Goat peace offering (3:12-16). "All fat is the LORD's" (3:16-17). Perpetual statute. No eating fat or blood. Fat = best portion belongs to God. Foreshadows Lord's Supper communion.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Sacred portions*

---

### Chapter 4: Sin Offering

#### 4:1-12 — Sin of the Anointed Priest
**Lore:** Sin offering (חַטָּאת, chattat) for unintentional sin (בִּשְׁגָגָה, bishgagah). High priest's sin (4:3-12): bull, blood sprinkled 7x before veil, applied to altar horns. Carcass burned outside camp (prefigures Christ outside city, Heb 13:11-13).
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Priestly atonement*

#### 4:13-21 — Sin of the Whole Congregation
**Lore:** Community sin (4:13-21): elders lay hands on bull, same blood ritual. Corporate guilt requires corporate atonement. "And the priest shall make atonement for them, and they shall be forgiven" (4:20).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Corporate forgiveness*

#### 4:22-35 — Sin of a Leader or Common Person
**Lore:** Leader's sin (4:22-26): male goat, blood on bronze altar horns. Common person (4:27-35): female goat or lamb. Priest eats remainder. Variable offerings based on status, but same forgiveness. Christ our sin offering (2 Cor 5:21).
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Universal atonement*

---

### Chapter 5: Specific Sins and Guilt Offering

#### 5:1-13 — Graduated Sin Offerings
**Lore:** Specific sins requiring offerings: withholding testimony, touching unclean things, rash oaths (5:1-4). Confession required (5:5). Graduated offerings: lamb/goat (5:6), two birds for poor (5:7-10), flour for very poor (5:11-13). God's grace accommodates all economic levels.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Accessible atonement*

#### 5:14-19 — Guilt Offering for Sacred Violations
**Lore:** Guilt offering (אָשָׁם, asham) for unintentional sacrilege (5:14-19): ram, restitution + 20%. Misusing holy things requires both sacrifice and payment. Debt metaphor for sin.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Sacred restitution*

#### 6:1-7 — Guilt Offering for Interpersonal Wrongs
**Lore:** Guilt offering for deception, theft, false oaths (6:1-7): restore item + 20%, then ram offering. Sin affects both vertical (God) and horizontal (neighbor) relationships. Christ is our guilt offering (Isa 53:10).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Relational restoration*

---

### Chapter 6: Priestly Instructions

#### 6:8-13 — The Perpetual Fire
**Lore:** Burnt offering regulations (6:8-13): fire must never go out (6:13). Priest removes ashes daily in linen garments. Perpetual fire = God's constant presence and readiness to receive worship. Christ's continual intercession.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Unceasing worship*

#### 6:14-23 — Grain Offering for Priests
**Lore:** Grain offering regulations (6:14-18): priests eat remainder in holy place. Aaron's ordination offering (6:19-23): fine flour, half morning/half evening, wholly burned. Priestly portion sustains those who serve.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Priestly provision*

#### 6:24-30 — Sin Offering Regulations
**Lore:** Sin offering is most holy (6:24-30). Priest who offers it eats it in holy place (6:26). Blood on garment must be washed. Earthen vessel broken, bronze scoured (6:28). But sin offerings with blood brought into tent are burned, not eaten (6:30). Extreme care with holy things.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Holiness demands care*

---

### Chapter 7: Final Offering Instructions

#### 7:1-10 — Guilt Offering and Priestly Shares
**Lore:** Guilt offering regulations (7:1-10): most holy, priest eats it. Priest who offers burnt offering gets the hide (7:8). Grain offerings belong to all sons of Aaron equally (7:9-10). God provides for His servants through offerings.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Priestly sustenance*

#### 7:11-21 — Peace Offering Types and Timeline
**Lore:** Three peace offerings: thanksgiving (eaten same day, 7:12-15), vow/freewill (two days, 7:16-17). Third-day eating makes it abomination (פִּגּוּל, piggul) and bearer guilty (7:18). Unclean person eating = cut off (7:19-21). Freshness and purity required.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Timely worship*

#### 7:22-27 — Fat and Blood Prohibition
**Lore:** Absolute prohibitions: no eating fat (7:22-25) or blood (7:26-27). Both result in being cut off. Fat = best portion for God alone. Blood = life reserved for atonement (17:11). Christ redefines "drinking blood" as covenant participation (John 6:53-56).
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Sacred restrictions*

#### 7:28-38 — Peace Offering Portions and Summary
**Lore:** Peace offering portions (7:28-34): breast waved, given to priests; right thigh is priest's. Perpetual due from Israel. Summary (7:35-38): laws of burnt, grain, sin, guilt, ordination, and peace offerings commanded at Sinai. Sacrificial system complete.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Covenant portions*

## Part II: Priesthood Inaugurated (Chapters 8-10)

### Chapter 8: Ordination of Aaron

#### 8:1-13 — Washing, Vesting, Anointing
**Lore:** Moses consecrates Aaron and sons (8:1-5). Public ordination before congregation. Washing with water (8:6), vesting in holy garments (8:7-9), anointing tabernacle and Aaron (8:10-12). "Anointed" (מָשִׁיחַ, mashiach) = messiah, prefiguring Christ the ultimate High Priest.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Consecration ritual*

#### 8:14-30 — Three Offerings for Ordination
**Lore:** Sin offering: bull (8:14-17). Burnt offering: ram (8:18-21). Ordination offering: ram (8:22-29). Blood on right ear, thumb, toe = sanctifying hearing, action, walk (8:23-24). Blood and oil sprinkled on garments (8:30). Complete sanctification.
*Verses: 17 | Theme: Triple purification*

#### 8:31-36 — Seven Days at the Tent
**Lore:** Aaron and sons remain at tent entrance 7 days, eating ordination meat (8:31-35). Must not leave or they die. Moses: "This is what the LORD has commanded" (8:36). Obedience crucial. Seven days = complete consecration period.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Complete obedience*

---

### Chapter 9: First Offerings

#### 9:1-14 — Aaron's First Service
**Lore:** Eighth day = new beginning (9:1). Aaron offers sin offering (calf) and burnt offering (ram) for himself (9:8-14), then for the people (9:15-21). "For today the LORD will appear to you" (9:4). Anticipation builds.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Priestly service begins*

#### 9:15-24 — God's Glory Appears
**Lore:** Aaron offers people's sin, burnt, grain, and peace offerings (9:15-21). Aaron blesses people (9:22). Moses and Aaron bless together. "And the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people. And fire came out from before the LORD and consumed the burnt offering and the pieces of fat on the altar" (9:23-24). Divine fire! People worship. God validates the priesthood.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Divine acceptance*

---

### Chapter 10: Strange Fire

#### 10:1-7 — Nadab and Abihu Die
**Lore:** "Now Nadab and Abihu...offered unauthorized fire (אֵשׁ זָרָה, esh zarah, "strange fire") before the LORD" (10:1). Immediately after God's glory appears, Aaron's sons presume to use common fire. "And fire came out from before the LORD and consumed them, and they died" (10:2). Instant judgment. "Among those who are near me I will be sanctified" (10:3). Aaron silent. No public mourning allowed for remaining priests (10:6-7). Central narrative of Leviticus: God's absolute holiness demands reverence.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Holy fire only*

#### 10:8-20 — Sober Judgment Required
**Lore:** God speaks directly to Aaron (rare): "Drink no wine or strong drink...when you go into the tent of meeting, lest you die" (10:9-10). Distinguish holy from common, clean from unclean (10:10). Moses finds they burned sin offering instead of eating it (10:16-19). Aaron's defense: given today's tragedy, eating would be inappropriate. Moses accepts. Context matters, but holiness is non-negotiable.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Discernment required*
