# Numbers 18-20: Priests, Purification, and Passing

## Part VII: Priestly Provisions and Red Heifer (Chapters 18-19)

### Chapter 18: Priests and Levites

#### 18:1-7 — Priestly Responsibility and Guard Duty
**Lore:** "So the LORD said to Aaron, 'You and your sons and your father's house with you shall bear iniquity connected with the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you shall bear iniquity connected with your priesthood'" (18:1). After Korah's revolt, God clarifies: priests bear responsibility for sanctuary violations. Heavy burden! Levites assist but priests alone approach holy things (18:2-3). "And you shall guard the sanctuary and the altar, that there may never again be wrath on the people of Israel" (18:5). Priests = buffer between holy God and sinful people. "And I give your priesthood as a gift, and any outsider who comes near shall be put to death" (18:7). Priesthood = divine gift, not human right. Christ: ultimate High Priest bearing our iniquity (Isa 53:11; Heb 9:28).
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Priestly responsibility*

#### 18:8-20 — Priestly Portions from Offerings
**Lore:** "Then the LORD spoke to Aaron, 'Behold, I have given you charge of the contributions made to me, all the consecrated things of the people of Israel. I have given them to you as a portion and to your sons as a perpetual due'" (18:8). Priests receive: most holy offerings (grain, sin, guilt—eaten in holy place, 18:9-10), wave offerings (breast and thigh of peace offerings, 18:11), firstfruits (oil, wine, grain, 18:12-13), devoted things (18:14), firstborn redemption money (18:15-16). "All the best of the oil and all the best of the wine and of the grain, the firstfruits of what they give to the LORD, I give to you" (18:12). God provides for those who serve Him. "Everything devoted in Israel shall be yours" (18:14). But: "You shall have no inheritance in their land, neither shall you have any portion among them. I am your portion and your inheritance among the people of Israel" (18:20). Priests get no tribal land—God Himself is their inheritance! Psalm 16:5: "The LORD is my chosen portion." Philippians 3:8: Paul counts everything loss "because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ."
*Verses: 13 | Theme: God as inheritance*

#### 18:21-32 — Levites Receive Tithes, Give Tithe of Tithe
**Lore:** "To the Levites I have given every tithe in Israel for an inheritance, in return for their service that they do, their service in the tent of meeting" (18:21). Levites = no land inheritance, receive tithes instead (18:23-24). But Levites must tithe their tithe! "When you take from the people of Israel the tithe that I have given you from them for your inheritance, then you shall present a contribution from it to the LORD, a tithe of the tithe. And your contribution shall be counted to you as though it were the grain of the threshing floor, and as the fullness of the winepress" (18:26-27). Give tithe to priests (Aaron's line, 18:28). "Out of all the gifts to you, you shall present every contribution due to the LORD; from each its best part is to be dedicated" (18:29). Even those who receive must give. Malachi 3:10: "Bring the full tithe into the storehouse." Jesus: tithe without neglecting justice, mercy, faithfulness (Matt 23:23). Systematic giving supports God's work.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Tithe of tithe*

### Chapter 19: The Red Heifer

#### 19:1-10 — Red Heifer Ritual for Purification
**Lore:** "Now the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying, 'This is the statute of the law that the LORD has commanded: Tell the people of Israel to bring you a red heifer without defect, in which there is no blemish, and on which a yoke has never come'" (19:1-2). Unique sacrifice: red (rare color), female (unusual—usually male), unblemished, never yoked (never worked). Give to Eleazar the priest; taken outside camp and slaughtered (19:3). Eleazar sprinkles blood toward tent 7× (19:4). Entire heifer burned: hide, flesh, blood, dung (19:5). "And the priest shall take cedarwood and hyssop and scarlet yarn, and throw them into the fire burning the heifer" (19:6). Same materials as leper cleansing (Lev 14:4). "Then the priest shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and afterward he may come into the camp. But the priest shall be unclean until evening" (19:7). Paradox: ritual purifies others but defiles participants! Clean man gathers ashes, stores outside camp (19:9). "It is for the water for impurity; it is a sin offering" (19:9). Ashes + living water = purification for corpse-contact (19:10). Hebrews 9:13-14: "If the blood of goats and bulls, and the sprinkling of defiled persons with the ashes of a heifer, sanctify for the purification of the flesh, how much more will the blood of Christ...purify our conscience." Red heifer prefigures Christ: rare, costly, outside camp (Heb 13:12), paradoxically pure yet bearing impurity.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Purification paradox*

#### 19:11-22 — Laws of Corpse Impurity
**Lore:** "Whoever touches the dead body of any person shall be unclean seven days" (19:11). Must purify with water of purification on 3rd and 7th days (19:12). Failure = cut off; defiled God's tabernacle (19:13). Anyone in tent with corpse becomes unclean; open vessels unclean (19:14-15). Touching corpse, grave, bone = 7 days unclean (19:16). **Purification process:** Take red heifer ashes, add fresh water in vessel (19:17). Clean person dips hyssop, sprinkles tent, vessels, people on 3rd and 7th days (19:18-19). On 7th day: washes clothes, bathes; clean at evening (19:19). "But the man who is unclean and does not cleanse himself, that person shall be cut off from the midst of the assembly, since he has defiled the sanctuary of the LORD. Because the water for impurity has not been thrown on him, he is unclean" (19:20). Person sprinkling and person sprinkled both must wash (19:21). "And whatever the unclean person touches shall be unclean, and anyone who touches it shall be unclean until evening" (19:22). Death = ultimate defilement. Requires elaborate cleansing. Points to Christ's victory over death (1 Cor 15:54-57).
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Death defiles*

## Part VIII: Final Tragedies (Chapter 20)

### Chapter 20: Water from Rock, Edom Refuses, Aaron Dies

#### 20:1-13 — Water from the Rock at Meribah
**Lore:** "And the people of Israel, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed in Kadesh. And Miriam died there and was buried there" (20:1). Miriam's death briefly noted. No water again (20:2). "And the people quarreled with Moses and said, 'Would that we had perished when our brothers perished before the LORD! Why have you brought the assembly of the LORD into this wilderness, that we should die here, both we and our cattle?'" (20:3-4). Same complaint, different generation (cf. Ex 17:1-7). "And why have you made us come up out of Egypt to bring us to this evil place? It is no place for grain or figs or vines or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink" (20:5). Moses and Aaron fall on faces; God's glory appears (20:6). "Take the staff, and assemble the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and tell the rock before their eyes to yield its water" (20:7-8). **Speak to rock.** But Moses: "'Hear now, you rebels: shall we bring water for you out of this rock?' And Moses lifted up his hand and struck the rock with his staff twice, and water came out abundantly" (20:10-11). Moses disobeys: (1) Calls them rebels instead of representing God's mercy. (2) Says "shall WE bring" (taking credit). (3) Strikes twice instead of speaking. Water flows anyway (grace!), but God says: "Because you did not believe in me, to uphold me as holy in the eyes of the people of Israel, therefore you shall not bring this assembly into the land that I have given them" (20:12). Moses barred from Canaan! Even great leaders face consequences for dishonoring God. "These are the waters of Meribah (מְרִיבָה, 'quarreling'), where the people of Israel quarreled with the LORD, and through them he showed himself holy" (20:13). Paul references this: 1 Cor 10:4, "the Rock was Christ."
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Moses's failure*

#### 20:14-21 — Edom Refuses Passage
**Lore:** From Kadesh, Moses sends messengers to Edom's king: "Thus says your brother Israel: You know all the hardship that we have met...And when we cried to the LORD, he heard our voice and sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. And here we are in Kadesh, a city on the edge of your territory. Please let us pass through your land" (20:14-17). Requests highway passage, promises no trespassing, will pay for water (20:17-19). "But Edom said to him, 'You shall not pass through, lest I come out with the sword against you'" (20:18). Refuses. "And Edom came out against them with a large army and with a strong force. Thus Edom refused to give Israel passage through his territory, so Israel turned away from him" (20:20-21). Edom = descendants of Esau (Jacob's brother). Ancient rivalry resurfaces. Israel forced to detour around. Obadiah pronounces judgment on Edom for this. Family feuds span generations.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Brother refuses help*

#### 20:22-29 — Aaron's Death on Mount Hor
**Lore:** "And they journeyed from Kadesh, and the people of Israel, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor" (20:22). "And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron at Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, 'Let Aaron be gathered to his people, for he shall not enter the land that I have given to the people of Israel, because you rebelled against my command at the waters of Meribah'" (20:23-24). Aaron also punished for Meribah. "Take Aaron and Eleazar his son and bring them up to Mount Hor. And strip Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son. And Aaron shall be gathered to his people and shall die there'" (20:25-26). Public transfer of priesthood. Moses obeys; they ascend mountain in sight of congregation (20:27). "And Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son. And Aaron died there on the top of the mountain" (20:28). Aaron = 123 years old (Num 33:39). Moses and Eleazar descend; congregation mourns 30 days (20:29). Aaron's priestly line continues through Eleazar. Hebrews 7:23-24: "The former priests were many in number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office, but he [Christ] holds his priesthood permanently, because he continues forever." Christ = permanent High Priest.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Priestly succession*
