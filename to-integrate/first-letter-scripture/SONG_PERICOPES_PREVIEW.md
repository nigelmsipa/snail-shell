# Song of Solomon - Pericope Preview

## Selected Pericopes (Sample)

### Chapter 1: Opening Desire
1. **Let Him Kiss Me** (v1-4)
   - Opening declaration of desire
   - "Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth"

2. **Dark But Lovely** (v5-8)
   - "I am dark but lovely, O daughters of Jerusalem"
   - "Do not gaze at me because I am dark"

3. **My Beloved Is to Me** (v12-14)
   - Exchange of love imagery
   - Myrrh and henna blossoms

### Chapter 2: Spring's Arrival
4. **His Banner Over Me Is Love** (v3-7)
   - Under the shade of the apple tree
   - "He brought me to the banqueting house"
   - First Adjuration: "Do not stir up or awaken love"

5. **The Time of Singing** (v8-13)
   - "Arise, my love, my beautiful one, and come away"
   - "The winter is past, the rain is over and gone"
   - Springtime imagery

6. **Catch the Little Foxes** (v14-15)
   - "The foxes that spoil the vineyards"

### Chapter 3: Night and Wedding
7. **I Sought Him I Love** (v1-5)
   - Famous night search passage
   - "I sought him whom my soul loves"
   - "I found him whom my soul loves"
   - Second Adjuration

8. **Who Is This Coming Up?** (v6-11)
   - Royal wedding procession
   - Solomon's palanquin
   - "Go out, O daughters of Zion"

### Chapter 4: The Bride's Beauty
9. **You Are Altogether Beautiful** (v1-7)
   - Detailed praise of the bride
   - "You are altogether beautiful, my love"
   - "There is no flaw in you"

10. **A Garden Enclosed** (v12-15)
    - "A garden locked is my sister, my bride"
    - Imagery of sealed fountain and springs

### Chapter 8: Love's Culmination
11. **Love Is Strong as Death** (v5-7) ⭐ CLIMAX
    - "Set me as a seal upon your heart"
    - **"Love is strong as death, jealousy is fierce as the grave"**
    - "Many waters cannot quench love"
    - "Rivers cannot wash it away"

12. **Make Haste, My Beloved** (v13-14)
    - Final exchange
    - Closing call

## Thematic Arc

The 28 pericopes follow this progression:

1. **Chapters 1-2**: Initial attraction and desire (10 pericopes)
   - Mutual admiration
   - Longing and seeking
   - First declarations of love

2. **Chapters 3-4**: Wedding and union (6 pericopes)
   - Night search and finding
   - Royal wedding procession
   - Celebration of beauty and love

3. **Chapters 5-6**: Separation and reunion (6 pericopes)
   - Second search
   - Description of beloved
   - Restoration

4. **Chapters 7-8**: Mature love and commitment (6 pericopes)
   - Deep intimacy
   - **The Seal passage** - theological climax
   - Final affirmation
