## Psalm 103: Bless the LORD, O My Soul

#### 103:1-14 — Who Forgives All Your Iniquity
**Lore:** "Of David" (superscription). **Praise psalm** for God's benefits. "Bless the LORD, O my soul, and all that is within me, bless his holy name\! Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits, who forgives all your iniquity, who heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from the pit, who crowns you with steadfast love and mercy, who satisfies you with good so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's" (103:1-5). **FIVE BENEFITS**: forgives, heals, redeems, crowns with chesed/mercy, satisfies/renews. "The LORD works righteousness and justice for all who are oppressed. He made known his ways to Moses, his acts to the people of Israel. The LORD is merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love" (103:6-8, v.8 quotes Exod 34:6). "As a father shows compassion to his children, so the LORD shows compassion to those who fear him. For he knows our frame; he remembers that we are dust" (103:13-14). **Father's compassion**. God remembers we're dust (Gen 2:7, 3:19).
*Verses: 14 | Theme: God's compassionate benefits*

#### 103:15-22 — His Steadfast Love Is from Everlasting
**Lore:** "As for man, his days are like grass; he flourishes like a flower of the field; for the wind passes over it, and it is gone, and its place knows it no more. But the steadfast love of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting on those who fear him" (103:15-17). Human transience vs. God's eternal chesed. "Bless the LORD, O you his angels, you mighty ones who do his word, obeying the voice of his word\! Bless the LORD, all his hosts, his ministers, who do his will\! Bless the LORD, all his works, in all places of his dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul\!" (103:20-22). **Universal call**—angels, hosts, all works, soul. Begins and ends: "Bless LORD, O my soul."
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Eternal steadfast love*

## Psalm 104: O LORD, How Manifold Are Your Works

#### 104:1-23 — Creation's Order
**Lore:** "Bless the LORD, O my soul\! O LORD my God, you are very great\! You are clothed with splendor and majesty, covering yourself with light as with a garment" (104:1-2). **Creation hymn**—follows Gen 1. "You set the earth on its foundations, so that it should never be moved" (104:5). Earth's stability. "You make springs gush forth in the valleys" (104:10). Water cycle. "You cause the grass to grow for the livestock and plants for man to cultivate" (104:14). Food provision. "You have made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows its time for setting" (104:19). Celestial order. "O LORD, how manifold are your works\! In wisdom have you made them all; the earth is full of your creatures" (104:24). **Manifold works** in wisdom.
*Verses: 23 | Theme: Creation's order*

#### 104:24-35 — Renew the Face of the Ground
**Lore:** "When you send forth your Spirit, they are created, and you renew the face of the ground" (104:30). **Spirit's creative work** (cf. Gen 1:2). "May the glory of the LORD endure forever; may the LORD rejoice in his works" (104:31). Doxology. "I will sing to the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praise to my God while I have being. Let sinners be consumed from the earth, and let the wicked be no more\! Bless the LORD, O my soul\! Praise the LORD\!" (104:33-35). Personal vow. Sinners removed. **Hallelujah**—first in Psalter\!
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Spirit renews creation*

## Psalm 105: Remember His Covenant Forever

#### 105:1-15 — Give Thanks to the LORD
**Lore:** "Oh give thanks to the LORD; call upon his name; make known his deeds among the peoples\! Sing to him, sing praises to him; tell of all his wondrous works\!" (105:1-2). Thanksgiving call. "Remember the wondrous works that he has done, his miracles, and the judgments he uttered, O offspring of Abraham, his servant, children of Jacob, his chosen ones\!" (105:5-6). **Remember**—salvation history. "He remembers his covenant forever, the word that he commanded, for a thousand generations, the covenant that he made with Abraham, his sworn promise to Isaac" (105:8-9). **Abrahamic covenant**. "To you I will give the land of Canaan as your portion for an inheritance" (105:11).
*Verses: 15 | Theme: Remember God's covenant*

#### 105:16-45 — From Egypt to Canaan
**Lore:** "When he summoned a famine on the land and broke all supply of bread, he had sent a man ahead of them, Joseph, who was sold as a slave" (105:16-17). **Joseph's story**. "Then Israel came to Egypt; Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. And the LORD made his people very fruitful" (105:23-24). Egypt sojourn. "He sent Moses, his servant, and Aaron, whom he had chosen. They performed his signs among them and miracles in the land of Ham" (105:26-27). **Plagues**. "He brought his people out with joy, his chosen ones with singing. And he gave them the lands of the nations, and they took possession of the fruit of the peoples' toil, that they might keep his statutes and observe his laws. Praise the LORD\!" (105:43-45). **Exodus and conquest**. Purpose: obedience. **Hallelujah** ending.
*Verses: 30 | Theme: Salvation history from Abraham to Canaan*

## Psalm 106: Save Us, O LORD Our God

#### 106:1-23 — We Have Sinned with Our Fathers
**Lore:** "Praise the LORD\! Oh give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever\!" (106:1). **Hallelujah** opening. "Both we and our fathers have sinned; we have committed iniquity; we have done wickedness. Our fathers, when they were in Egypt, did not consider your wondrous works; they did not remember the abundance of your steadfast love, but rebelled by the sea, at the Red Sea" (106:6-7). **Confession**—continues Ps 105's history negatively. "Yet he saved them for his name's sake, that he might make known his mighty power" (106:8). Saved despite rebellion. "They made a calf in Horeb and worshiped a metal image" (106:19). **Golden calf** (Exod 32). "Therefore he said he would destroy them—had not Moses, his chosen one, stood in the breach before him, to turn away his wrath from destroying them" (106:23). **Moses' intercession** (Exod 32:11-14).
*Verses: 23 | Theme: Confession of ancestral sin*

#### 106:24-48 — Save Us and Gather Us
**Lore:** "Then they despised the pleasant land, having no faith in his promise. They murmured in their tents, and did not obey the voice of the LORD" (106:24-25). **Wilderness rebellion**. "Many times he delivered them, but they were rebellious in their purposes and were brought low through their iniquity" (106:43). Cycle repeated. "Nevertheless, he looked upon their distress, when he heard their cry. For their sake he remembered his covenant, and relented according to the abundance of his steadfast love" (106:44-45). Despite rebellion, God remembers covenant. "Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from among the nations, that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise" (106:47). **Prayer for regathering** (exile context). "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting\! And let all the people say, 'Amen\!' Praise the LORD\!" (106:48). **DOXOLOGY ENDING BOOK 4**. Double **Hallelujah** (opening/closing). **Amen** from people.
*Verses: 25 | Theme: Despite sin, God's covenant endures*
