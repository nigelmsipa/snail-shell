The Translation
King James Version
Authorized, 1611

In 1604 King James I commissioned a new English Bible. Forty-seven scholars, working in six companies at Oxford, Cambridge, and Westminster, labored seven years — building on Tyndale's pioneering work and the Bishops' Bible. What they made was not merely a text but a cadence: English at its most stately and rhythmic. Four centuries on, it remains the most printed and most enduring book in the language.
