# Deuteronomy 9-11: Remember Your Rebellion, God's Grace

## Part IV: Not Because of Your Righteousness (Chapters 9-11)

### Chapter 9: Israel's Stubbornness

#### 9:1-6 — Not Because of Your Righteousness
**Lore:** "Hear, O Israel: you are to cross over the Jordan today, to go in to dispossess nations greater and mightier than you, cities great and fortified up to heaven, a people great and tall, the sons of the Anakim, whom you know, and of whom you have heard it said, 'Who can stand before the sons of Anak?'" (9:1-2). Giants again! "Know therefore today that he who goes over before you as a consuming fire is the LORD your God. He will destroy them and subdue them before you. So you shall drive them out and make them perish quickly, as the LORD has promised you" (9:3). God = warrior. "Do not say in your heart, after the LORD your God has thrust them out before you, 'It is because of my righteousness that the LORD has brought me in to possess this land,' whereas it is because of the wickedness of these nations that the LORD is driving them out before you. Not because of your righteousness or the uprightness of your heart are you going in to possess their land, but because of the wickedness of these nations the LORD your God is driving them out from before you, and that he may confirm the word that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob" (9:4-5). Critical theology: Israel undeserving! Conquest = judgment on Canaanites + fulfillment of promise to patriarchs. "Know, therefore, that the LORD your God is not giving you this good land to possess because of your righteousness, for you are a stubborn people" (9:6). Humbling truth. Grace alone saves.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Not your righteousness*

#### 9:7-24 — Remember: You Rebelled from the Start
**Lore:** "Remember and do not forget how you provoked the LORD your God to wrath in the wilderness. From the day you came out of the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have been rebellious against the LORD" (9:7). Litany of rebellion begins. Golden calf (9:9-21). Moses on mountain 40 days/nights (9:9). God gave two stone tablets "written with the finger of God" (9:10). "The LORD said to me, 'Arise, go down quickly...your people...have acted corruptly...I will destroy them'" (9:12-14). Moses intercedes (9:18-20, 25-29). "I took hold of the two tablets and threw them out of my two hands and broke them before your eyes" (9:17). Burned golden calf, ground to powder (9:21). "At Taberah also, and at Massah and at Kibroth-hattaavah you provoked the LORD to wrath" (9:22). "You have been rebellious against the LORD from the day that I knew you" (9:24). Constant rebellion catalogued.
*Verses: 18 | Theme: Constant rebellion*

#### 9:25-29 — Moses's Intercession
**Lore:** "So I lay prostrate before the LORD for these forty days and forty nights, because the LORD had said he would destroy you. And I prayed to the LORD, 'O Lord GOD, do not destroy your people...Remember your servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Do not regard the stubbornness of this people...lest the land from which you brought us say, "Because the LORD was not able to bring them into the land that he promised them"'" (9:25-29). Powerful intercession! Appeals to God's reputation and promise to patriarchs. Moses = mediator, foreshadowing Christ (1 Tim 2:5).
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Intercession saves*

### Chapter 10: New Tablets and Circumcised Hearts

#### 10:1-11 — New Tablets, Ark, Levites
**Lore:** "At that time the LORD said to me, 'Cut for yourself two tablets of stone like the first, and come up to me on the mountain and make an ark of wood. And I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets that you broke'" (10:1-2). Second chance! Covenant renewed. Moses made ark, God wrote Ten Commandments again (10:3-5). Levites chosen to carry ark, minister before LORD (10:8-9). "I stayed on the mountain, as at the first time, forty days and forty nights, and the LORD listened to me that time also. The LORD was unwilling to destroy you" (10:10). Grace triumphs.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Covenant renewed*

#### 10:12-22 — What Does the LORD Require?
**Lore:** "And now, Israel, what does the LORD your God require of you, but to fear the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, to love him, to serve the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, and to keep the commandments and statutes of the LORD, which I am commanding you today for your good?" (10:12-13). Classic summary! Micah 6:8 echoes this. "Behold, to the LORD your God belong heaven and the heaven of heavens, the earth with all that is in it. Yet the LORD set his heart in love on your fathers and chose their offspring after them" (10:14-15). God's sovereignty + elective love. "Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be no longer stubborn" (10:16). **Heart circumcision!** Internal transformation required. Romans 2:29: "real circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit." "For the LORD your God is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great, the mighty, and the awesome God, who is not partial and takes no bribe. He executes justice for the fatherless and the widow, and loves the sojourner" (10:17-18). God's character: just, loving to vulnerable. "You shall fear the LORD your God. You shall serve him and hold fast to him" (10:20). "Your fathers went down to Egypt seventy persons, and now the LORD your God has made you as numerous as the stars of heaven" (10:22). From 70 to multitude!
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Love and justice*

### Chapter 11: Choose Blessing or Curse

#### 11:1-12 — Remember God's Mighty Acts
**Lore:** "You shall therefore love the LORD your God and keep his charge, his statutes, his rules, and his commandments always" (11:1). Love = obedience. "And consider today...the discipline of the LORD your God, his greatness, his mighty hand and his outstretched arm, his signs and his deeds that he did in Egypt...and what he did to the army of Egypt...how he made the water of the Red Sea flow over them...and what he did to Dathan and Abiram...how the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up" (11:2-7). Personal witnesses! Egypt, Red Sea, Korah's rebellion. "For the land that you are entering to take possession of it is not like the land of Egypt, from which you have come, where you sowed your seed and irrigated it, like a garden of vegetables. But the land that you are going over to possess is a land of hills and valleys, which drinks water by the rain from heaven, a land that the LORD your God cares for" (11:10-12). Egypt = human irrigation. Canaan = rain-dependent, requiring faith in God's provision.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Land requires faith*

#### 11:13-25 — Obedience Brings Rain and Victory
**Lore:** "And if you will indeed obey my commandments that I command you today, to love the LORD your God, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul, he will give the rain for your land in its season, the early rain and the later rain, that you may gather in your grain and your wine and your oil. And he will give grass in your fields for your livestock, and you shall eat and be full. Take care lest your heart be deceived, and you turn aside and serve other gods and worship them; then the anger of the LORD will be kindled against you, and he will shut up the heavens, so that there will be no rain, and the land will yield no fruit, and you will perish quickly off the good land that the LORD is giving you" (11:13-17). Conditional blessings: obedience = rain; disobedience = drought. Elijah's drought (1 Kgs 17-18) fulfills this. "You shall therefore lay up these words of mine in your heart and in your soul, and you shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes. You shall teach them to your children, talking of them when you are sitting in your house, and when you are walking by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise. You shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates" (11:18-20). Repeats 6:6-9. Total life integration. "For if you will be careful to do all this commandment that I command you to do, loving the LORD your God, walking in all his ways, and holding fast to him, then the LORD will drive out all these nations before you...Every place on which the sole of your foot treads shall be yours...No one shall be able to stand against you. The LORD your God will lay the fear of you and the dread of you on all the land that you shall tread, as he promised you" (11:22-25). Victory promised for obedience.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Obedience = blessing*

#### 11:26-32 — Blessing and Curse: Choose!
**Lore:** "See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse: the blessing, if you obey the commandments of the LORD your God, which I command you today, and the curse, if you do not obey the commandments of the LORD your God, but turn aside from the way that I am commanding you today, to go after other gods that you have not known" (11:26-28). Binary choice: life or death. "And when the LORD your God brings you into the land that you are entering to take possession of it, you shall set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal" (11:29). Ceremony instructions (fulfilled Josh 8:30-35). "Are they not beyond the Jordan, west of the road, toward the going down of the sun, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oak of Moreh?" (11:30). Specific geography. "For you are to cross over the Jordan to go in to take possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving you. And when you possess it and live in it, you shall be careful to do all the statutes and the rules that I am setting before you today" (11:31-32). Final exhortation. Prepares for law code (ch 12-26). Joshua 24:15: "Choose this day whom you will serve."
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Choose blessing*
