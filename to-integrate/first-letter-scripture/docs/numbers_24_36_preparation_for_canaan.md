# Numbers 24-36: Final Oracles and Preparation for Conquest

## Part XI: Balaam's Final Oracles and Baal-Peor (Chapters 24-25)

### Chapter 24: Third and Fourth Oracles

#### 24:1-9 — Third Oracle: Star from Jacob
**Lore:** "When Balaam saw that it pleased the LORD to bless Israel, he did not go, as at other times, to look for omens, but set his face toward the wilderness" (24:1). No more divination—accepts God's will. "And Balaam lifted up his eyes and saw Israel camping tribe by tribe. And the Spirit of God came upon him" (24:2). **Third oracle** (24:3-9): "The oracle of Balaam the son of Beor, the oracle of the man whose eye is opened, the oracle of him who hears the words of God, who sees the vision of the Almighty, falling down with his eyes uncovered: How lovely are your tents, O Jacob, your encampments, O Israel! Like valleys that stretch afar, like gardens beside a river, like aloes that the LORD has planted, like cedar trees beside the waters. Water shall flow from his buckets, and his seed shall be in many waters; his king shall be higher than Agag, and his kingdom shall be exalted. God brings him out of Egypt and is for him like the horns of the wild ox; he shall eat up the nations, his adversaries, and shall break their bones in pieces and pierce them through with his arrows. He crouched, he lay down like a lion and like a lioness; who will rouse him up? Blessed are those who bless you, and cursed are those who curse you" (24:3-9). Magnificent! Echoes Genesis 12:3; 49:9. Balak furious, strikes hands together: "I called you to curse my enemies, and behold, you have blessed them these three times" (24:10). Orders Balaam home without reward (24:11). Balaam: "Did I not tell your messengers...even if Balak should give me his house full of silver and gold, I would not be able to go beyond the word of the LORD, to do either good or bad of my own will. What the LORD speaks, that will I speak?" (24:12-13). Finally resigned to God's sovereignty.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Irresistible blessing*

#### 24:14-25 — Fourth Oracle: The Star and Scepter Prophecy
**Lore:** Before leaving, Balaam delivers final oracle: "Come, I will let you know what this people will do to your people in the latter days" (24:14). **Fourth oracle** (24:15-19): "The oracle of Balaam the son of Beor, the oracle of the man whose eye is opened, the oracle of him who hears the words of God, and knows the knowledge of the Most High, who sees the vision of the Almighty, falling down with his eyes uncovered: I see him, but not now; I behold him, but not near: a star shall come out of Jacob, and a scepter shall rise out of Israel; it shall crush the forehead of Moab and break down all the sons of Sheth. Edom shall be dispossessed; Seir also, his enemies, shall be dispossessed. Israel is doing valiantly. And one from Jacob shall exercise dominion and destroy the survivors of cities!" (24:15-19). **Messianic prophecy!** "Star from Jacob" = Christ. Matthew 2:2: Wise men seek "his star." Revelation 22:16: "I am...the bright morning star." Scepter = royal authority. Genesis 49:10: "The scepter shall not depart from Judah." This prophecy points to ultimate King, Jesus. Additional brief oracles: Amalek (24:20), Kenites (24:21-22), Asshur and Eber (24:23-24). Balaam departs (24:25). God uses even pagan prophet to speak messianic hope! Numbers, book of rebellion, ends with promise of coming King.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Messianic star*

### Chapter 25: Baal-Peor Apostasy

#### 25:1-5 — Israel Worships Baal at Peor
**Lore:** "While Israel lived in Shittim, the people began to whore with the daughters of Moab. These invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods. So Israel yoked himself to Baal of Peor" (25:1-3). Sexual immorality + idolatry. After God protected from Balaam's curse, Israel defeats itself through sin! "And the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel. And the LORD said to Moses, 'Take all the chiefs of the people and hang them in the sun before the LORD, that the fierce anger of the LORD may turn away from Israel.' And Moses said to the judges of Israel, 'Each of you kill those of his men who have yoked themselves to Baal of Peor'" (25:4-5). Harsh judgment required. Later revealed: Balaam counseled this strategy (31:16; Rev 2:14). Couldn't curse Israel, so made them curse themselves through sin.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Self-inflicted defeat*

#### 25:6-15 — Phinehas's Zeal Stops Plague
**Lore:** "And behold, one of the people of Israel came and brought a Midianite woman to his family, in the sight of Moses and in the sight of the whole congregation of the people of Israel, while they were weeping in the entrance of the tent of meeting" (25:6). Brazenflagrancy during national repentance! "When Phinehas the son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose and left the congregation and took a spear in his hand and went after the man of Israel into the chamber and pierced both of them, the man of Israel and the woman through her belly" (25:7-8). Zealous execution. "Thus the plague on the people of Israel was stopped. Nevertheless, those who died by the plague were twenty-four thousand" (25:8-9). 24,000 dead! "And the LORD said to Moses, 'Phinehas the son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned back my wrath from the people of Israel, in that he was jealous with my jealousy among them, so that I did not consume the people of Israel in my jealousy. Therefore say, "Behold, I give to him my covenant of peace, and it shall be to him and to his descendants after him the covenant of a perpetual priesthood, because he was jealous for his God and made atonement for the people of Israel"'" (25:10-13). Phinehas rewarded with covenant of peace—eternal priesthood. Zealousness for God's honor. Psalm 106:30-31: "Then Phinehas stood up and intervened, and the plague was stayed. And that was counted to him as righteousness from generation to generation forever."
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Zeal for God's honor*

#### 25:16-18 — Command to Attack Midian
**Lore:** "And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Harass the Midianites and strike them down, for they have harassed you with their wiles, with which they beguiled you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of the chief of Midian, their sister, who was killed on the day of the plague on account of Peor'" (25:16-18). Judgment pronounced. Executed in chapter 31. Midianites = descendants of Abraham through Keturah (Gen 25:1-2), but led Israel to sin. Spiritual corruption worse than military attack.
*Verses: 3 | Theme: Judgment on corrupters*

## Part XII: Second Census and Inheritance Laws (Chapters 26-27)

### Chapter 26: Second Census of New Generation

#### 26:1-51 — Counting the New Generation
**Lore:** "After the plague, the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar the son of Aaron, the priest, 'Take a census of all the congregation of the people of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, by their fathers' houses, all in Israel who are able to go to war'" (26:1-2). Second census—40 years after first (ch 1). New generation. Tribal totals (26:5-50): Reuben 43,730 (was 46,500); Simeon 22,200 (was 59,300—huge drop!); Gad 40,500 (was 45,650); Judah 76,500 (was 74,600); Issachar 64,300 (was 54,400); Zebulun 60,500 (was 57,400); Manasseh 52,700 (was 32,200); Ephraim 32,500 (was 40,500); Benjamin 45,600 (was 35,400); Dan 64,400 (was 62,700); Asher 53,400 (was 41,500); Naphtali 45,400 (was 53,400). **Total: 601,730** (was 603,550). Virtually same number! God's faithfulness despite generation's death. Wilderness wandering didn't diminish Israel. Only Caleb and Joshua remain from first census (26:64-65). Fulfills God's word (14:29-30).
*Verses: 51 | Theme: New generation, same promise*

#### 26:52-65 — Land Distribution by Lot
**Lore:** "The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Among these the land shall be divided for inheritance according to the number of names. To a large tribe you shall give a large inheritance, and to a small tribe you shall give a small inheritance; every tribe shall be given its inheritance in proportion to its list'" (26:52-54). Fair distribution by size. "But the land shall be divided by lot. According to the names of the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit. Their inheritance shall be divided according to lot between the larger and the smaller'" (26:55-56). Lot = divine selection (Prov 16:33). Combines size (fairness) with lot (divine sovereignty). Levites counted separately: 23,000 males 1 month+ (26:57-62). No land inheritance. Note: "Not one of them was left, except Caleb...and Joshua...For the LORD had said of them, 'They shall die in the wilderness.' Not one of them was left, except Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun" (26:64-65). Sobering bookend. Entire unfaithful generation gone. Faith alone survives.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Fair inheritance*
