## Design Principles Applied

### ✓ Verse Density Target: 7-8 verses/pericope
- All chapters now average 7.3-8.3 verses per pericope
- No pericopes shorter than 5 verses
- Most pericopes 6-12 verses (optimal for memorization)

### ✓ Title Requirements: Max 8 words, evocative
- "How Lonely Sits the City" (5 words)
- "Is It Nothing to You?" (5 words)
- "Yet This I Call to Mind" (6 words)
- "Good to Wait Quietly for Salvation" (6 words)
- All titles are memorable, not descriptive summaries

### ✓ Preserved Famous Passages
- "Is it nothing to you?" (1:12) - Now in pericope 1:2
- "The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases" (3:22-23) - Now in pericope 3:3
- "Great is Your faithfulness" (3:23) - Now in pericope 3:3
- "Good to wait quietly for salvation" (3:26) - Now distinct pericope 3:4
- All remain accessible and highlighted

### ✓ Respect Acrostic Structure
- Chapters 1, 2, 4, 5 are 22-verse acrostics (Hebrew alphabet)
- Chapter 3 is triple acrostic (66 verses, 3 per letter)
- Divisions respect poetic boundaries
- Don't arbitrarily split stanzas
