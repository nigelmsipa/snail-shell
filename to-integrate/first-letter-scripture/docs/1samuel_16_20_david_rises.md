# 1 Samuel 16-20: David's Rise

## Overview
God's choice (David) vs. people's choice (Saul). Spirit transfers. Goliath victory demonstrates faith. Jonathan's covenant—surrenders throne. Saul's jealousy becomes murderous. Divine protection. Arrow test. Tearful farewell. Central theme: heart over appearance, trust God's timing.

---

## Chapter 16: David Anointed

#### 16:1-13 — The Anointing of the Shepherd
**Lore:** "The LORD said to Samuel, 'Fill your horn with oil, and go. I will send you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have provided for myself a king among his sons'" (16:1). "When they came, he looked on Eliab and thought, 'Surely the LORD's anointed is before him.' But the LORD said to Samuel, 'Do not look on his appearance or on the height of his stature, because I have rejected him. For the LORD sees not as man sees: man looks on the outward appearance, but the LORD looks on the heart'" (16:6-7). **CENTRAL THEOLOGY**—God's criteria vs. human. Seven sons rejected. "There remains yet the youngest, but behold, he is keeping the sheep" (16:11). **DAVID** (דָּוִד) = "beloved"—youngest, shepherd, not considered! "Then Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brothers. And the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon David from that day forward" (16:13). Spirit comes upon David.
*Verses: 13 | Theme: Heart over appearance*

#### 16:14-23 — Spirit Transfers
**Lore:** "Now the Spirit of the LORD departed from Saul, and a harmful spirit from the LORD tormented him" (16:14). Spirit leaves Saul. David enters service as musician. "And Saul loved him greatly, and he became his armor-bearer. And whenever the harmful spirit from God was upon Saul, David took the lyre and played it with his hand. So Saul was refreshed and was well" (16:21-23). Music soothes. Anointed serves rejected while waiting.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Spirit departs*

## Chapter 17: Goliath

#### 17:1-16 — The Giant's Challenge
**Lore:** "And there came out from the camp of the Philistines a champion named Goliath of Gath, whose height was six cubits and a span" (17:4). Over 9 feet. "He stood and shouted to the ranks of Israel, 'I defy the ranks of Israel this day. Give me a man, that we may fight together'" (17:10). Defies God! "When Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, they were dismayed and greatly afraid" (17:11). Paralyzed by fear—40 days.
*Verses: 16 | Theme: Giant's defiance*

#### 17:17-30 — David Volunteers
**Lore:** David delivers food, arrives during challenge. "And David said to the men who stood by him, 'What shall be done for the man who kills this Philistine and takes away the reproach from Israel? For who is this uncircumcised Philistine, that he should defy the armies of the living God?'" (17:26). Spiritual perspective! Eliab's jealousy (17:28). David persists.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Faith vs. fear*

#### 17:31-40 — Testimony and Preparation
**Lore:** "But David said to Saul, 'Your servant used to keep sheep for his father. And when there came a lion, or a bear, and took a lamb from the flock, I went after him and struck him... Your servant has struck down both lions and bears, and this uncircumcised Philistine shall be like one of them'" (17:34-36). Past faithfulness! "The LORD who delivered me from the paw of the lion and from the paw of the bear will deliver me from the hand of this Philistine" (17:37). Saul's armor doesn't fit (17:38-39). "Then he took his staff in his hand and chose five smooth stones from the brook" (17:40). Simple tools.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Wilderness prepared*

#### 17:41-58 — Victory and Fame
**Lore:** "Then David said to the Philistine, 'You come to me with a sword and with a spear and with a javelin, but I come to you in the name of the LORD of hosts, the God of the armies of Israel, whom you have defied... that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel... For the battle is the LORD's'" (17:45-47). Faith declaration! "And David put his hand in his bag and took out a stone and slung it and struck the Philistine on his forehead. The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground" (17:49). One shot! Complete victory.
*Verses: 18 | Theme: Battle is LORD's*

## Chapter 18: Jealousy Begins

#### 18:1-9 — Covenant and Envy
**Lore:** "As soon as he had finished speaking to Saul, the soul of Jonathan was knit to the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul. Then Jonathan made a covenant with David, because he loved him as his own soul. And Jonathan stripped himself of the robe that was on him and gave it to David, and his armor" (18:1-4). Crown prince surrenders throne! "The women came out of all the cities of Israel, singing and dancing... 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands'" (18:6-7). Fateful song. "And Saul was very angry... 'They have ascribed to David ten thousands, and to me they have ascribed thousands, and what more can he have but the kingdom?' And Saul eyed David from that day on" (18:8-9). Jealousy ignited.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Covenant vs. jealousy*

#### 18:10-30 — Murder Attempts and Marriage Trap
**Lore:** "The next day a harmful spirit from God rushed upon Saul... Saul had his spear in his hand. And Saul hurled the spear... But David evaded him twice" (18:10-11). First murder attempts! Saul's strategy: remove David, give dangerous commands, hopes Philistines kill him. Marriage trap—demands 100 Philistine foreskins as bride-price (18:25). "David arose and went, along with his men, and killed two hundred of the Philistines" (18:27). Exceeds requirement! "But when Saul saw and knew that the LORD was with David... Saul was even more afraid of David. So Saul was David's enemy continually" (18:28-29).
*Verses: 21 | Theme: Deadly schemes*

## Chapter 19: Divine Protection

#### 19:1-10 — Jonathan's Intercession
**Lore:** "And Saul spoke to Jonathan his son and to all his servants, that they should kill David" (19:1). Jonathan intercedes: "Let not the king sin against his servant David, because he has not sinned against you, and because his deeds have brought good to you... Why then will you sin against innocent blood by killing David without cause?'" (19:4-5). Saul swears oath (19:6). "Then a harmful spirit from the LORD came upon Saul, as he sat in his house with his spear in his hand. And David was playing the lyre. And Saul sought to pin David to the wall with the spear, but he eluded Saul" (19:9-10). Breaks oath—second spear attack!
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Broken oaths*

#### 19:11-24 — Flight to Samuel
**Lore:** "Michal, David's wife, told him, 'If you do not escape with your life tonight, tomorrow you will be killed.' So Michal let David down through the window" (19:11-12). Window escape like Rahab. "Now David fled and escaped, and he came to Samuel at Ramah" (19:18). Flees to prophet. "Then Saul sent messengers to take David, and when they saw the company of the prophets prophesying, and Samuel standing as head over them, the Spirit of God came upon the messengers of Saul, and they also prophesied" (19:20). Divine protection—three waves incapacitated! "Then he himself went to Ramah... And the Spirit of God came upon him also, and as he went he prophesied... and lay naked all that day and all that night. Thus it is said, 'Is Saul also among the prophets?'" (19:23-24). Saul incapacitated—can't touch David at prophet's dwelling.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Spirit protects*

## Chapter 20: Final Test

#### 20:1-23 — Arrow Test Arranged
**Lore:** "Then David fled from Naioth in Ramah and came and said before Jonathan, 'What have I done?... there is but a step between me and death'" (20:1-3). David proposes test—miss feast to gauge Saul's reaction. "If I am still alive, show me the steadfast love of the LORD, that I may not die; and do not cut off your steadfast love from my house forever, when the LORD cuts off every one of the enemies of David from the face of the earth.' And Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David" (20:14-16). Covenant renewed—Jonathan asks David show hesed to descendants. Arrow signal arranged (20:19-23).
*Verses: 23 | Theme: Testing loyalty*

#### 20:24-42 — Tearful Farewell
**Lore:** Second day of feast: "And Saul said to Jonathan his son, 'Why has the son of Jesse not come to the meal, either yesterday or today?'" (20:27). Jonathan gives excuse. "Then Saul's anger was kindled against Jonathan, and he said to him, 'You son of a perverse, rebellious woman... For as long as the son of Jesse lives on the earth, neither you nor your kingdom shall be established... he shall surely die.' Then Jonathan answered Saul his father, 'Why should he be put to death? What has he done?' But Saul hurled his spear at him to strike him" (20:30-33). Attacks son! Irrevocably turned. Arrow signal given. "And they kissed one another and wept with one another, David weeping the most. Then Jonathan said to David, 'Go in peace, because we have sworn both of us in the name of the LORD, saying, "The LORD shall be between you and me, and between your offspring and my offspring, forever"'" (20:41-42). Tearful farewell—covenant forever.
*Verses: 19 | Theme: Covenant transcends*

**1 SAMUEL 16-20 COMPLETE!**

**Pericopes: 15** (5 chapters = 3/chapter)

**Theme Summary:** David anointed—Spirit transfers. Goliath victory shows faith defeats fear. Jonathan's covenant—surrenders throne willingly. Saul's jealousy → murder attempts, broken oaths, marriage trap. Divine protection at Ramah—Spirit incapacitates Saul. Arrow test confirms irrevocable hatred. Tearful parting—covenant bond survives. Pattern: God protects His anointed while teaching patience for His timing.
