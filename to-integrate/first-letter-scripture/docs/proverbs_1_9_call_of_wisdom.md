# Proverbs 1-9: PrMP 1 — The Call of Wisdom: The Two Paths

## Overview
**THE FOUNDATION** of the entire book. These 9 chapters form a sustained dramatic narrative presenting life's ultimate choice between two personified voices: **Lady Wisdom** (חָכְמָה, chokmah) and **Dame Folly** (the adulteress, אִשָּׁה זָרָה, ishah zarah). **Structure**: Preamble (1:1-7, book's purpose and motto) → Ten parental discourses alternating between positive exhortations to wisdom and negative warnings against folly → Climactic contrast (Ch 9, two competing banquets). **Central theological anchor**: "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge" (1:7), the book's controlling principle forming an inclusio with 31:30. **Literary form**: Extended poems, not brief proverbs. Speech-heavy (direct addresses from father, mother, Wisdom personified). **Pedagogical strategy**: Internalize the WHY before the WHAT (Chapters 1-9 provide motivation and worldview; Chapters 10-31 provide specific application). **Christological preview**: Wisdom personified (Ch 8) = pre-incarnate Christ (1 Cor 1:24, 30; Col 2:3). Central themes: Parental instruction, sexual purity, diligence vs. sloth, cosmic Wisdom present at creation, the Two Ways (Psalm 1 parallel), call-and-response drama, covenant faithfulness, the "simple" as target audience.

## Chapter 1: Introduction and Wisdom's First Call

#### 1:1-7 — The Proverbs of Solomon: The Fear of the LORD Is the Beginning of Knowledge
**Lore:** "The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel" (1:1). **SUPERSCRIPTION**—"Proverbs" (מִשְׁלֵי, mishlei, plural of mashal, similitude/parable/wise saying). "Solomon" (שְׁלֹמֹה, Shlomoh) = David's son, wisest king (1 Kgs 3:12, 4:29-34, composed 3,000 proverbs). Canonical attribution even though book contains non-Solomonic collections (Ch 30-31). "To know wisdom and instruction; to perceive the words of understanding; To receive the instruction of wisdom, justice, and judgment, and equity" (1:2-3). **PURPOSE STATEMENT**—fivefold infinitives of intent: (1) know wisdom (חָכְמָה, chokmah), (2) instruction (מוּסָר, musar, discipline/correction), (3) perceive understanding (בִּינָה, binah), (4) receive instruction in wise dealing (הַשְׂכֵּל, haskel), (5) righteousness, justice, equity (צֶדֶק וּמִשְׁפָּט וּמֵישָׁרִים, tzedeq umishpat umeisharim). Comprehensive education = intellectual, moral, practical. "To give subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion" (1:4). **TARGET AUDIENCE**—"simple" (פֶּתִי, peti, naive/gullible, open to either wisdom or folly, Prov 1:22, 32; 7:7; 8:5; 9:4). "Young man" (נַעַר, na'ar). Book = preventative medicine, protect naive from folly's seduction. "A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels: To understand a proverb, and the interpretation; the words of the wise, and their dark sayings" (1:5-6). **ADVANCED STUDENTS ALSO**—even wise benefit (perpetual growth). "Proverb" (מָשָׁל, mashal), "interpretation" (מְלִיצָה, melitzah, figure of speech), "dark sayings" (חִידָה, chidah, riddles/enigmatic sayings). Wisdom = lifelong pursuit, not one-time attainment. **"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction"** (1:7). **BOOK'S MOTTO**—"fear of LORD" (יִרְאַת יְהוָה, yirat YHWH) appears 14x in Proverbs (1:7, 29; 2:5; 8:13; 9:10; 10:27; 14:26-27; 15:16, 33; 16:6; 19:23; 22:4; 23:17; 31:30). "Beginning" (רֵאשִׁית, reshit, first/best/chief, not merely starting point but foundation/essence). "Knowledge" (דַּעַת, da'at). "Fools" (אֱוִילִים, evilim, morally deficient, not intellectually challenged). **Inclusio with 31:30**—"woman who fears LORD shall be praised" bookends entire anthology. **Theological definition**: Fear ≠ terror, but reverent awe, covenant loyalty, submission to divine authority (Deut 10:12-13; Ps 111:10; Eccl 12:13). NT: "Fear of God" = "wisdom from above" (James 3:17). Christ embodied perfect fear of LORD (Isa 11:2-3). This verse = hermeneutical key to entire book—all practical wisdom flows from this theological root.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Foundation*

#### 1:8-19 — My Son, Hear Your Father's Instruction: Enticers and Violent Men
**Lore:** "My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother" (1:8). **FIRST PARENTAL DISCOURSE**—"my son" (בְּנִי, beni) appears 23x in Prov 1-9, direct address creating intimacy. "Father's instruction" (מוּסַר אָבִיךָ, musar avikha), "mother's law/teaching" (תּוֹרַת אִמֶּךָ, torat immekha). Both parents = teachers (Deut 6:7; Prov 6:20; 31:1, Lemuel's mother). "For they shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck" (1:9). Wisdom = adornment (4:9; cf. Gen 41:42, Joseph's chain). Visible honor. "My son, if sinners entice thee, consent thou not" (1:10). **WARNING AGAINST GANG VIOLENCE**—"sinners" (חַטָּאִים, chatta'im), "entice" (פָתָה, patah, seduce/deceive). First temptation = peer pressure. "If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause: Let us swallow them up alive as the grave; and whole, as those that go down into the pit: We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoil: Cast in thy lot among us; let us all have one purse" (1:11-14). **GANG'S SEDUCTIVE PITCH**—violence for profit. "Lay wait for blood" (נֶאֶרְבָה לְדָם, ne'ervah ledam), "swallow alive as Sheol" (כִּשְׁאוֹל נִבְלָעֵם חַיִּים, kishol nivla'em chayyim, cf. Num 16:30-33, Korah swallowed). "Precious substance" (הוֹן יָקָר, hon yaqar), "spoil" (שָׁלָל, shalal, plunder). "One purse" (כִּיס אֶחָד, kis echad) = share loot equally. Appeal to greed + belonging. "My son, walk not thou in the way with them; refrain thy foot from their path: For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood" (1:15-16). **COUNTER-COMMAND**—"walk not in way" (אַל־תֵּלֵךְ בְּדֶרֶךְ, al-telekh baderekh). "Feet run to evil" (רַגְלֵיהֶם לָרַע יָרוּצוּ, ragleihem lara' yarutzu, Isa 59:7, same phrase). "Surely in vain the net is spread in the sight of any bird. And they lay wait for their own blood; they lurk privily for their own lives" (1:17-18). **IRONIC REVERSAL**—bird sees net, avoids it (obvious trap). Gang members = less wise than birds (don't see they're trapping themselves). "Lay wait for OWN blood" = self-destruction. Boomerang justice. "So are the ways of every one that is greedy of gain; which taketh away the life of the owners thereof" (1:19). **GENERAL PRINCIPLE**—"greedy of gain" (בֹּצֵעַ בָּצַע, botze'a batza, cuts off unjust gain). Ill-gotten wealth "takes away life of owners" (cf. James 5:1-6). **Practical application**: Refuse peer pressure, avoid violent companions, understand greed's self-destructive trajectory. NT: "Love of money = root of all evil" (1 Tim 6:10). Judas = greedy of gain, self-destruction (Matt 27:3-5).
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Violence*

#### 1:20-33 — Wisdom Cries in the Streets: How Long, You Simple Ones?
**Lore:** "Wisdom crieth without; she uttereth her voice in the streets: She crieth in the chief place of concourse, in the openings of the gates: in the city she uttereth her words" (1:20-21). **LADY WISDOM'S FIRST CALL**—"Wisdom" (חָכְמוֹת, chokmot, plural of majesty or personification plural). PERSONIFIED as woman publicly preaching. "Streets" (חוּץ, chutz), "chief place of concourse" (רֹאשׁ הֹמִיּוֹת, rosh homiyyot, head of noisy crowds), "gates" (שְׁעָרִים, she'arim, city gates = legal/commercial center, Deut 21:19; Ruth 4:1). Public, universal call—no one can claim ignorance. Contrast: Dame Folly also calls in streets (9:14-15), but Wisdom = first/legitimate claim. **Christological**: Christ preached publicly (John 18:20, "I spake openly to world"). "How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity? and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge?" (1:22). **THREEFOLD AUDIENCE**—(1) "Simple ones" (פְּתָיִם, petayim, naive, still redeemable, 9:4); (2) "Scorners" (לֵצִים, letzim, mockers, hardened rebels, 3:34; 9:7-8; 13:1; 14:6; 15:12); (3) "Fools" (כְּסִילִים, kesilim, dull-minded, reject instruction, 1:7; 10:23). Rhetorical question = lament over wasted opportunity. "Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you, I will make known my words unto you" (1:23). **OFFER OF GRACE**—"Turn" (שׁוּבוּ, shuvu, repent). "Reproof" (תּוֹכַחְתִּי, tochachti, correction). "Pour out my spirit" (אַבִּיעָה לָכֶם רוּחִי, abbi'ah lakhem ruchi) = generous, abundant revelation (Joel 2:28-29; Acts 2:17). Divine initiative precedes human response. "Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded; But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof: I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your fear cometh" (1:24-26). **JUDGMENT FOR REJECTION**—Wisdom called, they refused. "Stretched out hand" (נָטִיתִי יָדִי, natiti yadi, Isa 65:2, God's persistent invitation). "Set at nought" (פָּרַעְתֶּם, para'tem, ignored/nullified). "Laugh at calamity" (אֶשְׂחַק בְּאֵידְכֶם, es'chaq be'eidkhem), "mock" (אֶלְעַג, el'ag) = divine retribution mirrors human scorn. God's laughter = judicial response (Ps 2:4; 37:13). "When your fear cometh as desolation, and your destruction cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish cometh upon you. Then shall they call upon me, but I will not answer; they shall seek me early, but they shall not find me" (1:27-28). **TOO LATE**—"fear" (פַּחְדְּכֶם, pachdekhem), "destruction" (אֵידְכֶם, eidkhem), "whirlwind" (סוּפָה, sufah, storm). When calamity strikes, prayers ignored (Isa 1:15; Jer 11:11; Mic 3:4; Zech 7:13). Opportunity window closes. Parallel: Rich man and Lazarus (Luke 16:24-26, "great gulf fixed"). "For that they hated knowledge, and did not choose the fear of the LORD: They would none of my counsel: they despised all my reproof. Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices" (1:29-31). **SELF-INFLICTED CONSEQUENCE**—"hated knowledge," "did not choose fear of LORD" (cf. 1:7, foundation rejected). "Eat fruit of own way" (וְיֹאכְלוּ מִפְּרִי דַרְכָּם, veyokhlu mipri darkam) = harvest what you plant (Gal 6:7-8). "Filled with own devices" (מִמֹּעֲצֹתֵיהֶם יִשְׂבָּעוּ, mimmoatzoteihem yisba'u) = stuffed/satiated with consequences (Isa 3:10-11). "For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them. But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely, and shall be quiet from fear of evil" (1:32-33). **CONTRASTING DESTINIES**—"turning away" (מְשׁוּבַת, meshuvat, apostasy), "prosperity of fools destroys" (שַׁלְוַת כְּסִילִים תְּאַבְּדֵם, shalvat kesilim te'abdem, false security). Vs. "hearken → dwell safely" (יִשְׁכֹּן־בֶּטַח, yishkon-betach, Deut 33:12, 28). "Quiet from fear" (שַׁאֲנַן מִפַּחַד רָעָה, sha'anan miphachad ra'ah, Ps 112:7). **Two Ways established**: Death (reject Wisdom) vs. Life (heed Wisdom). Foundation for rest of book.
*Verses: 14 | Theme: Call*

## Chapter 2: The Benefits of Seeking Wisdom

#### 2:1-11 — If You Seek Wisdom as Silver, Then You Will Understand the Fear of the LORD
**Lore:** "My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and hide my commandments with thee; So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom, and apply thine heart to understanding; Yea, if thou criest after knowledge, and liftest up thy voice for understanding; If thou seekest her as silver, and searchest for her as for hid treasures" (2:1-4). **CONDITIONAL PROTASIS**—massive "if" clause (vv.1-4) before "then" (v.5). **FIVEFOLD EFFORT REQUIRED**: (1) receive/accept words (לָקַח, laqach); (2) hide/treasure commandments (צָפַן, tzafan, store up, Matt 6:19-21); (3) incline ear (attentive listening); (4) cry after knowledge (active pursuit, not passive); (5) seek as silver/treasure (mine for it, Job 28:1-11, mining metaphor). Effort level = prospector's intensity. "Then shalt thou understand the fear of the LORD, and find the knowledge of God" (2:5). **APODOSIS/RESULT**—"then understand fear of LORD" (אָז תָּבִין יִרְאַת יְהוָה, az tavin yirat YHWH). Echoes 1:7 (fear of LORD = beginning). Deep seeking → experiential knowledge of God. "For the LORD giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding" (2:6). **DIVINE SOURCE**—human effort prerequisite, but God = ultimate Giver (James 1:5, "If any lack wisdom, let him ask of God"). "Out of his mouth" (מִפִּיו, mippiv) = revelation, God speaks wisdom (Deut 8:3; Matt 4:4, man lives by word from God's mouth). "He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous: he is a buckler to them that walk uprightly. He keepeth the paths of judgment, and preserveth the way of his saints" (2:7-8). **DIVINE PROTECTION**—"layeth up" (יָצְפֹּן, yatzpon, stores/treasures), "sound wisdom" (תּוּשִׁיָּה, tushiyyah, practical/effective wisdom). "Buckler" (מָגֵן, magen, shield, Ps 3:3; 18:2). God guards path of righteous. "Then shalt thou understand righteousness, and judgment, and equity; yea, every good path. When wisdom entereth into thine heart, and knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul; Discretion shall preserve thee, understanding shall keep thee" (2:9-11). **INTERNAL TRANSFORMATION**—wisdom enters heart (לֵב, lev), becomes pleasant (נָעֵם, na'em, delight, not burden). "Discretion" (מְזִמָּה, mezimmah, prudence/plan) + understanding = internal GPS guiding away from danger. Wisdom = protective force.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Seeking*

#### 2:12-22 — Wisdom Delivers You From the Way of Evil and the Strange Woman
**Lore:** "To deliver thee from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh froward things; Who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness; Who rejoice to do evil, and delight in the frowardness of the wicked; Whose ways are crooked, and they froward in their paths" (2:12-15). **FIRST DELIVERANCE: FROM EVIL MEN**—"deliver" (לְהַצִּילְךָ, lehatzilkha, rescue/save). "Evil man" (אִישׁ רָע, ish ra), "froward" (תַּהְפֻּכוֹת, tahpukhot, perverse). "Leave paths of uprightness" (עֹזְבֵי אָרְחוֹת יֹשֶׁר, ozvei orchot yosher) = apostates. "Walk in ways of darkness" = moral blindness (John 3:19-20). "Rejoice to do evil" = depravity delights in wickedness (Rom 1:32, "not only do same, but have pleasure in them"). "Crooked ways" (עִקְּשִׁים, iqqeshim, twisted, Deut 32:5; Phil 2:15). Wisdom = moral compass avoiding these companions. "To deliver thee from the strange woman, even from the stranger which flattereth with her words; Which forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth the covenant of her God" (2:16-17). **SECOND DELIVERANCE: FROM ADULTERESS**—"strange woman" (אִשָּׁה זָרָה, ishah zarah), "stranger" (נָכְרִיָּה, nokhriyyah, foreign/forbidden woman). Not necessarily ethnic foreigner, but "other than your wife" (5:20; 6:24; 7:5; 20:16; 23:27). "Flattereth" (הֶחֱלִיקָה, hechliqqah, smooth talk, 5:3; 7:21). "Forsaketh guide of youth" = abandoned husband? "Covenant of her God" (בְּרִית אֱלֹהֶיהָ, berit eloheha) = marriage covenant (Mal 2:14). Adultery = covenant breaking. "For her house inclineth unto death, and her paths unto the dead. None that go unto her return again, neither take they hold of the paths of life" (2:18-19). **DEATH IMAGERY**—"house inclines to death" (שָׁחָה אֶל־מָוֶת בֵּיתָהּ, shachah el-mavet beitah), "paths to the dead/Rephaim" (רְפָאִים, refa'im, shades/spirits in Sheol). "None return" = point of no return (7:27; 9:18). Hyperbolic warning (David returned after Bathsheba, but with devastating consequences). Sexual sin = particularly destructive (1 Cor 6:18). "That thou mayest walk in the way of good men, and keep the paths of the righteous. For the upright shall dwell in the land, and the perfect shall remain in it. But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth, and the transgressors shall be rooted out of it" (2:20-22). **POSITIVE CONCLUSION**—wisdom → walk with righteous → dwell in land (Ps 37:3, 9, 11, 22, 29, 34; Matt 5:5, "meek inherit earth"). "Cut off from land" = covenant curse (Deut 28:63; Ps 37:38). Land tenure = reward for righteousness (Promised Land theology).
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Deliverance*

## Chapter 3: Trust in the LORD and the Blessings of Wisdom

#### 3:1-12 — Trust in the LORD With All Your Heart; Whom the LORD Loves He Corrects
**Lore:** "My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments: For length of days, and long life, and peace, shall they add to thee" (3:1-2). **THIRD PARENTAL DISCOURSE**—"forget not" (אַל־תִּשְׁכַּח, al-tishkach, Deut 6:12; 8:11). "Heart keep commandments" (לֵב יִצֹּר, lev yitzor). Rewards: "length of days" (אֹרֶךְ יָמִים, orekh yamim), "peace" (שָׁלוֹם, shalom). Covenant blessings (Deut 30:20; 1 Kgs 3:14). "Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart: So shalt thou find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man" (3:3-4). "Mercy and truth" (חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת, chesed ve'emet, covenant love + faithfulness, Exod 34:6). "Bind about neck" (קָשְׁרֵם עַל־גַּרְגְּרֹתֶךָ, qoshrem al-gargerotekha, Deut 6:8; 11:18, phylacteries). "Write on heart" = internalize (Jer 31:33; 2 Cor 3:3). Result: "favor with God and man" (Luke 2:52, Jesus grew in favor). **"Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths"** (3:5-6). **MOST FAMOUS VERSES IN PROVERBS**—"Trust in LORD" (בְּטַח אֶל־יְהוָה, betach el-YHWH, Ps 37:3; Isa 26:4). "With all heart" (בְּכָל־לִבֶּךָ, bekhol-libekha, total commitment). "Lean not on own understanding" (אַל־תִּשָּׁעֵן עַל־בִּינָתְךָ, al-tisha'en al-binatkha) = don't rely on human wisdom alone (Isa 55:8-9, God's thoughts higher). "Acknowledge him" (דָּעֵהוּ, da'ehu, know/recognize Him). "Direct paths" (יְיַשֵּׁר אָרְחֹתֶיךָ, yeyasher orchotekha, make straight, Ps 37:23). Divine guidance promised. "Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the LORD, and depart from evil. It shall be health to thy navel, and marrow to thy bones" (3:7-8). "Not wise in own eyes" (אַל־תְּהִי חָכָם בְּעֵינֶיךָ, al-tehi chakham be'einekha, Rom 12:16; Isa 5:21). Humility required. "Health to navel" (רִפְאוּת לְשָׁרֶּךָ, rifu'ut lesharerekha, healing), "marrow to bones" (שִׁקּוּי לְעַצְמוֹתֶיךָ, shiqquy le'atzmotekha, refreshment) = physical benefits of spiritual obedience (psychosomatic truth). "Honour the LORD with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase: So shall thy barns be filled with plenty, and thy presses shall burst out with new wine" (3:9-10). **FIRSTFRUITS PRINCIPLE**—"honour LORD with substance" (כַּבֵּד אֶת־יְהוָה מֵהוֹנֶךָ, kabbed et-YHWH mehonekha). "Firstfruits" (רֵאשִׁית, reshit, Exod 23:19; Lev 23:10; Deut 26:2). Giving first portion = trust. Reward: "barns filled" (אֲסָמֶיךָ שָׂבָע, asamekha sava), "presses burst" (וִיקָבֶיךָ תִּירוֹשׁ יִפְרֹצוּ, viqavekha tirosh yifrotzu). Generous giver blessed (Mal 3:10; Luke 6:38; 2 Cor 9:6-11). **"My son, despise not the chastening of the LORD; neither be weary of his correction: For whom the LORD loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth"** (3:11-12). **DISCIPLINE AS LOVE**—"despise not chastening" (אַל־תִּמְאַס מוּסַר יְהוָה, al-timas musar YHWH). "Weary" (קוּץ, qutz, loathe). "Whom LORD loves, He corrects" (כִּי אֶת אֲשֶׁר יֶאֱהַב יְהוָה יוֹכִיחַ, ki et asher ye'ehav YHWH yokhiach). Quoted Heb 12:5-6 (discipline = proof of sonship). "Father delights in son" (וּכְאָב אֶת־בֵּן יִרְצֶה, ukhe'av et-ben yirtzeh). Suffering ≠ rejection, often = refining (Job 23:10; 1 Pet 1:6-7).
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Trust*

#### 3:13-20 — Happy Is the Man Who Finds Wisdom; By Wisdom the LORD Founded the Earth
**Lore:** "Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding. For the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold" (3:13-14). **BEATITUDE**—"Happy" (אַשְׁרֵי, ashrei, blessed, Ps 1:1; 119:1; Matt 5:3-11). "Finds wisdom" (מָצָא חָכְמָה, matza chokmah) = discovery requiring search (2:4-5). "Merchandise" (סַחְרָהּ, sachrah), wisdom more profitable than silver/gold (8:10-11, 19; Job 28:15-19). "She is more precious than rubies: and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her. Length of days is in her right hand; and in her left hand riches and honour" (3:15-16). "More precious than rubies" (מִפְּנִינִים, mippeninim, pearls/corals, 8:11; 20:15; 31:10). "Right hand" = long life (אֹרֶךְ יָמִים, orekh yamim), "left hand" = riches + honor (עֹשֶׁר וְכָבוֹד, osher vekhavod). Wisdom PERSONIFIED holding gifts (foreshadows Ch 8). Both temporal and eternal rewards. "Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her" (3:17-18). "Ways of pleasantness" (דַּרְכֵי־נֹעַם, darkhei-no'am), "paths of peace" (שָׁלוֹם, shalom) = not burden but delight. **"Tree of life"** (עֵץ חַיִּים, etz chayyim)—CRITICAL PHRASE. Four times in Proverbs (3:18; 11:30; 13:12; 15:4). Echoes Gen 2:9; 3:22, 24 (Eden's tree). Wisdom = path back to tree (Rev 2:7; 22:2, 14, ultimate restoration). "Happy" (אַשְׁרֵי, ashrei) repeated, inclusio. **"The LORD by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding hath he established the heavens. By his knowledge the depths are broken up, and the clouds drop down the dew"** (3:19-20). **COSMIC WISDOM**—God USED wisdom as instrument in creation (בְּחָכְמָה, bechokmah). "Founded earth" (יָסַד־אָרֶץ, yasad-aretz), "established heavens" (כּוֹנֵן שָׁמַיִם, khonen shamayim), "depths broken up" (תְּהוֹמוֹת נִבְקָעוּ, tehomot nivqa'u, Gen 7:11, fountains of deep), "clouds drop dew" (שְׁחָקִים יִרְעֲפוּ־טָל, shechaqim yir'afu-tal). Wisdom = God's blueprint (8:22-31 expands). Christ = "wisdom of God" (1 Cor 1:24), "by Him all things created" (Col 1:16; John 1:3). Seeking wisdom = aligning with cosmic order.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Value*

#### 3:21-35 — Keep Sound Wisdom; Do Not Withhold Good From Those to Whom It Is Due
**Lore:** "My son, let not them depart from thine eyes: keep sound wisdom and discretion: So shall they be life unto thy soul, and grace to thy neck" (3:21-22). "Sound wisdom" (תּוּשִׁיָּה, tushiyyah, 2:7; 8:14; 18:1), "discretion" (מְזִמָּה, mezimmah, prudence, 2:11). "Life to soul" (חַיִּים לְנַפְשֶׁךָ, chayyim lenafshekha), "grace to neck" (ornament). Wisdom = vitality. "Then shalt thou walk in thy way safely, and thy foot shall not stumble. When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet" (3:23-24). **PRACTICAL SECURITY**—"walk safely" (לָבֶטַח, lavetach, Ps 23:4). "Foot not stumble" (Ps 91:11-12; 121:3). "Lie down not afraid" (לֹא־תִפְחָד, lo-tifchad), "sleep sweet" (וְעָרְבָה שְׁנָתֶךָ, ve'arVah shenatekha, Ps 4:8; 127:2). Wisdom → peace of mind. "Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh. For the LORD shall be thy confidence, and shall keep thy foot from being taken" (3:25-26). "Sudden fear/terror" (מִפַּחַד פִּתְאֹם, miphachad pit'om, Isa 47:11), "desolation of wicked" (שֹׁאַת רְשָׁעִים, sho'at resha'im). "LORD thy confidence" (יְהוָה יִהְיֶה בְכִסְלֶךָ, YHWH yihyeh vekhislekha, 14:26). Divine protection promised. **"Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it. Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again, and to morrow I will give; when thou hast it by thee"** (3:27-28). **NEIGHBORLY OBLIGATION**—"withhold not good" (אַל־תִּמְנַע־טוֹב, al-timna-tov) from those entitled (בַּעֲלָיו, be'alav, its owners/due recipients). "Power of hand" (לְאֵל יָדֶךָ, le'el yadekha, ability). Don't delay helping (James 2:15-16; 4:17; 1 John 3:17). "Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee. Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm" (3:29-30). Don't plot against neighbor (חָרַשׁ רָעָה, charash ra'ah, engineer harm). Don't quarrel needlessly (רִיב, riv, strive, Rom 12:18). "Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways. For the froward is abomination to the LORD: but his secret is with the righteous" (3:31-32). "Envy not oppressor" (אַל־תְּקַנֵּא בְּאִישׁ חָמָס, al-teqanne be'ish chamas, Ps 37:1; 73:3). "Froward = abomination" (תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, to'avat YHWH, 11:20; 15:26). "Secret with righteous" (סוֹד יְהוָה לִירֵאָיו, sod YHWH lirei'av, Ps 25:14, intimate counsel). "The curse of the LORD is in the house of the wicked: but he blesseth the habitation of the just. Surely he scorneth the scorners: but he giveth grace unto the lowly. The wise shall inherit glory: but shame shall be the promotion of fools" (3:33-35). **CONTRASTS**—curse vs. blessing (מְאֵרַת vs. בָּרֵךְ, me'erat vs. barekh, Deut 28). "He scorns scorners" (לַלֵּצִים הוּא־יָלִיץ, laletzim hu-yalitz, gives them back their own medicine). "Grace to lowly" (וְלַעֲנָוִים יִתֶּן־חֵן, vela'anavim yitten-chen, James 4:6; 1 Pet 5:5, same quote). "Wise inherit glory" (כָּבוֹד, kavod), "fools promoted to shame" (קָלוֹן, qalon, dishonor). Ironic: fools exalted to public disgrace.

## Chapter 4: The Path of the Righteous vs. The Path of the Wicked

#### 4:1-9 — Hear, My Children: Get Wisdom, Get Understanding
**Lore:** "Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding. For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law" (4:1-2). **FOURTH DISCOURSE**—"children" (בָּנִים, banim, plural), multiple sons addressed. "Good doctrine" (לֶקַח טוֹב, leqach tov, good teaching). "For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother. He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live" (4:3-4). **GENERATIONAL TRANSMISSION**—father recounts HIS father's teaching (three generations: grandfather → father → son). "Tender" (רַךְ, rakh, young), "only beloved" (יָחִיד, yachid, cherished). Wisdom = family legacy (Deut 6:7; 2 Tim 1:5; 3:15). **"Get wisdom, get understanding: forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth. Forsake her not, and she shall preserve thee: love her, and she shall keep thee"** (4:5-6). **IMPERATIVE VERBS**—"get wisdom" (קְנֵה חָכְמָה, qeneh chokmah), "get understanding" (קְנֵה בִינָה, qeneh binah). Repeated for emphasis. Active pursuit required. "Forsake not... love her" = Wisdom as bride to be courted. "Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding. Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her. She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace: a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee" (4:7-9). "Wisdom = principal thing" (רֵאשִׁית חָכְמָה, reshit chokmah, first/chief). "With all thy getting" (בְּכָל־קִנְיָנְךָ, bekhol-qinyanekha, with all your possessions). Worth everything. "Exalt her... embrace her" = romantic imagery. "Crown of glory" (עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת, ateret tiferet, 16:31; Isa 28:5; 62:3). Wisdom = royal coronation.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Pursuit*

#### 4:10-19 — The Path of the Righteous Is Like the Shining Sun
**Lore:** "Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many. I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths. When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble" (4:10-12). Long life promised (years multiplied). "Steps not straitened" (לֹא־יֵצַר צַעֲדֶךָ, lo-yetzar tza'adekha, not cramped/hindered). "Run not stumble" (תָרוּץ וְלֹא תִכָּשֵׁל, tarutz velo tikkashel, Ps 119:32). Freedom of movement. "Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go: keep her; for she is thy life" (4:13). "Take fast hold" (הַחֲזֵק, hachazek, grip tightly). "She is thy life" (הִיא חַיֶּיךָ, hi chayyekha). Wisdom = life itself. "Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men. Avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away" (4:14-15). **FOURFOLD COMMAND TO AVOID**—"enter not... go not... avoid... turn from." Emphatic repetition. Total separation from wicked's path. "For they sleep not, except they have done mischief; and their sleep is taken away, unless they cause some to fall. For they eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence" (4:16-17). Wicked = insomniacs driven by evil. "Bread of wickedness" (לֶחֶם רֶשַׁע, lechem resha), "wine of violence" (יֵין חֲמָסִים, yein chamasim) = sustenance from evil deeds. Addiction to wrongdoing. **"But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day. The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble"** (4:18-19). **FAMOUS CONTRAST**—"path of just" (אֹרַח צַדִּיקִים, orach tzaddiqim) = "shining light" (כְּאוֹר נֹגַהּ, ke'or nogah), progressive illumination. "More and more" (הוֹלֵךְ וָאוֹר, holekh va'or, going and brightening). "Perfect day" (נְכוֹן הַיּוֹם, nekhon hayyom, established day, high noon). Vs. "way of wicked" (דֶּרֶךְ רְשָׁעִים, derekh resha'im) = "darkness" (אֲפֵלָה, afelah), stumbling blind. Two paths = light vs. darkness (Ps 1; Matt 7:13-14; John 8:12; 12:35).
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Paths*

#### 4:20-27 — Keep Your Heart With All Diligence; Ponder the Path of Your Feet
**Lore:** "My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings. Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thine heart. For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh" (4:20-22). Standard opening. "Life... health to flesh" (מַרְפֵּא לְכָל־בְּשָׂרוֹ, marpe lekhol-besaro, healing, 3:8). **"Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life"** (4:23). **CRITICAL VERSE**—"keep heart" (נְצֹר לִבְּךָ, netzor libekha, guard). "With all diligence" (מִכָּל־מִשְׁמָר, mikkol-mishmar, above all guarding, military watchfulness). "Out of it = issues of life" (כִּי־מִמֶּנּוּ תּוֹצְאוֹת חַיִּים, ki-mimmennu totze'ot chayyim, from it flow springs of life). Heart = source/wellspring (Matt 12:34; 15:18-19; Luke 6:45, "out of abundance of heart mouth speaks"). Control heart = control life. "Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee" (4:24). **SPEECH CONTROL**—"froward mouth" (עִקְּשׁוּת פֶּה, iqqeshut peh, crooked/perverse speech). "Perverse lips" (לָזוּת שְׂפָתַיִם, lazut sefatayim). Guard tongue (James 3:2-12). "Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established. Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil" (4:25-27). **BODILY DISCIPLINE**—eyes straight ahead (focused), feet on established path. "Ponder path" (פַּלֵּס מַעְגַּל רַגְלֶךָ, palles ma'gal raglekha, make level the track). "Turn not right/left" (Deut 5:32; 28:14; Josh 1:7). Single-minded devotion. Whole person (heart, mouth, eyes, feet) aligned with wisdom.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Discipline*

## Chapter 5: Warning Against the Adulteress

#### 5:1-14 — The Lips of the Strange Woman Drip Honey, But Her End Is Bitter as Wormwood
**Lore:** "My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding: That thou mayest regard discretion, and that thy lips may keep knowledge" (5:1-2). Standard opening. "Regard discretion" (לִשְׁמֹר מְזִמּוֹת, lishmor mezimmot, guard prudence). **"For the lips of a strange woman drop as an honeycomb, and her mouth is smoother than oil: But her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a twoedged sword"** (5:3-4). **SEDUCTION WARNING**—"strange woman" (אִשָּׁה זָרָה, ishah zarah, 2:16). "Lips drip honeycomb" (נֹפֶת תִּטֹּפְנָה שִׂפְתֵי זָרָה, nofet titofnah siftei zarah, sweetness). "Smoother than oil" (חָלָק מִשֶּׁמֶן חִכָּהּ, chalaq mishemen chikkah, slippery/flattering). Initial attraction. "But her end" (וְאַחֲרִיתָהּ, ve'acharitah) = "bitter as wormwood" (לַעֲנָה, la'anah, Deut 29:18; Jer 9:15; Amos 5:7), "sharp as twoedged sword" (חֲרֵבָה פִּיּוֹת, charevah fiyyot). Consequences devastating. "Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on hell. Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, that thou canst not know them" (5:5-6). "Feet go to death" (מָוֶת, mavet), "steps to Sheol" (שְׁאוֹל, sheol, 2:18-19; 7:27). "Ways moveable" (נָעוּ, na'u, unstable/wandering). Can't trust her path. "Hear me now therefore, O ye children, and depart not from the words of my mouth. Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house: Lest thou give thine honour unto others, and thy years unto the cruel" (5:7-9). **AVOIDANCE COMMAND**—"remove way far" (הַרְחֵק מֵעָלֶיהָ דַּרְכֶּךָ, harchek me'aleha darkekha, distance yourself). "Not nigh her door" (avoid proximity). "Give honor to others" = loss of reputation. "Years to cruel" = life consumed. "Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth; and thy labours be in the house of a stranger; And thou mourn at the last, when thy flesh and thy body are consumed, And say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof; And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me! I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly" (5:10-14). **CONSEQUENCES**—wealth to strangers, body consumed (STDs?), final regret. "How I hated instruction" = too late repentance. "Almost in all evil" = public disgrace narrowly escaped (or total ruin).

"Drink waters" = sexual satisfaction within marriage (legitimate). "Fountains dispersed" (v.16) = debated: (1) children from marriage flow outward (blessing to community); (2) rhetorical question ("Should your fountains be dispersed?" No!). Context favors exclusivity (v.17). "Only thine own, not strangers'" = sexual exclusivity. **"Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth. Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe; let her breasts satisfy thee at all times; and be thou ravished always with her love"** (5:18-19). **CELEBRATE MARITAL LOVE**—"fountain blessed" (בָּרוּךְ, barukh). "Wife of youth" (אֵשֶׁת נְעוּרֶךָ, eshet ne'urekha, Mal 2:14-15). "Loving hind, pleasant roe" (אַיֶּלֶת אֲהָבִים וְיַעֲלַת־חֵן, ayyelet ahavim veya'alat-chen, graceful deer). "Breasts satisfy" (דַּדֶּיהָ יְרַוֻּךָ, daddeha yerawwukha, Song 7:7-8). "Ravished with love" (תִּשְׁגֶּה, tishgeh, intoxicated/captivated). **HOLY EROTICISM**—marital sex celebrated, not merely tolerated. God-designed delight (Gen 2:24-25; Song of Solomon; 1 Cor 7:3-5; Heb 13:4). "And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger? For the ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD, and he pondereth all his goings. His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sins. "Ways before LORD's eyes" (5:21, omniscience, 15:3; Jer 16:17; Heb 4:13). "Holden with cords of sins" (2:18-19, entanglement). "Die without instruction" (בְּאֵין מוּסָר, be'ein musar) = refuse discipline, perish. Adultery = self-destruction.

## Chapter 6: Warnings Against Surety, Sloth, and Adultery

#### 6:1-5 — If You Have Become Surety for Your Friend, Deliver Yourself Like a Gazelle
**Lore:** "My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger, Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth" (6:1-2). **FINANCIAL WARNING—SURETY**—"surety" (עָרַב, arav, pledge/guarantee for another's debt, 11:15; 17:18; 20:16; 22:26; 27:13). "Stricken hand" (תָּקַעְתָּ כַפֶּךָ, taqa'ta khappekha, handshake sealing deal). "Snared... taken" (יָקַשְׁתָּ... נִלְכַּדְתָּ, yaqashta... nilkadta) = trapped. Rash promises = bondage. "Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend. Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids. Deliver thyself as a roe from the hand of the hunter, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler" (6:3-5). **URGENT ESCAPE**—"deliver thyself" (הִנָּצֵל, hinnatzel, rescue yourself). "Humble thyself" (הִתְרַפֵּס, hitrappes, prostrate yourself, beg release). "No sleep" = urgency. "As roe/bird from hunter" = escape trap immediately. Financial entanglement dangerous (Rom 13:8, "owe no man").
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Surety*

#### 6:6-11 — Go to the Ant, You Sluggard; Consider Her Ways and Be Wise
**Lore:** **"Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise: Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler, Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest"** (6:6-8). **ANTI-SLOTH PROVERB**—"ant" (נְמָלָה, nemalah, only here + 30:25). "Sluggard" (עָצֵל, atzel, lazy person, 10:26; 13:4; 15:19; 19:24; 20:4; 21:25; 22:13; 24:30; 26:13-16). Ant = model of diligence. "No guide/overseer/ruler" (אֵין קָצִין שֹׁטֵר וּמֹשֵׁל, ein qatzin shoter umoshel) = self-motivated. "Provideth in summer, gathers in harvest" = seasonal wisdom, prepares ahead. Nature teaches wisdom (Job 12:7-8; Matt 6:26). "How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep? Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep: So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man" (6:9-11). **SLUGGARD'S EXCUSE**—"How long?" (rhetorical, indefinite delay). "Little sleep, little slumber, little folding hands" = perpetual procrastination (24:33, identical). "Poverty as traveler" (כִּמְהַלֵּךְ, kimehalekh, as one walking, inevitably approaches). "Want as armed man" (כְּאִישׁ מָגֵן, ke'ish magen, as man with shield, invader/robber). Laziness = guaranteed poverty. Diligence required (2 Thess 3:10, "if any would not work, neither should he eat").
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Diligence*

#### 6:12-19 — A Worthless Person Sows Discord; Seven Things the LORD Hates
**Lore:** "A naughty person, a wicked man, walketh with a froward mouth. He winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers; Frowardness is in his heart, he deviseth mischief continually; he soweth discord" (6:12-14). **TROUBLEMAKER PROFILE**—"naughty person" (אָדָם בְּלִיַּעַל, adam beliyya'al, worthless man, Deut 13:13; Judg 19:22; 1 Sam 2:12). "Froward mouth" (עִקְּשׁוּת פֶּה, iqqeshut peh, perverse speech). "Winks... speaks with feet... teaches with fingers" = body language signaling deceit/conspiracy. "Sows discord" (מְדָנִים יְשַׁלֵּחַ, medanim yeshallech, sends out strife). Divisive person. "Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly; suddenly shall he be broken without remedy" (6:15). Sudden judgment for troublemaker. **"These six things doth the LORD hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him"** (6:16). **NUMERICAL SAYING** (x/x+1 pattern, 30:15-31; Amos 1:3ff). "Six... seven" = escalation/completeness. "Hate" (שָׂנֵא, sane), "abomination" (תּוֹעֵבָה, to'evah). List: **(1) proud look** (עֵינַיִם רָמוֹת, einayim ramot, haughty eyes); **(2) lying tongue** (לְשׁוֹן שָׁקֶר, leshon shaqer); **(3) hands shedding innocent blood** (יָדַיִם שֹׁפְכוֹת דָּם־נָקִי, yadayim shofkhot dam-naqi); **(4) heart devising wicked imaginations** (לֵב חֹרֵשׁ מַחְשְׁבוֹת אָוֶן, lev choresh machshevot aven); **(5) feet swift to run to mischief** (רַגְלַיִם מְמַהֲרוֹת לָרוּץ לָרָעָה, raglayim memaharot larutz lara'ah); **(6) false witness** (יָפִיחַ כְּזָבִים עֵד שָׁקֶר, yafiach kezavim ed shaqer); **(7) he that sows discord among brethren** (וּמְשַׁלֵּחַ מְדָנִים בֵּין אַחִים, umshalleach medanim bein achim). Climax = discord (worst social sin). God hates relational destruction.
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Discord*

#### 6:20-35 — Keep Your Father's Command; The Adulteress Hunts for Precious Life
**Lore:** "My son, keep thy father's commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother: Bind them continually upon thine heart, and tie them about thy neck" (6:20-21). Echoes 1:8; 3:3. Parental teaching = protection. "When thou goest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and when thou awakest, it shall talk with thee. For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life" (6:22-23). **LAMP METAPHOR**—"commandment = lamp" (מִצְוָה נֵר, mitzvah ner), "law = light" (תּוֹרָה אוֹר, torah or, Ps 119:105). Guidance day/night. "Reproofs = way of life" (discipline prevents death). "To keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman. Lust not after her beauty in thine heart; neither let her take thee with her eyelids. For by means of a whorish woman a man is brought to a piece of bread: and the adulteress will hunt for the precious life" (6:24-26). **ADULTERESS WARNING (FOURTH)**—"evil woman" (אֵשֶׁת רָע, eshet ra). "Flattery of tongue" (חֶלְקַת לָשׁוֹן, chelqat lashon, smooth speech). "Lust not after beauty" (אַל־תַּחְמֹד יָפְיָהּ, al-tachmod yafeha, Matt 5:28, lust in heart = adultery). "Whorish woman" (אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה, ishah zonah, prostitute) vs. "adulteress" (אֵשֶׁת אִישׁ, eshet ish, another man's wife). Prostitute costs money, adulteress costs life. "Hunt for precious life" (נֶפֶשׁ יְקָרָה, nefesh yeqarah) = soul-hunter. "Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his clothes not be burned? Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be burned? So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife; whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent" (6:27-29). **FIRE METAPHOR**—adultery = playing with fire. Inevitable consequences. "Men do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry; But if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the substance of his house" (6:30-31). Comparison: thief (understandable motive, still punished) vs. adulterer (no excuse). "But whoso committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul. A wound and dishonour shall he get; and his reproach shall not be wiped away. For jealousy is the rage of a man: therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance. He will not regard any ransom; neither will he rest content, though thou givest many gifts" (6:32-35). **ADULTERY'S CONSEQUENCES**—(1) lacks understanding (חֲסַר־לֵב, chasar-lev, senseless); (2) destroys own soul; (3) wound/dishonor; (4) reproach permanent; (5) husband's jealous rage unstoppable (קִנְאָה, qin'ah, Num 5:11-31; Song 8:6); (6) no ransom accepted. Physical + social + spiritual destruction. Exod 20:14; Lev 20:10 = death penalty.

## Chapter 7: The Seduction Scene — A Vivid Warning

#### 7:1-5 — Keep My Commands and Live; Bind Them on Your Fingers
**Lore:** "My son, keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee. Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye. Bind them upon thy fingers, write them upon the table of thine heart" (7:1-3). Standard opening, intensified. "Apple of eye" (אִישׁוֹן עֵינֶךָ, ishon eynekha, pupil, Deut 32:10; Ps 17:8; Zech 2:8). Most precious/protected. "Bind on fingers" (Deut 6:8). "Say unto wisdom, Thou art my sister; and call understanding thy kinswoman: That they may keep thee from the strange woman, from the stranger which flattereth with her words" (7:4-5). **WISDOM AS FAMILY**—"sister" (אֲחֹתִי, achoti, Song 4:9-12; 5:1-2, intimate). "Kinswoman" (מֹדָע, moda, close relative). Wisdom = protective family member. Defense against adulteress.
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Protection*

#### 7:6-27 — The Young Man Void of Understanding: A Vivid Seduction
**Lore:** "For at the window of my house I looked through my casement, And beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, a young man void of understanding, Passing through the street near her corner; and he went the way to her house, In the twilight, in the evening, in the black and dark night" (7:6-9). **NARRATOR AS OBSERVER**—father watches from window. "Simple ones" (פְּתָיִם, petayim, naive). "Void of understanding" (חֲסַר־לֵב, chasar-lev, lacking heart/sense). "Near her corner" = proximity danger (6:27-28). "Twilight... evening... black and dark night" (נֶשֶׁף בְּעֶרֶב יוֹם בְּאִישׁוֹן לַיְלָה וַאֲפֵלָה, neshef be'erev yom be'ishon laylah va'afelah) = progressive darkness (cover of night, deeds of darkness, John 3:19-20; Rom 13:12; Eph 5:11). **"And, behold, there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart. (She is loud and stubborn; her feet abide not in her house: Now is she without, now in the streets, and lieth in wait at every corner.)"** (7:10-12). **ADULTERESS DESCRIBED**—"attire of harlot" (שִׁית זוֹנָה, shit zonah, prostitute's garb). "Subtil of heart" (וּנְצֻרַת לֵב, untzu rat lev, guarded/secretive heart, hidden agenda). "Loud and stubborn" (הֹמִיָּה וְסֹרָרֶת, homiyyah vesoraret, rebellious, Deut 21:18-20). "Feet not in house" = restless, neglects domestic role (1 Tim 5:13; Titus 2:5). Hunts streets/corners. "So she caught him, and kissed him, and with an impudent face said unto him, I have peace offerings with me; this day have I payed my vows. Therefore came I forth to meet thee, diligently to seek thy face, and I have found thee" (7:13-15). **SEDUCTION SPEECH**—"caught and kissed" (aggressive). "Impudent face" (הֵעֵזָה פָנֶיהָ, he'ezah faneha, hardened face, shameless). "Peace offerings" (זִבְחֵי שְׁלָמִים, zivchei shelamim, Lev 3; 7:11-21) = meal from sacrificed animal (religious veneer, twisted piety). "Sought thy face, found thee" = flattery (you're special). "I have decked my bed with coverings of tapestry, with carved works, with fine linen of Egypt. I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning: let us solace ourselves with loves. For the goodman is not at home, he is gone a long journey: He hath taken a bag of money with him, and will come home at the day appointed" (7:16-20). **DETAILED ENTICEMENT**—luxurious bed (Egyptian linen, exotic spices: myrrh מֹר, aloes אֲהָלוֹת, cinnamon קִנָּמוֹן). "Take fill of love" (נִרְוֶה דֹדִים, nirveh dodim, be satiated with lovemaking, euphemism). "Until morning" = all night. "Husband gone" (הָאִישׁ אֵין בְּבֵיתוֹ, ha'ish ein beveito). "Long journey, bag of money, appointed day" = calculated opportunity. Premeditated adultery. "With her much fair speech she caused him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she forced him. He goeth after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as a fool to the correction of the stocks; Till a dart strike through his liver; as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for his life" (7:21-23). **CAPITULATION + DOOM**—"much fair speech" (רֹב לִקְחָהּ, rov liqchah, abundance of persuasion). "Flattering lips forced" (חֶלְקַת שְׂפָתֶיהָ תַּדִּיחֶנּוּ, chelqat sefateha taddichennu, smooth lips compelled). **THREE ANIMAL METAPHORS**: (1) ox to slaughter (unaware); (2) fool to stocks (punishment); (3) bird to snare (trapped). "Dart through liver" (חֵץ יְפַלַּח כְּבֵדוֹ, chetz yefallach khevedo) = fatal wound. "Knoweth not it's for his life" = oblivious to mortal danger. **"Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children, and attend to the words of my mouth. Let not thine heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths. For she hath cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her. Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death"** (7:24-27). **FINAL WARNING**—"heart not decline" (אַל־יֵשְׂטְ לִבְּךָ, al-yeset libekha). "Cast down many wounded" (רַבִּים חֲלָלִים הִפִּילָה, rabbim chalalim hippilah, many slain she felled). "Strong men slain" (וַעֲצֻמִים כָּל־הֲרֻגֶיהָ, va'atzumim kol-harugeha, mighty ones killed). Body count high. "House = way to Sheol" (דַּרְכֵי שְׁאוֹל בֵּיתָהּ, darkhei sheol beitah, 2:18; 5:5; 9:18). "Chambers of death" (חַדְרֵי־מָוֶת, chadrei-mavet, inner rooms of death). Most vivid, extended warning against adultery in Scripture.

## Chapter 8: Wisdom's Self-Commendation — Present at Creation

#### 8:1-11 — Does Not Wisdom Cry? Her Fruit Is Better Than Gold
**Lore:** "Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding put forth her voice? She standeth in the top of high places, by the way in the places of the paths. She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors" (8:1-3). **WISDOM'S SECOND CALL** (cf. 1:20-21). Public, prominent locations. Wisdom = accessible, not hidden. "Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man. O ye simple, understand wisdom: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart" (8:4-5). Universal call. "Simple" (פְּתָיִם, petayim), "fools" (כְּסִילִים, kesilim) addressed. Wisdom available to all. "Hear; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things. For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips. All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them" (8:6-8). **WISDOM'S CREDENTIALS**—"excellent things" (נְגִידִים, negidim, noble things). "Truth" (אֱמֶת, emet). "Righteousness" (צֶדֶק, tzedeq). "Nothing froward/perverse" (no deceit, vs. adulteress 7:21). "They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge. Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold. For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it" (8:9-11). Echoes 3:13-15. Wisdom > wealth. "Plain to him that understands" (נְכֹחִים לַמֵּבִין, nekho chim lamevin) = straightforward once eyes opened.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Value*

#### 8:12-21 — I, Wisdom, Dwell With Prudence; By Me Kings Reign
**Lore:** "I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions. The fear of the LORD is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate" (8:12-13). **WISDOM'S CHARACTER**—"dwell with prudence" (שָׁכַנְתִּי עָרְמָה, shakhanti ormah). "Fear of LORD = hate evil" (cf. 1:7, foundation). Hates pride, arrogance, evil, perverse speech. "Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength. By me kings reign, and princes decree justice. By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth" (8:14-16). **POLITICAL AUTHORITY FROM WISDOM**—"counsel... sound wisdom... understanding... strength" (Wisdom's attributes). "By me kings reign" (בִּי מְלָכִים יִמְלֹכוּ, bi melakhim yimlokhu, Dan 2:21; Rom 13:1). Legitimate government grounded in divine wisdom. "I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me. Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness. My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver. I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment: That I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasures" (8:17-21). **RECIPROCAL LOVE**—"love them that love me" (אֹהֲבַי, ohavai). "Seek early = find" (promise, Jer 29:13; Matt 7:7). "Durable riches" (עֹשֶׁר עָתָק, osher ataq, enduring wealth, not temporary). "Inherit substance" (יֵשׁ, yesh, real being/existence, true riches). Wisdom gives eternal treasure (Matt 6:19-20).
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Authority*

#### 8:22-31 — The LORD Possessed Me in the Beginning; I Was Brought Forth Before the Mountains
**Lore:** **"The LORD possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old. I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was"** (8:22-23). **WISDOM'S ETERNAL PRE-EXISTENCE**—"LORD possessed me" (יְהוָה קָנָנִי, YHWH qanani, acquired/possessed, or "created"?). Debated: Arians used this for Christ as created being. Orthodox: "possessed/had" (eternal relationship, not temporal creation). "Beginning of his way" (רֵאשִׁית דַּרְכּוֹ, reshit darko, first of His works, Mic 5:2). "Set up from everlasting" (מֵעוֹלָם נִסַּכְתִּי, me'olam nissakti, from eternity I was installed). "When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when there were no fountains abounding with water. Before the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth" (8:24-25). **BEFORE CREATION**—"brought forth" (חוֹלָלְתִּי, cholalti, birthed/born, used twice). Poetic personification or literal pre-existence? Christological reading: Christ = Wisdom, eternally begotten (John 1:1-3, "In beginning was Word... Word was with God"). "While as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the highest part of the dust of the world. When he prepared the heavens, I was there: when he set a compass upon the face of the depth: When he established the clouds above: when he strengthened the fountains of the deep: When he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment: when he appointed the foundations of the earth" (8:26-29). **PRESENT AT CREATION**—"I was there" (שָׁם אָנִי, sham ani, eyewitness). Creation details match Gen 1; Job 38. Wisdom = God's co-worker? Or poetic description of God's wisdom at work? **"Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him; Rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth; and my delights were with the sons of men"** (8:30-31). **MASTER WORKMAN**—"by him" (אֶצְלוֹ, etzlo, beside Him). "As one brought up" (כְּאָמוֹן, ke'amon) = debated: "master workman/architect" (LXX, harmonizing, ἁρμόζουσα) or "little child/nursling" (playful intimacy). "Daily his delight" (שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים, sha'ashu'im, delights/pleasures). "Rejoicing always" (מְשַׂחֶקֶת, mesacheket, playing/sporting). "Delights with sons of men" = wisdom's affection toward humanity. **Christological fulfillment**: Col 1:15-17, Christ = image of invisible God, firstborn over creation, by Him all things created; John 1:3, "all things made through Him"; Heb 1:2, "through whom He made worlds." Wisdom = pre-incarnate Christ.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Creation*

#### 8:32-36 — Blessed Is the Man Who Hears Me; He Who Sins Against Me Wrongs His Own Soul
**Lore:** "Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways. Hear instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not. Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors" (8:32-34). **EXHORTATION**—"blessed" (אַשְׁרֵי, ashrei, 3:13, 18). "Keep my ways" = obedience. "Watching daily at gates" = eager student waiting at Wisdom's house. Devotion imagery. "For whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD. But he that sinneth against me wrongeth his own soul: all they that hate me love death" (8:35-36). **STARK CHOICE**—find Wisdom = find life + LORD's favor. Sin against Wisdom = wrong own soul (self-destruction). "Hate me = love death" (אֹהֲבֵי מָוֶת, ohavei mavet, choose death, Deut 30:19). Two ways climax. Wisdom = life itself (John 14:6, Christ = way, truth, life).
*Verses: 5 | Theme: Life*

## Chapter 9: The Two Banquets — Wisdom vs. Folly

#### 9:1-6 — Wisdom Has Built Her House and Hewn Out Her Seven Pillars
**Lore:** **"Wisdom hath builded her house, she hath hewn out her seven pillars"** (9:1). **WISDOM'S HOUSE**—"built house" (בָּנְתָה בֵיתָהּ, banetah veitah). "Hewn seven pillars" (חָצְבָה עַמּוּדֶיהָ שִׁבְעָה, chatzevah ammudeh a shiv'ah). Seven = completeness/perfection (Gen 2:2; Rev 1:4). Pillars = structural stability. **Memory Palace**: Seven pillars = seven collections of Proverbs? Symbolic house of wisdom. "She hath killed her beasts; she hath mingled her wine; she hath also furnished her table. She hath sent forth her maidens: she crieth upon the highest places of the city" (9:2-3). **BANQUET PREPARED**—"killed beasts" (slaughtered animals, feast). "Mingled wine" (מָסְכָה יֵינָהּ, maskhah yenah, mixed with spices, Ps 75:8; Isa 5:22). "Furnished table" (עָרְכָה שֻׁלְחָנָהּ, arkhah shulchanah, set table). "Sent maidens" (servants invite guests). "Highest places" (public announcement, 8:2). "Whoso is simple, let him turn in hither: as for him that wanteth understanding, she saith to him, Come, eat of my bread, and drink of the wine which I have mingled. Forsake the foolish, and live; and go in the way of understanding" (9:4-6). **INVITATION**—"simple" (פֶּתִי, peti), "wanteth understanding" (חֲסַר־לֵב, chasar-lev, same as 7:7). "Turn in hither" (יָסֻר הֵנָּה, yasur hennah). "Eat bread, drink wine" = covenant meal, fellowship (Isa 55:1-3; Matt 22:1-14, wedding feast; Luke 14:16-24; John 6:35, Christ = bread of life; Rev 19:9, marriage supper). "Forsake foolish, live" = leave old companions, gain life.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Invitation*

#### 9:7-12 — He Who Corrects a Scoffer Gets Shame; Give Instruction to a Wise Man
**Lore:** "He that reproveth a scorner getteth to himself shame: and he that rebuketh a wicked man getteth himself a blot. Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee. Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be yet wiser: teach a just man, and he will increase in learning" (9:7-9). **TEACHABILITY**—"scorner" (לֵץ, letz, 1:22) vs. "wise man" (חָכָם, chakham). Scorner hates correction, wise man loves it. Defining characteristic of wisdom: receives reproof (10:17; 12:1; 13:18; 15:5, 10, 12, 31-32; 19:20, 25; 21:11; 25:12; 27:5-6; 28:23). Fool unteachable. **"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding"** (9:10). **BOOK'S MOTTO REPEATED** (1:7). "Beginning of wisdom" (תְּחִלַּת חָכְמָה, techillat chokmah, start/foundation). "Knowledge of the holy" (דַּעַת קְדֹשִׁים, da'at qedoshim, knowing the Holy One/holy things). Inclusio within Ch 1-9 (1:7 → 9:10, brackets opening section). "For by me thy days shall be multiplied, and the years of thy life shall be increased. If thou be wise, thou shalt be wise for thyself: but if thou scornest, thou alone shalt bear it" (9:11-12). Wisdom benefits self, folly harms self. Personal responsibility.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Teachability*

#### 9:13-18 — The Foolish Woman Is Clamorous; Her Guests Are in the Depths of Hell
**Lore:** "A foolish woman is clamorous: she is simple, and knoweth nothing. For she sitteth at the door of her house, on a seat in the high places of the city, To call passengers who go right on their ways" (9:13-15). **DAME FOLLY INTRODUCED**—"foolish woman" (אֵשֶׁת כְּסִילוּת, eshet kesilut, Woman Folly). "Clamorous" (הֹמִיָּה, homiyyah, loud/boisterous, 7:11). "Simple, knows nothing" (פְּתַיּוּת וּבַל־יָדְעָה מָּה, petayyut uval-yad'ah mah, naive + ignorant). Sits at door (mimics Wisdom 8:34, but passively waiting vs. actively inviting). "High places" (same location as Wisdom 9:3, 14, direct competition). "Passengers going right on ways" = she targets those ALREADY on right path (tries to seduce away from Wisdom). **"Whoso is simple, let him turn in hither: and as for him that wanteth understanding, she saith to him, Stolen waters are sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant"** (9:16-17). **COUNTERFEIT INVITATION**—identical opening to Wisdom's call (9:4, 16). But offer different: "Stolen waters sweet" (מַיִם־גְּנוּבִים יִמְתָּקוּ, mayim-genuvim yimtaqu), "bread in secret pleasant" (וְלֶחֶם סְתָרִים יִנְעָם, velechem setarim yin'am). Forbidden = alluring (illicit sex, 5:15-20; 7:18). Temptation = perversion of good (theft of water vs. legitimate drink; secret bread vs. open banquet). Satan's tactic: counterfeit. "But he knoweth not that the dead are there; and that her guests are in the depths of hell" (9:18). **REALITY**: "dead there" (רְפָאִים שָׁם, refa'im sham, shades, 2:18). "Guests in depths of Sheol" (בְּעִמְקֵי שְׁאוֹל קְרֻאֶיהָ, be'imqei sheol qeru'eha). Folly's house = death (5:5; 7:27). **CLIMAX OF CHAPTERS 1-9**: Two women, two houses, two invitations, two destinies (life vs. death). Reader must choose. **Transition**: Ch 1-9 = WHY choose wisdom (motivation, worldview). Ch 10-31 = WHAT wisdom looks like (application, specific proverbs). Foundation laid, now build.
*Verses: 6 | Theme: Folly*
