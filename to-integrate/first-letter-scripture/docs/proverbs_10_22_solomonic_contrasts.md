# Proverbs 10:1-22:16: PrMP 2 — The Solomonic Contrasts: The Righteous vs. The Wicked

## Overview
**THE CORE COLLECTION** of Proverbs—375 individual sayings (10:1-22:16) forming the "treasury of practical wisdom" that most people associate with the book's title. **Superscription**: "The proverbs of Solomon" (10:1). **Literary form**: Sharp, antithetical parallelism (A vs. B structure) dominates chapters 10-15, creating forcible contrasts between two lifestyles. **Thematic clusters**: Though individual proverbs appear somewhat random, recurring themes emerge: righteous vs. wicked, wise vs. fool, diligent vs. lazy, wealth vs. poverty, speech (tongue/lips/mouth), family relationships, divine sovereignty. **Mnemonic strategy**: Group by theme rather than strict chapter divisions to aid retention. **Pedagogical purpose**: Apply the worldview (fear of LORD from Ch 1-9) to daily decisions—speech, work, money, relationships, justice. **Transition from PrMP 1**: Chapters 1-9 = WHY wisdom (theological foundation, dramatic motivation). Chapters 10-22:16 = WHAT wisdom looks like (specific, actionable guidance). **Canonical significance**: "Spiritual common sense"—not secular pragmatism but God's revealed instruction for godly living in fallen world. Central themes: Two ways (life/death paths continued), retribution (eventual justice), divine oversight (LORD sees all), speech ethics, economic wisdom, family honor, social justice.

---

**Note on Organization**: Due to the collection's massive size (374 verses) and non-narrative structure, pericopes are organized thematically within chapters rather than forcing artificial narrative breaks. Each pericope maintains 6-12 verse target while grouping topically related proverbs.

## Chapters 10-11: Foundation - The Righteous vs. The Wicked

#### 10:1-10 — A Wise Son Makes a Glad Father; The Mouth of the Wicked Covers Violence
**Lore:** **"The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish son is the heaviness of his mother"** (10:1). **SUPERSCRIPTION** + **FIRST PROVERB**—"Proverbs of Solomon" (מִשְׁלֵי שְׁלֹמֹה, mishlei Shelomoh). New section begins (distinct from 1:1). "Wise son" (בֵּן חָכָם, ben chakham) vs. "foolish son" (בֵּן כְּסִיל, ben kesil). Family theme = launching point (parent's joy/sorrow tied to child's choices). "Treasures of wickedness profit nothing: but righteousness delivereth from death" (10:2). Ill-gotten wealth = no lasting value. "Righteousness delivers from death" (צְדָקָה תַּצִּיל מִמָּוֶת, tzedaqah tatzil mimmavet, 11:4; cf. Dan 4:27; Matt 6:19-20). "The LORD will not suffer the soul of the righteous to famish: but he casteth away the substance of the wicked" (10:3). Divine provision guaranteed for righteous. Wicked's wealth = rejected. "He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich" (10:4). **DILIGENCE THEME**—"slack hand" (כַּף רְמִיָּה, kaph remiyyah, deceitful/lazy palm) vs. "diligent hand" (יַד חָרוּצִים, yad charutzim). Work ethic determines economic outcome. "He that gathereth in summer is a wise son: but he that sleepeth in harvest is a son that causeth shame" (10:5). Seasonal wisdom—work when opportunity available (ant in 6:6-8). Laziness during harvest = family disgrace. "Blessings are upon the head of the just: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked. The memory of the just is blessed: but the name of the wicked shall rot" (10:6-7). Contrasts: blessings vs. violence, blessed memory vs. rotting name. Legacy matters. "The wise in heart will receive commandments: but a prating fool shall fall" (10:8). Teachability vs. talkativeness. "Prating fool" (אֱוִיל שְׂפָתַיִם, ewil sefatayim, fool of lips). "He that walketh uprightly walketh surely: but he that perverteth his ways shall be known. He that winketh with the eye causeth sorrow: but a prating fool shall fall" (10:9-10). Integrity = security. "Winks eye" (קֹרֵץ עַיִן, qoretz ayin, signals deceit, 6:13). **Themes established**: Family, wealth/poverty, diligence/sloth, speech, righteousness/wickedness.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Contrasts*

#### 10:11-21 — The Mouth of the Righteous Is a Well of Life; Hatred Stirs Up Strifes

**Lore:** "The mouth of a righteous man is a well of life: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked" (10:11). **SPEECH FOCUS**—"well of life" (מְקוֹר חַיִּים, meqor chayyim, fountain, 13:14; 14:27; 16:22). Righteous speech life-giving (vs. wicked's violence-concealing words). "Hatred stirreth up strifes: but love covereth all sins" (10:12). Quoted 1 Pet 4:8, James 5:20. "Hatred" (שִׂנְאָה, sin'ah) creates conflict. "Love covers" (אַהֲבָה תְּכַסֶּה, ahavah tekhasseh) = forgiveness (not enabling sin, but not publicizing offenses). "In the lips of him that hath understanding wisdom is found: but a rod is for the back of him that is void of understanding" (10:13). Wise lips vs. foolish back (needs discipline). "Wise men lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the foolish is near destruction" (10:14). Store wisdom silently vs. speak foolishly (self-destruction). "The rich man's wealth is his strong city: the destruction of the poor is their poverty" (10:15). Wealth = protection (pragmatic observation, not moral endorsement of riches, cf. 18:11; Luke 12:16-21, rich fool). "The labour of the righteous tendeth to life: the fruit of the wicked to sin" (10:16). Outcome differs: righteousness → life, wickedness → sin. "He is in the way of life that keepeth instruction: but he that refuseth reproof erreth" (10:17). Accepting correction = path to life (12:1; 13:18; 15:5, 10, 31-32). "He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool" (10:18). Two speech sins: (1) concealed hatred + false pleasantness (hypocrisy); (2) open slander. Both = folly. "In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin: but he that refraineth his lips is wise" (10:19). **CRITICAL VERSE**—verbosity invites sin. Restraint = wisdom (17:27-28; Eccl 5:2-3; James 1:19, "swift to hear, slow to speak"). "The tongue of the just is as choice silver: the heart of the wicked is little worth. The lips of the righteous feed many: but fools die for want of wisdom" (10:20-21). Righteous speech valuable (silver), feeds many (edifies community). Fools starve intellectually/spiritually.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Speech*

#### 10:22-32 — The Blessing of the LORD Makes Rich; The Fear of the Wicked Shall Come Upon Him
**Lore:** **"The blessing of the LORD, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow with it"** (10:22). **DIVINE PROSPERITY**—"blessing of LORD" (בִּרְכַּת יְהוָה, birkat YHWH) = source of true wealth. "Adds no sorrow" (לֹא־יוֹסִיף עֶצֶב עִמָּהּ, lo-yosif etzev immah) = guilt-free, curse-free (vs. 15:27; 1 Tim 6:9-10). "It is as sport to a fool to do mischief: but a man of understanding hath wisdom" (10:23). Fool enjoys evil (depravity as entertainment). Wise man has wisdom (tautology emphasizing contrast). "The fear of the wicked, it shall come upon him: but the desire of the righteous shall be granted" (10:24). **SELF-FULFILLING CONSEQUENCES**—wicked's fears realized (Job 3:25, "thing I feared has come upon me"). Righteous's desires granted (Ps 37:4; Matt 6:33). "As the whirlwind passeth, so is the wicked no more: but the righteous is an everlasting foundation" (10:25). Wicked = temporary (swept away). Righteous = permanent foundation (Matt 7:24-27, house on rock). "As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that send him" (10:26). **SLUGGARD METAPHOR**—irritant/ineffective. Unreliable employee. "The fear of the LORD prolongeth days: but the years of the wicked shall be shortened" (10:27). Long life for righteous, shortened for wicked (general principle, exceptions exist: Ps 73:3-12; Eccl 7:15). "The hope of the righteous shall be gladness: but the expectation of the wicked shall perish" (10:28). Contrasting futures. "The way of the LORD is strength to the upright: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity" (10:29). LORD's way = fortress for righteous, ruin for wicked. "The righteous shall never be removed: but the wicked shall not inhabit the earth" (10:30). Land tenure theme (Ps 37:9, 11, 22, 29; Matt 5:5). "The mouth of the just bringeth forth wisdom: but the froward tongue shall be cut out. The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable: but the mouth of the wicked speaketh frowardness" (10:31-32). Speech theme returns. Righteous = wisdom/acceptable. Wicked = perverse (tongue cut out, Ps 12:3).
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Blessing*

#### 11:1-8 — A False Balance Is Abomination to the LORD; When Pride Comes, Then Comes Shame
**Lore:** **"A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight"** (11:1). **ECONOMIC JUSTICE**—"false balance" (מֹאזְנֵי מִרְמָה, mo'znei mirmah, deceptive scales, Lev 19:36; Deut 25:13-16; Amos 8:5; Mic 6:11). "Abomination" (תּוֹעֲבַת, to'avat) = strong covenant term (detestable to YHWH). "Just weight" (אֶבֶן שְׁלֵמָה, even shelemah, perfect stone) = God's delight. Commerce = moral arena. "When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom" (11:2). **PRIDE PRINCIPLE**—pride (זָדוֹן, zadon, arrogance) precedes disgrace (16:18; 18:12; Dan 4:30-37, Nebuchadnezzar). Humility (צְנוּעִים, tzenu'im, modest ones) accompanies wisdom. "The integrity of the upright shall guide them: but the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them" (11:3). Integrity (תֻּמָּה, tummah, completeness) = reliable guide. Crookedness self-destructs. "Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death" (11:4). **ESCHATOLOGICAL WARNING**—wealth useless in judgment (Zeph 1:18; Ezek 7:19; Luke 12:20). Only righteousness saves (צְדָקָה, tzedaqah, 10:2). "The righteousness of the perfect shall direct his way: but the wicked shall fall by his own wickedness. The righteousness of the upright shall deliver them: but transgressors shall be taken in their own naughtiness" (11:5-6). Self-directed outcomes: righteousness guides/delivers, wickedness destroys/traps. "When a wicked man dieth, his expectation shall perish: and the hope of unjust men perisheth" (11:7). Death ends wicked's hope (vs. righteous, Ps 49:15; 73:24). "The righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked cometh in his stead" (11:8). Substitution pattern (Est 7:10, Haman on Mordecai's gallows; Dan 6:24, accusers to lions' den).
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Justice*

#### 11:9-16 — An Hypocrite with His Mouth Destroys His Neighbor; A Gracious Woman Retains Honor
**Lore:** "An hypocrite with his mouth destroyeth his neighbour: but through knowledge shall the just be delivered" (11:9). "Hypocrite" (חָנֵף, chaneph, godless/profane person) uses speech as weapon. Knowledge protects righteous. "When it goeth well with the righteous, the city rejoiceth: and when the wicked perish, there is shouting" (11:10). Community celebrates righteous prosperity, wicked's downfall (29:2; Est 8:15). "By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted: but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked" (11:11). Righteous bless cities (Gen 18:26-32, Abraham's intercession for Sodom). Wicked speech ruins. "He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding holdeth his peace" (11:12). Fool mocks neighbor. Wise man stays silent (restraint). "A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter" (11:13). **GOSSIP WARNING**—"talebearer" (רָכִיל, rakhil, slanderer, Lev 19:16). Faithful spirit (נֶאֱמַן־רוּחַ, ne'eman-ruach) keeps confidences. "Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety" (11:14). **LEADERSHIP PRINCIPLE**—"counsel" (תַּחְבֻּלוֹת, tachbulot, guidance/strategy, 20:18; 24:6). Multiple advisors = safety (15:22). "He that is surety for a stranger shall smart for it: and he that hateth suretiship is sure" (11:15). **DEBT WARNING** (repeated 6:1-5; 17:18; 20:16; 22:26-27; 27:13). Co-signing loans = financial danger. "A gracious woman retaineth honour: and strong men retain riches" (11:16). Gracious woman (אֵשֶׁת־חֵן, eshet-chen, woman of grace/charm) = honor. Contrast: strong men retain only riches (not honor).
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Community*

#### 11:17-23 — The Merciful Man Does Good to His Own Soul; The Desire of the Righteous Is Only Good
**Lore:** "The merciful man doeth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel troubleth his own flesh" (11:17). **SELF-IMPACT**—kindness (חֶסֶד, chesed) benefits self. Cruelty harms self (Matt 5:7; 25:40). "The wicked worketh a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness shall be a sure reward" (11:18). Deceit = unstable income. Righteousness sowing = certain reward (Gal 6:7-9). "As righteousness tendeth to life: so he that pursueth evil pursueth it to his own death" (11:19). Two paths (Deut 30:15-20). "They that are of a froward heart are abomination to the LORD: but such as are upright in their way are his delight" (11:20). "Froward heart" (עִקְּשֵׁי־לֵב, iqqeshei-lev, twisted heart) = abomination. Upright = delight (שָׁלֵם, shalem, blameless). "Though hand join in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished: but the seed of the righteous shall be delivered" (11:21). "Hand join in hand" = conspiracy/oath (wicked confederacy doesn't escape judgment). Righteous offspring protected. "As a jewel of gold in a swine's snout, so is a fair woman which is without discretion" (11:22). **MEMORABLE SIMILE**—beauty without wisdom = absurdity (like golden nose ring on pig). "Discretion" (טַעַם, ta'am, taste/judgment). External beauty valueless without character. "The desire of the righteous is only good: but the expectation of the wicked is wrath" (11:23). Righteous desire good outcomes. Wicked expect/receive wrath.
*Verses: 7 | Theme: Character*

#### 11:24-31 — There Is That Scatters, and Yet Increases; He That Wins Souls Is Wise
**Lore:** **"There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty"** (11:24). **GENEROSITY PARADOX**—giving leads to increase (2 Cor 9:6-11; Luke 6:38). Hoarding leads to loss. "The liberal soul shall be made fat: and he that watereth shall be watered also himself" (11:25). "Liberal soul" (נֶפֶשׁ־בְּרָכָה, nefesh-berakhah, soul of blessing) prospers. Water giver receives water (reciprocity principle). "He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him: but blessing shall be upon the head of him that selleth it" (11:26). Grain hoarding during famine = cursed (creating artificial scarcity for profit, Amos 8:4-6). Fair selling = blessed. "He that diligently seeketh good procureth favour: but he that seeketh mischief, it shall come unto him" (11:27). Seek good → find favor. Seek evil → find evil (self-fulfilling). "He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the righteous shall flourish as a branch" (11:28). Wealth trust = unstable (Ps 52:7; Mark 10:24; 1 Tim 6:17). Righteous = flourishing tree (Ps 1:3; Jer 17:7-8). "He that troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: and the fool shall be servant to the wise of heart" (11:29). Family troublemaker inherits nothing (wind = emptiness, Eccl 5:16). Reversal: fool serves wise. "The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls is wise" (11:30). **EVANGELISM VERSE**—righteous produces life (Gen 2:9; 3:22; Rev 2:7; 22:2, 14). "Wins souls" (לֹקֵחַ נְפָשׁוֹת, loqeach nefashot, takes/captures souls) = brings to wisdom/life. Dan 12:3, "turn many to righteousness." "Behold, the righteous shall be recompensed in the earth: much more the wicked and the sinner" (11:31). Quoted 1 Pet 4:18. If righteous punished for sins (chastisement), how much more wicked (judgment)?
*Verses: 8 | Theme: Generosity*

## Chapter 12: Discipline and Speech

#### 12:1-9 — Whoso Loves Instruction Loves Knowledge; A Virtuous Woman Is a Crown to Her Husband
**Lore:** **"Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is brutish"** (12:1). **TEACHABILITY TEST**—loving correction = loving knowledge. Hating rebuke = "brutish" (בַּעַר, ba'ar, stupid as cattle, Ps 73:22). Discipline separates wise from fools (theme throughout Proverbs). "A good man obtaineth favour of the LORD: but a man of wicked devices will he condemn" (12:2). Good (טוֹב, tov) obtains LORD's favor. Schemer (מְזִמּוֹת, mezimmot, evil plans) condemned. "A man shall not be established by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall not be moved" (12:3). Wickedness = unstable foundation. Righteousness = immovable root (tree metaphor, Ps 1:3). "A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed is as rottenness in his bones" (12:4). **VIRTUOUS WOMAN** (אֵשֶׁת־חַיִל, eshet-chayil, woman of valor, 31:10-31). Crown vs. bone rot (honor vs. decay). "The thoughts of the righteous are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceit" (12:5). Internal contrast: righteous think truth, wicked plan lies. "The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them" (12:6). Wicked speech = ambush. Upright speech = deliverance. "The wicked are overthrown, and are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand" (12:7). Permanence theme (10:25, 30). "A man shall be commended according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised" (12:8). Public reputation matches character. "He that is despised, and hath a servant, is better than he that honoureth himself, and lacketh bread" (12:9). Better humble with resources than arrogant without (substance over image).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Discipline*

#### 12:10-18 — A Righteous Man Regards the Life of His Beast; Lying Lips Are Abomination to the LORD
**Lore:** "A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel" (12:10). **ANIMAL WELFARE**—"regards life" (יוֹדֵעַ נֶפֶשׁ, yodea nefesh, knows soul/needs of animal, Deut 25:4). Even wicked's "mercies" are cruel. Righteousness extends to all creatures. "He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain persons is void of understanding" (12:11). Diligent farming = abundance. Chasing fantasies = poverty (28:19). "The wicked desireth the net of evil men: but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit" (12:12). Wicked covet others' ill-gotten gains. Righteous produce own fruit legitimately. "The wicked is snared by the transgression of his lips: but the just shall come out of trouble" (12:13). **SPEECH TRAP**—words ensnare wicked (18:7). Righteous escape. "A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth: and the recompence of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him" (12:14). Words + works = dual reward (18:20). "The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise" (12:15). **UNTEACHABILITY**—fool = self-justified (3:7; 26:12; 28:26). Wise = counsel-receiver. "A fool's wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame" (12:16). Fool = quick-tempered (displays anger immediately, 29:11). Prudent overlooks insults. "He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit" (12:17). Truth-telling = righteousness. False testimony = deceit (14:5, 25; 19:5, 9). "There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise is health" (12:18). **SPEECH POWER**—reckless words wound. Wise words heal (15:4; 16:24; 25:11).
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Speech*

#### 12:19-28 — The Lip of Truth Shall Be Established Forever; In the Way of Righteousness Is Life
**Lore:** **"The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment"** (12:19). **PERMANENCE OF TRUTH**—truth endures eternally. Lies temporary (Ps 117:2). "Deceit is in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors of peace is joy" (12:20). Evil imaginers harbor deceit. Peacemakers find joy (Matt 5:9). "There shall no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief" (12:21). Divine protection principle (Ps 91; Rom 8:28, "all things work for good"). Wicked filled with trouble. "Lying lips are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight" (12:22). "Abomination" (תּוֹעֵבָה, to'evah, 6:16-19; 11:1). Truth-dealers = God's delight. "A prudent man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness" (12:23). Wise = restrained (doesn't display all knowledge). Fool broadcasts folly. "The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute" (12:24). **LEADERSHIP EARNED**—diligence leads to authority. Laziness leads to servitude (forced labor/taxes). "Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad" (12:25). **ENCOURAGEMENT**—anxiety weighs down (דְּאָגָה, de'agah, worry). Kind word lifts up (15:23; 16:24; 25:11). "The righteous is more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked seduceth them" (12:26). Righteous = careful guide to neighbors. Wicked's way misleads. "The slothful man roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man is precious" (12:27). Lazy doesn't finish tasks (catches game but won't cook it). Diligent values possessions. "In the way of righteousness is life; and in the pathway thereof there is no death" (12:28). **CLIMACTIC VERSE**—righteousness path = life (eternal life foreshadowed, John 11:25-26).
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Truth*

## Chapter 13: Wisdom, Wealth, and Consequences

#### 13:1-10 — A Wise Son Hears His Father's Instruction; Only by Pride Comes Contention
**Lore:** "A wise son heareth his father's instruction: but a scorner heareth not rebuke" (13:1). **FAMILY INSTRUCTION**—wise son teachable (10:1; 15:5). "Scorner" (לֵץ, letz, mocker) unteachable (9:7-8; 15:12). "A man shall eat good by the fruit of his mouth: but the soul of the transgressors shall eat violence" (13:2). Speech determines diet: good words → good outcomes; treacherous words → violence consumed. "He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction" (13:3). **SPEECH RESTRAINT** (10:19; 21:23; James 1:19). Guarded mouth preserves life. "The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat" (13:4). **LAZINESS PARADOX**—sluggard wants but won't work (result: nothing). Diligent prospers. "A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame" (13:5). Righteous abhors falsehood. Wicked causes disgust. "Righteousness keepeth him that is upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner" (13:6). Righteousness = guardian. Wickedness = destroyer. "There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches" (13:7). **WEALTH PARADOX**—appearance vs. reality (cf. 2 Cor 6:10, "poor yet making many rich"; Rev 3:17, Laodicea). "The ransom of a man's life are his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke" (13:8). Rich can pay ransom (buy freedom). Poor ignored (can't be extorted, mixed blessing). "The light of the righteous rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out" (13:9). Light = life/prosperity (20:20; 24:20; Job 18:5-6; 21:17). Wicked's lamp extinguished. "Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom" (13:10). **PRIDE = STRIFE SOURCE** (28:25). Humility + counsel = wisdom.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Wisdom*

#### 13:11-20 — Wealth Gotten by Vanity Shall Be Diminished; He That Walks with Wise Men Shall Be Wise
**Lore:** "Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase" (13:11). **HONEST WEALTH**—"by vanity" (מֵהֶבֶל, mehevel, from vapor/deception, quick schemes) decreases. Gradual labor increases (20:21; 21:5). "Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life" (13:12). **LONGING DELAYED**—prolonged waiting = heartsickness. Fulfilled desire = life tree (healing, 3:18). "Whoso despiseth the word shall be destroyed: but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded" (13:13). Despise instruction → destruction. Fear/revere command → reward (1:7, fear of LORD). "The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death" (13:14). **TORAH AS LIFE** (תּוֹרַת חָכָם, torat chakham, instruction of wise) = fountain (10:11; 14:27; 16:22). Avoids death traps. "Good understanding giveth favour: but the way of transgressors is hard" (13:15). Good sense gains favor. Treachery = harsh path (rough/permanent ruin). "Every prudent man dealeth with knowledge: but a fool layeth open his folly" (13:16). Prudent acts knowledgeably. Fool displays stupidity openly. "A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health" (13:17). Unreliable messenger causes disaster. Trustworthy envoy brings healing. "Poverty and shame shall be to him that refuseth instruction: but he that regardeth reproof shall be honoured" (13:18). Reject correction → poverty + disgrace. Accept rebuke → honor (15:31-32). "The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but it is abomination to fools to depart from evil" (13:19). Fulfilled desire = sweet. Fools detest leaving evil (perverse values). "He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed" (13:20). **ASSOCIATION PRINCIPLE**—companions shape character (1 Cor 15:33, "bad company corrupts"). Choose wisely.
*Verses: 10 | Theme: Wealth*

#### 13:21-25 — Evil Pursues Sinners; A Good Man Leaves an Inheritance to His Children's Children

"Even a fool, when he holdeth his peace, is counted wise: and he that shutteth his lips is esteemed a man of understanding" (17:28). **SILENCE = WISDOM APPEARANCE**—even fool seems wise if silent (better to remain silent and thought fool than speak and remove doubt, cf. Job 13:5).

## Chapter 18: Words, Wealth, and Wise Decision-Making

#### 18:1-12 — A Fool Has No Delight in Understanding; Before Destruction the Heart of Man Is Haughty
**Lore:** "Through desire a man, having separated himself, seeketh and intermeddleth with all wisdom" (18:1). **INTERPRETIVE CHALLENGE**—either: (1) seeker isolates to pursue wisdom, OR (2) isolationist selfishly rejects wisdom. Context suggests #2: self-willed isolation vs. community wisdom. "A fool hath no delight in understanding, but that his heart may discover itself" (18:2). **FOOL'S SPEECH**—no interest in understanding, only expressing opinions (modern social media epitomized). "When the wicked cometh, then cometh also contempt, and with ignominy reproach" (18:3). Wicked brings scorn + disgrace. "The words of a man's mouth are as deep waters, and the wellspring of wisdom as a flowing brook" (18:4). **SPEECH DEPTH**—wise words = deep waters (profound). Wisdom source = flowing stream (continual, fresh). "It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to overthrow the righteous in judgment" (18:5). **JUDICIAL PARTIALITY**—favoring wicked to condemn righteous = evil (17:15; Ex 23:2-3). "A fool's lips enter into contention, and his mouth calleth for strokes" (18:6). **FOOL INVITES BEATING**—contentious speech summons punishment (19:29; 26:3). "A fool's mouth is his destruction, and his lips are the snare of his soul" (18:7). **SELF-DESTRUCTION**—fool's words trap/destroy self (12:13; 13:3). "The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly" (18:8). **GOSSIP WOUNDS** (repeated 26:22). Slander penetrates deeply (like food to stomach, internalized). "He also that is slothful in his work is brother to him that is a great waster" (18:9). **LAZINESS = DESTRUCTION**—sluggard = kin to vandal (passive neglect = active destruction). "The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe" (18:10). **DIVINE REFUGE**—LORD's name = fortress (Ps 20:1; 61:3). Righteous finds safety. "The rich man's wealth is his strong city, and as an high wall in his own conceit" (18:11). **WEALTH'S ILLUSION** (10:15). Rich think wealth protects (false security, Luke 12:16-21). "Before destruction the heart of man is haughty, and before honour is humility" (18:12). **PRIDE/HUMILITY** (11:2; 15:33; 16:18). Pride → fall. Humility → honor.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Speech*

#### 18:13-24 — He That Answers a Matter Before He Hears It; A Man That Has Friends Must Show Himself Friendly
**Lore:** "He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him" (18:13). **PREMATURE JUDGMENT**—answering before listening = folly (modern "hot takes" condemned, John 7:51). "The spirit of a man will sustain his infirmity; but a wounded spirit who can bear?" (18:14). **INNER STRENGTH**—human spirit endures physical illness. Crushed spirit unbearable (17:22; 15:13). "The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge; and the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge" (18:15). Prudent acquire knowledge (heart + ear engaged). "A man's gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men" (18:16). **GIFT POWER** (17:8; 21:14). Present opens doors to influential (Gen 43:11, brothers to Joseph). "He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him" (18:17). **TWO SIDES**—first testimony appears right until cross-examined (need for due process). "The lot causeth contentions to cease, and parteth between the mighty" (18:18). **LOTS RESOLVE DISPUTES** (16:33; Josh 14:2; Acts 1:26). Impartial decision method. "A brother offended is harder to be won than a strong city: and their contentions are like the bars of a castle" (18:19). **FAMILY BREACH**—alienated brother harder to reconcile than conquering fortress. Disputes = locked gates. "A man's belly shall be satisfied with the fruit of his mouth; and with the increase of his lips shall he be filled" (18:20). **SPEECH CONSEQUENCES** (12:14). Words fill life (good/bad). "Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof" (18:21). **TONGUE'S POWER** (critical verse). Speech determines outcomes (Jas 3:5-10). Words create/destroy. "Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the LORD" (18:22). **MARRIAGE BLESSING**—good wife = divine favor (12:4; 19:14; 31:10-31; Gen 2:18). "The poor useth intreaties; but the rich answereth roughly" (18:23). **CLASS DYNAMICS**—poor plead humbly. Rich respond harshly (social reality, not ideal). "A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: and there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother" (18:24). **FRIENDSHIP RECIPROCITY**—to have friends, be friendly. True friend more loyal than family (17:17; John 15:13-15; Ruth 1:16-17).

## Chapter 19: Poverty, Wealth, and Parental Discipline

#### 19:1-12 — Better Is the Poor That Walks in His Integrity; He That Gets Wisdom Loves His Own Soul
**Lore:** **"Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his lips, and is a fool"** (19:1). **"BETTER THAN" + POVERTY**—poor with integrity > rich perverse fool (28:6). Character > wealth. "Also, that the soul be without knowledge, it is not good; and he that hasteth with his feet sinneth" (19:2). Zeal without knowledge = bad (Rom 10:2). Haste = sin. "The foolishness of man perverteth his way: and his heart fretteth against the LORD" (19:3). **SELF-CAUSED TROUBLE**—folly ruins path, then blames God (Lam 3:39). "Wealth maketh many friends; but the poor is separated from his neighbour" (19:4). **SOCIAL REALITY** (14:20; 19:6-7). Wealth attracts. Poverty repels. "A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall not escape" (19:5). **FALSE TESTIMONY** (repeated 19:9; 14:5, 25; 21:28). Liar won't escape. "Many will intreat the favour of the prince: and every man is a friend to him that giveth gifts" (19:6). Favor seekers flock to generous (gift-givers popular, 17:8; 18:16). "All the brethren of the poor do hate him: how much more do his friends go far from him? he pursueth them with words, yet they are wanting to him" (19:7). **POVERTY'S ISOLATION**—even family avoids poor. Friends flee. He pleads, they vanish (tragic social observation). "He that getteth wisdom loveth his own soul: he that keepeth understanding shall find good" (19:8). **SELF-LOVE = WISDOM**—acquiring wisdom = self-care (3:21-22; 4:6-9). Guarding understanding finds good. "A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall perish" (19:9). **REPEATED WARNING** (19:5). Emphasizes severity of false testimony. "Delight is not seemly for a fool; much less for a servant to have rule over princes" (19:10). **SOCIAL DISRUPTION**—fool enjoying luxury = inappropriate. Slave ruling nobles = worse (30:21-23, "earth cannot bear"). "The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory to pass over a transgression" (19:11). **PATIENCE GLORY** (14:29; 15:18; 16:32; 20:3). Overlooking offense = honor (not weakness). "The king's wrath is as the roaring of a lion; but his favour is as dew upon the grass" (19:12). **ROYAL POWER** (16:14-15; 20:2). King's anger = lion's roar (terrifying). Favor = dew (refreshing, life-giving).
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Integrity*

#### 19:13-23 — A Prudent Wife Is from the LORD; The Fear of the LORD Tends to Life
**Lore:** "A foolish son is the calamity of his father: and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping" (19:13). **FAMILY TROUBLE**—foolish son = disaster (10:1; 17:21, 25). Quarrelsome wife = perpetual dripping (27:15, leaky roof). "House and riches are the inheritance of fathers: and a prudent wife is from the LORD" (19:14). **DIVINE GIFT**—property inherited from fathers. Wise wife = God's gift (18:22; 31:10, "who can find?"). "Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger" (19:15). **LAZINESS = POVERTY**—sluggard sleeps. Result: hunger (6:9-11; 20:13; 24:33-34). "He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die" (19:16). Obey instruction = preserve life. Despise = death. "He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD; and that which he hath given will he pay him again" (19:17). **LENDING TO GOD**—mercy to poor = loan to LORD (14:21, 31; 22:9; Matt 25:40, "done unto least = done unto me"). God repays. "Chasten thy son while there is hope, and let not thy soul spare for his crying" (19:18). **PARENTAL DISCIPLINE** (13:24; 22:15; 23:13-14; 29:15, 17). Discipline early while formative (don't let tears deter correction). "A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment: for if thou deliver him, yet thou must do it again" (19:19). Hot-tempered person suffers consequences. Rescuing enables (repeated cycle). "Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end" (19:20). **TEACHABILITY**—accept advice to gain wisdom (long-term benefit). "There are many devices in a man's heart; nevertheless the counsel of the LORD, that shall stand" (19:21). **DIVINE SOVEREIGNTY** (16:1, 9; 21:30-31). Human plans abundant. God's purpose prevails (Isa 46:10). "The desire of a man is his kindness: and a poor man is better than a liar" (19:22). Man's desire = show loyalty (חֶסֶד, chesed). Poor honest man > wealthy liar. "The fear of the LORD tendeth to life: and he that hath it shall abide satisfied; he shall not be visited with evil" (19:23). **FEAR OF LORD** = life path (1:7; 14:27; 15:33). Abides satisfied, protected from evil.
*Verses: 11 | Theme: Fear*

#### 19:24-29 — The Sluggard Buries His Hand in His Dish; Judgments Are Prepared for Scorners
**Lore:** "A slothful man hideth his hand in his bosom, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth again" (19:24). **EXTREME LAZINESS** (repeated 26:15). Too lazy to feed self (hyperbolic, illustrates absurd extent). "Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding, and he will understand knowledge" (19:25). **DIFFERENT RESPONSES**—punish mocker = naive learn. Rebuke wise = gains knowledge (9:7-9; 21:11). "He that wasteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is a son that causeth shame and bringeth reproach" (19:26). **ELDER ABUSE**—child squandering father's wealth + driving away mother = disgraceful (28:24; Ex 21:17; Matt 15:4-6). "Cease, my son, to hear the instruction that causeth to err from the words of knowledge" (19:27). **AVOID FALSE TEACHING**—stop listening to instruction leading astray (1 Tim 6:3-5; 2 Tim 4:3-4). "An ungodly witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity" (19:28). Corrupt witness mocks justice. Wicked consume evil (enjoy unrighteousness). "Judgments are prepared for scorners, and stripes for the back of fools" (19:29). **FOOL'S FATE**—punishment awaits mockers (18:6; 26:3). Beatings for fools (inevitable consequences).

## Chapter 20: Wine, Kings, and Divine Weighing

#### 20:1-12 — Wine Is a Mocker; The Hearing Ear and the Seeing Eye, the LORD Has Made Both
**Lore:** **"Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise"** (20:1). **ALCOHOL WARNING**—wine (יַיִן, yayin) mocks. Strong drink (שֵׁכָר, shekhar, beer/liquor) brawls. Deception by intoxication = folly (23:29-35; 31:4-7; Eph 5:18). "The fear of a king is as the roaring of a lion: whoso provoketh him to anger sinneth against his own soul" (20:2). **ROYAL DANGER** (16:14; 19:12). King's rage = lion's roar. Provoking = self-harm. "It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling" (20:3). **CONFLICT AVOIDANCE** = honor (17:14; 19:11). Fool meddles (can't resist). "The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing" (20:4). **LAZINESS EXCUSE**—won't work in winter ("too cold"). Result: begging at harvest (excuses lead to poverty). "Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water; but a man of understanding will draw it out" (20:5). **WISDOM EXTRACTION**—plans buried deep (like water in well). Understanding person draws them out (skilled counselor elicits). "Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?" (20:6). **RARE LOYALTY**—many self-promote. Truly faithful rare (אִישׁ אֱמוּנוֹת, ish emunot, man of faithfulness, hard to find). "The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him" (20:7). **GENERATIONAL BLESSING**—righteous integrity blesses children (legacy of character). "A king that sitteth in the throne of judgment scattereth away all evil with his eyes" (20:8). **ROYAL JUSTICE**—king on judgment seat discerns evil (penetrating gaze, wisdom to judge). "Who can say, I have made my heart clean, I am pure from my sin?" (20:9). **UNIVERSAL SINFULNESS**—no one sinless (Ps 51:5; Eccl 7:20; Rom 3:23; 1 John 1:8). Rhetorical question. "Divers weights, and divers measures, both of them are alike abomination to the LORD" (20:10). **ECONOMIC JUSTICE** (11:1; 16:11; 20:23). Dishonest scales = abomination (double standards). "Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right" (20:11). **CHARACTER DISPLAYED EARLY**—children reveal character through actions (1 Sam 2:12-17, Eli's sons; 3:19, Samuel). "The hearing ear, and the seeing eye, the LORD hath made even both of them" (20:12). **DIVINE CREATION**—sensory faculties = God's gift (Ex 4:11; Ps 94:9). God designed human perception.
*Verses: 12 | Theme: Wisdom*

#### 20:13-24 — Love Not Sleep, Lest You Come to Poverty; Man's Goings Are of the LORD
**Lore:** "Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, and thou shalt be satisfied with bread" (20:13). **DILIGENCE** (6:9-11; 19:15; 24:33-34). Excessive sleep → poverty. Alertness → provision. "It is naught, it is naught, saith the buyer: but when he is gone his way, then he boasteth" (20:14). **NEGOTIATION TACTIC**—buyer disparages ("It's worthless!"). After purchase, brags about deal (ancient bargaining). "There is gold, and a multitude of rubies: but the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel" (20:15). **KNOWLEDGE > WEALTH** (3:14-15; 8:10-11; 16:16). Wise speech more valuable than gold/rubies. "Take his garment that is surety for a stranger: and take a pledge of him for a strange woman" (20:16). **DEBT WARNING** (6:1-5; 11:15; 17:18; 22:26-27; 27:13). If someone foolishly co-signs for stranger/immoral woman, demand collateral (protect yourself from their folly). "Bread of deceit is sweet to a man; but afterwards his mouth shall be filled with gravel" (20:17). **ILL-GOTTEN GAINS**—stolen/fraudulent food tastes sweet initially. Aftermath: mouth full of gravel (disgust, regret). "Every purpose is established by counsel: and with good advice make war" (20:18). **COUNSEL PRINCIPLE** (11:14; 15:22; 24:6). Plans succeed with advisors. War requires strategy. "He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips" (20:19). **GOSSIP + FLATTERY**—talebearer exposes secrets. Avoid flatterer (can't be trusted). "Whoso curseth his father or his mother, his lamp shall be put out in obscure darkness" (20:20). **PARENTAL HONOR** (Ex 20:12; 21:17; Lev 20:9; Matt 15:4). Cursing parents → lamp extinguished (life/lineage ended). "An inheritance may be gotten hastily at the beginning; but the end thereof shall not be blessed" (20:21). **QUICK WEALTH**—hasty inheritance (greedy for parents' death?) not blessed (13:11; 28:20, 22). "Say not thou, I will recompense evil; but wait on the LORD, and he shall save thee" (20:22). **VENGEANCE FORBIDDEN** (24:29; Lev 19:18; Deut 32:35; Rom 12:19, "vengeance is mine, saith the Lord"). Wait for God's justice. "Divers weights are an abomination unto the LORD; and a false balance is not good" (20:23). **REPEATED ECONOMIC JUSTICE** (20:10; 11:1; 16:11). Dishonest commerce = abomination. "Man's goings are of the LORD; how can a man then understand his own way?" (20:24). **DIVINE SOVEREIGNTY** (16:9; 19:21; Jer 10:23). God directs steps. Human understanding limited.

#### 20:25-30 — It Is a Snare to Devour That Which Is Holy; The Blueness of a Wound Cleanses Away Evil

Count cost first. "A wise king scattereth the wicked, and bringeth the wheel over them" (20:26). **ROYAL JUDGMENT**—wise king winnows wicked (separates like chaff), crushes with threshing wheel (20:8; 25:5). "The spirit of man is the candle of the LORD, searching all the inward parts of the belly" (20:27). **HUMAN SPIRIT**—"candle of LORD" (נֵר יְהוָה, ner YHWH, lamp of YHWH). Conscience = God's light searching inner being (1 Cor 2:11). "Mercy and truth preserve the king: and his throne is upholden by mercy" (20:28). **ROYAL STABILITY** (16:12; 25:5; 29:14). King's throne secured by חֶסֶד (chesed, covenant loyalty) + truth. "The glory of young men is their strength: and the beauty of old men is the gray head" (20:29). **GENERATIONAL STRENGTHS**—youth = physical strength. Age = gray hair (wisdom, experience, 16:31). "The blueness of a wound cleanseth away evil: so do stripes the inward parts of the belly" (20:30). **PUNISHMENT PURGES**—physical discipline cleanses evil (wounds/beatings correct inner corruption, Ps 119:67; Heb 12:11). Controversial but reflects ancient understanding.

## Chapter 21: The King's Heart and Divine Justice

#### 21:1-11 — The King's Heart Is in the Hand of the LORD; When the Scorner Is Punished, the Simple Is Made Wise
**Lore:** **"The king's heart is in the hand of the LORD, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will"** (21:1). **DIVINE SOVEREIGNTY OVER RULERS** (Dan 4:35; Rom 13:1). God controls king's decisions like irrigation channels (redirects at will, Ezra 6:22; 7:27). "Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts" (21:2). **SELF-JUSTIFICATION** (16:2; 30:12). Everyone self-justifies. God weighs hearts (motives). "To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice" (21:3). **ETHICS > RITUAL** (15:8; 1 Sam 15:22, "obey better than sacrifice"; Hos 6:6; Mic 6:6-8). Righteousness preferred over offerings. "An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin" (21:4). **PRIDE = SIN**—haughty eyes (6:17) + proud heart = wicked's lamp (רִיב נִיר, "lamp" = plowing/life activity). "The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want" (21:5). **DILIGENCE vs. HASTE** (13:11; 19:2; 28:20, 22). Careful planning → abundance. Rushing → poverty. "The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death" (21:6). **FRAUDULENT WEALTH**—riches by lies = fleeting vapor (pursues death, 10:2; 11:4). "The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment" (21:7). Violence of wicked drags them away (self-destruction). Refuse justice = ruined. "The way of man is froward and strange: but as for the pure, his work is right" (21:8). Guilty person's way = crooked/foreign. Pure person's work = straight. "It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house" (21:9). **"BETTER THAN" + MARRIAGE**—roof corner alone > mansion with quarrelsome wife (repeated 21:19; 25:24; cf. 19:13; 27:15). "The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes" (21:10). Wicked soul craves evil. Shows no mercy to neighbor. "When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge" (21:11). **LEARNING METHODS**—naive learns from mocker's punishment (19:25). Wise learns from instruction directly.

#### 21:12-22 — The Righteous Man Wisely Considers the House of the Wicked; The Name of the LORD Is a Strong Tower
**Lore:** "The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness" (21:12). **DIVINE OBSERVATION**—righteous observes wicked's house (learns). God overthrows wicked (judgment). "Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard" (21:13). **RECIPROCAL JUSTICE**—ignore poor's cry → your cry ignored (Matt 5:7; 18:23-35, unmerciful servant; Jas 2:13). "A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath" (21:14). **GIFT POWER** (17:8; 18:16). Secret gift appeases anger (Gen 32:20, Jacob's gift to Esau; 1 Sam 25:35, Abigail to David). "It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity" (21:15). Righteous rejoice in justice. Evildoers terrified by justice (judgment = destruction for them). "The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead" (21:16). **APOSTASY**—straying from wisdom path = dwelling among dead (Sheol, spiritual death). "He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich" (21:17). **HEDONISM → POVERTY**—pleasure lover impoverished. Wine + oil (luxuries) consume wealth. "The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright" (21:18). **SUBSTITUTION** (11:8). Wicked suffers punishment righteous deserved (Isa 43:3-4; Prov 11:8; Est 7:10, Haman). "It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman" (21:19). **"BETTER THAN" + MARRIAGE** (21:9; 25:24). Desert solitude > home with quarrelsome wife. "There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up" (21:20). Wise store wealth + oil. Fool squanders (spends everything, no savings). "He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour" (21:21). **PURSUIT REWARDS**—seeking righteousness + חֶסֶד (chesed, mercy) finds life, righteousness, honor (triple blessing). "A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof" (21:22). **WISDOM > STRENGTH** (24:5; Eccl 9:13-16). Wisdom conquers fortified city (strategy defeats force).

#### 21:23-31 — Whoso Keeps His Mouth and Tongue Keeps His Soul from Troubles; The Horse Is Prepared for Battle, but Safety Is of the LORD
**Lore:** "Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles" (21:23). **SPEECH RESTRAINT** (10:19; 13:3; 17:27-28). Guarded speech protects from trouble (Jas 3:2-10). "Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath" (21:24). **MOCKER DEFINED**—arrogance personified. Deals in proud rage (זָדוֹן, zadon, insolence). "The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour" (21:25). **SLUGGARD'S DEATH**—lazy desires without working. Result: death (wants but won't act). "He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not" (21:26). Wicked = greedy all day. Righteous = generous constantly (gives freely). "The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?" (21:27). **HYPOCRITICAL WORSHIP** (15:8). Wicked's offering = abomination. Worse when offered with evil intent (bribery, manipulation). "A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly" (21:28). **FALSE TESTIMONY** (19:5, 9). Liar perishes. Truthful witness endures (speaks consistently, not contradicting). "A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way" (21:29). Wicked = hardened countenance (brazen, shameless). Upright = considers path (thoughtful). "There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the LORD" (21:30). **DIVINE SUPREMACY**—no human wisdom/strategy opposes God successfully (19:21; Isa 8:10; 14:27; Job 42:2). "The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the LORD" (21:31). **MILITARY TRUST** (Ps 20:7; 33:17, "horse vain for safety"; Isa 31:1). Prepare for war, but victory = LORD's (divine sovereignty conclusion to chapter).

## Chapter 22:1-16: Conclusion of Solomonic Collection

#### 22:1-9 — A Good Name Is Rather to Be Chosen Than Great Riches; He That Has a Bountiful Eye Shall Be Blessed
**Lore:** **"A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold"** (22:1). **REPUTATION > WEALTH** (Eccl 7:1, "good name better than precious ointment"). Honor exceeds money. "The rich and poor meet together: the LORD is the maker of them all" (22:2). **COMMON CREATOR** (14:31; 17:5; 29:13; Job 31:15). God made rich + poor (shared humanity, equality before Creator). "A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished" (22:3). **WISDOM = FORESIGHT** (repeated 27:12). Prudent anticipates danger, avoids. Naive proceeds, suffers. "By humility and the fear of the LORD are riches, and honour, and life" (22:4). **TRIPLE REWARD**—humility + fear of LORD → riches, honor, life (3:16; 8:18). "Thorns and snares are in the way of the froward: he that doth keep his soul shall be far from them" (22:5). **PERVERSE PATH = DANGER**—crooked way full of traps. Soul-guarder avoids. "Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it" (22:6). **PARENTAL TRAINING** (most famous child-rearing verse). "Train up" (חֲנֹךְ, chanokh, dedicate/initiate). Early instruction lasts lifelong (general principle, not absolute guarantee). "The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender" (22:7). **DEBT = SLAVERY** (economic reality, not moral ideal). Borrowing creates servitude. "He that soweth iniquity shall reap vanity: and the rod of his anger shall fail" (22:8). **REAPING PRINCIPLE** (Job 4:8; Hos 8:7; Gal 6:7). Sow wickedness → reap emptiness. Rage rod fails. "He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor" (22:9). **GENEROUS EYE**—"bountiful eye" (עַיִן טוֹבָה, ayin tovah, lit. "good eye") = generosity (14:21, 31; 19:17; 2 Cor 9:6-7). Shares bread → blessing.
*Verses: 9 | Theme: Wisdom*

#### 22:10-16 — Cast Out the Scorner; The LORD Loves the Pure in Heart
**Lore:** "Cast out the scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease" (22:10). **REMOVE MOCKER**—expel scoffer → conflict ends (Gen 21:10, Hagar; Neh 4:1-5, Sanballat). Source of strife removed = peace. "He that loveth pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king shall be his friend" (22:11). Pure heart + gracious speech wins king's friendship (favor at court). "The eyes of the LORD preserve knowledge, and he overthroweth the words of the transgressor" (22:12). **DIVINE PROTECTION**—LORD guards knowledge (preserves truth-tellers). Overthrows traitor's words (exposes lies). "The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets" (22:13). **RIDICULOUS EXCUSE** (26:13, repeated). Sluggard invents absurd reason ("Lion in streets!") to avoid work. "The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: he that is abhorred of the LORD shall fall therein" (22:14). **ADULTERESS TRAP** (2:16-19; 5:3-14; 6:24-35; 7:5-27). Immoral woman = deep pit. LORD's judgment = falling into it. "Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him" (22:15). **PARENTAL DISCIPLINE** (13:24; 19:18; 23:13-14; 29:15, 17). Folly inherent in children. Correction removes (not abuse, but discipline). "He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches, and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want" (22:16). **ECONOMIC INJUSTICE**—exploit poor to get richer → poverty. Give to rich (bribery/flattery) → poverty. Both = judgment.

*Verses: 7 | Theme: Training*
